Antenna Group
is a leading international content, media and entertainment organization, owning and operating a diverse portfolio of global media assets across a variety of platforms: Television, Pay TV, OTT, Radio, Cinema Exhibition, Digital, Music, Publishing and Education, throughout Europe, North America and Australia. Antenna Group is currently seeking, to cover the following position:
Production Coordinator
About the Role
We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented
Production Coordinator
to join our team. In this role, you will oversee the planning, coordination, and execution of video productions, working closely with creative teams, directors, crew members, and external partners to ensure smooth workflows, efficient production logistics, and the timely, cost-effective delivery of projects in a fast-paced digital media environment.
Job Description
Organize and coordinate end-to-end shooting schedules, call sheets, locations, and equipment logistics
Serve as the main point of contact between creative teams, directors, crew, and external talent
Manage production budgets, vendor agreements, and post-production timelines
Troubleshoot on-set challenges to ensure shoots run efficiently and stay on schedule
Bachelor’s degree in Film and Television Production, Media Production, Audiovisual Arts, Communications, or a related field
Exceptional organizational, multitasking, and problem-solving skills under tight deadlines
Strong communication skills and ability to manage multi-disciplinary teams
Proficient with project management and spreadsheet tools (Excel, Asana, Trello, etc.)
Fluent in Greek & English, both written and spoken
High ethical standards fitting with the values of Antenna Group
