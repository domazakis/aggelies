At OnTheGoSystems, the company behind
WPML
,
Toolset
, and
PTC

hands-on, AI-native marketer to own how people find us – through search, AI assistants, and the partners and creators our customers already trust.
Content

AI is a core part of how we work.
We’re looking for someone who already uses AI agents, automation, integrations, and structured workflows to get real marketing work done – and can show us what they’ve achieved with them.
You won’t be joining an established marketing team or following a fixed playbook. You’ll have the freedom to
talk to our clients, understand our business, and find the strongest opportunities we should focus on next.
What You’ll Work On
Your
main focus will be content, search visibility and partnerships
:
Content creation
: planning and writing articles, guides, and tutorials that help our users and attract new ones
SEO, GEO, and AEO
: growing our visibility in search engines, AI assistants, and answer engines
Technical and content SEO across our websites
Understanding how AI tools describe and recommend our products, and improving it
Building collaborations
with partners, authors, and bloggers
Creating and co-creating content that ranks, gets cited, and earns links
Measuring what works, using our own analytics
Over time, other opportunities you identify
You’ll work hands-on, from identifying an opportunity through execution, measurement, and improvement.
What We’re Looking For
You already use
AI agents, automation, and advanced AI workflows in your everyday work.
You write clear, useful content yourself and can share examples, ideally on technical topics.
You have hands-on SEO experience and can show results you've achieved.
You're actively exploring GEO and AEO, and understand how visibility in AI-generated answers differs from traditional search.
You've built partnerships or collaborations with bloggers, creators, or other companies, and know how to make them work for both sides.
You're curious and take time to understand customers and the business before proposing solutions.
You have experience marketing technical products, ideally software, SaaS, WordPress, or similar.
You work independently and are comfortable in an environment where priorities can change, and not everything is defined in advance.
Why Join Us
OnTheGoSystems is a profitable, self-funded product company growing since 2007. WPML powers more than

You’ll have real ownership and the freedom to help build our marketing approach, work directly with our CEO and product teams, experiment with AI, and turn your ideas into real marketing initiatives.
We care about the people we work with and want to support them beyond their day-to-day work. Along with the flexibility of working remotely, we offer perks and benefits that support both work and life.
If you're excited about shaping how people find software in the age of AI search, creating content and partnerships that matter, and having the freedom to decide what comes next, we'd love to hear from you.
