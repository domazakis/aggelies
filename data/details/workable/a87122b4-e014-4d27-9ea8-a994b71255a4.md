Sleed
, a digital marketing agency with more than 10 years of field experience and a strategic partner of Google, is looking for a Digital Media Specialist to join our Digital Department.
This is a hands-on performance role for an experienced media professional. You will own the setup, optimization and results of paid media campaigns on Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads, use data (GA4 and beyond) to drive every decision, and be a trusted contact for our clients on everything media.
You will join one of our client teams, reporting to the Digital Media Manager and working on every account alongside a senior Strategy colleague. You will also guide Digital Media Executives in their day-to-day work.
Responsibilities
Campaign Management:
Plan, set up and manage campaigns end-to-end on Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads for medium and large clients: structure, audiences, budgets, bidding, creatives and ad copy, always aligned with the goals of each client.
Performance & Optimization:
Own the performance of your accounts. Monitor results daily, run structured tests and optimize budgets, bids, audiences and creatives to reach client KPIs such as ROAS, CPA or CPL. Flag risks and opportunities proactively.
Measurement & Analytics:
Use GA4 together with the ad platforms to see the full picture, make sure tracking and conversions are set up correctly, and turn the data into clear next steps.
Reporting:
Create and present reports (Looker Studio) that go beyond the numbers: what happened, why, and what we do next, in order to demonstrate the return on advertising spend to clients and company management.
Media Strategy & Planning:
Contribute to media plans, budget allocation and channel recommendations together with the Strategy team, and bring new ideas and platform best practices to your accounts.
Client Communication:
Be a trusted point of contact for clients on media topics: present results, explain decisions and recommendations in clear language, manage expectations and follow up with ownership.
Team Guidance:
Support and guide Digital Media Executives with setup, optimization and quality checks. Freely share knowledge, tests and best practices across the team.
AI-Powered Work:
Use AI tools to work faster and smarter (for example for analysis, ad copy and creative ideas, reporting and automation) and help the team adopt them.
Main KPIs:
Campaign performance against client goals, quality and accuracy of analysis and reports, client satisfaction, and the growth of the people you guide.
Be a Sleeder:
Take ownership, embrace adaptability, demonstrate resilience, spread positivity, and bring human passion to everything you do.
Experience:
3+ years of experience in digital marketing or paid media, ideally in a digital agency.
Paid Media & Analytics:
Strong, exceptional hands-on skills in Google Ads, Meta Ads and GA4: you have managed campaigns end-to-end and know them well beyond the basics. Working experience with TikTok Ads.
Data-Driven:
Analytical, results-oriented mindset with a strong focus on optimization: you make decisions based on data and can explain them.
