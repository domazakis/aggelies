QnR Group is a technology group with 35 years of experience, 8 companies, 4 delivery centers and 210+ professionals across Greece, Belgium, Cyprus and Poland. The Group’s capabilities covers software engineering, enterprise and generative AI, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, SAP and ServiceNow solutions, core banking and maritime intelligence.
