🎯 The Role
As our Web & Agentic AI Specialist, you'll own our web presence and deliver conversion-focused web and agentic AI experiences for every marketing pillar. You'll work hand-in-hand with the marketing team to design, build and optimize web pages, landing pages, microsites and web apps on Webflow, and you'll use agentic AI to create smarter, faster processes across the team.
⚡ What You'll Be Doing
Design & develop in Webflow:
Design and build high-performing, responsive pages, from concept to launch, with our website running on Webflow.
Analytics setup:
Set up and maintain tracking (GA4, Google Tag Manager, conversion events, ad pixels) and dashboards so we can measure what works.
Agentic AI & automation:
Design and deploy AI agents and automated workflows that create and optimize marketing processes (e.g. lead routing, content operations, reporting).
Forms & CRM integrations:
Connect forms, the website and marketing tools with our CRM so every lead is captured, enriched and routed correctly.
Landing pages, microsites & web apps:
Rapidly launch campaign landing pages and microsites, and create interactive web apps (e.g. calculators, quizzes, configurators) that drive leads.
Technical SEO & AI optimization
: Own technical SEO (site speed, Core Web Vitals, structured data, indexing) and optimize our content for AI-driven search (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity).
Collaborate with every pillar:
Partner with marketing team members to turn ideas, even the crazy ones, into live web experiences.
Conversion optimization:
Run A/B tests and continuously improve pages based on data.

3+ years of hands-on experience designing and developing websites, with a strong portfolio of live Webflow projects (please include the link with your application).
Advanced Webflow skills: CMS collections, interactions, components and responsive, mobile-first builds.
Solid HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and comfort working with APIs and webhooks.
A strong eye for UI/UX and conversion-focused design, with experience in Figma.
Practical experience with agentic AI and automation tools (e.g. n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI/Claude APIs or similar) used to build real workflows.
Experience integrating forms with CRM / marketing automation platforms (e.g. HubSpot, Salesforce or similar).
Hands-on analytics setup with GA4 and Google Tag Manager, including conversion tracking.
