About the role
As an Financial Advisory Manager, you will work alongside multidisciplinary teams to help corporates, investors, developers, public-sector bodies and funders understand, secure and manage financing solutions that support growth, acquisitions, strategic investments, infrastructure and energy projects.
Your role will include:
Manage the day-to-day delivery of financing, project finance and broader financial advisory engagements, including work planning, budgets, timelines, task allocation, quality and risk-management procedures, and client-ready outputs.
Build, review and challenge integrated financial models covering operational forecasts, cash flows, debt schedules, covenants, scenarios, sensitivities, and equity and lender returns.
Assess financing requirements, funding capacity, capital structures and refinancing alternatives, and support debt-raising and refinancing processes by preparing financing strategies, lender presentations, information memoranda, financial analyses and responses to due diligence enquiries.
Support infrastructure and energy assignments throughout the project lifecycle, including feasibility assessment, investment appraisal, bid support, risk allocation and transaction execution.
Contribute to valuations, business plans, feasibility studies, financial due diligence and other transaction-related analyses.
Review financial, commercial and operational information, identify key assumptions and risks, and ensure that analyses and supporting working papers are accurate, consistent and appropriately documented.
Prepare and review high-quality reports and presentations, maintain effective client communication, and coordinate inputs from lenders, investors, public-sector bodies and internal or external specialists.
Supervise, coach and review the work of junior team members, providing timely feedback and supporting their technical and professional development.
Contribute to proposals, pitches, market research, thought leadership and business-development initiatives, helping to build trusted client relationships
What we are looking for:
5-7 years of relevant professional experience in project finance, infrastructure finance, transaction services, corporate finance or financial advisory
Solid academic background in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, Engineering or another relevant discipline (MSc will be considered an advantage)
Proficient use of Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint; including hands-on experience developing and reviewing financial models
Previous experience in a relevant role in Financial Modelling will be considered an asset.
Proven ability to work in a deadline-driven environment and handle multiple projects simultaneously.
Demonstrated leadership in mentoring and reviewing the work of team members
Commitment to delivering services of high quality
Ability to interact effectively with people at all organizational levels
Strong analytical thinking with effective problem solving and decision making capabilities
Excellent communication and relationship building skills with clients and internal stakeholders
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both Greek and English
Professional qualifications, or progress toward ACA, ACCA, CFA or a similar certification, will be considered an asset
