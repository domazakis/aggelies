Founded in 1870, Papoutsanis SA is Greece’s largest soap manufacturer and one of Europe’s leading producers of soap and hotel cosmetics. From our state-of-the-art plant in Ritsona, we produce over 220 million high-quality, innovative products every year, reaching customers in more than 45 countries worldwide.
With a strong focus on innovation, brand development and sustainability, Papoutsanis continues to invest in its brands, products and marketing capabilities, strengthening its presence in both domestic and international markets.
We are now looking for a dynamic
Marketing Specialist
to join our team, reporting directly to the Group Brand Manager. The role will provide key support to the marketing team, contributing to our B2B marketing activities and strengthening Papoutsanis’ presence at international exhibitions, while also supporting the development and implementation of marketing plans for our domestic brands.
Key Responsibilities:
Coordinate the company’s participation in international trade exhibitions, including stand development, marketing materials and logistics.
Develop B2B marketing and sales materials, including presentations, leaflets and newsletters.
Support the development and implementation of brand marketing plans, new product launches and promotional activities.
Coordinate the development and updating of advertising, packaging and promotional materials.
Contribute to market analysis, brand performance tracking, reporting and the preparation of marketing presentations.
Support social media management and the update of product information across digital platforms and retail e-shops.
Assist in the organization of corporate events and the preparation of PR kits, product samples and promotional materials.
1–2 years of experience in Marketing, preferably within the FMCG, Personal Care or Beauty industry.
University degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Economics or a related field.
Strong digital skills and excellent command of MS Office, particularly Excel and PowerPoint.
Excellent command of English.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Willingness to travel and participate in international exhibitions.
Strong organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
Teamwork, proactive mindset and willingness to take initiative.
Nice To Have:
Experience in organizing or participating in international exhibitions and trade fairs.
Familiarity with AI-powered tools and digital marketing and analytics tools.
Knowledge of a second foreign language.
Competitive remuneration package.
Monthly meal vouchers (Ticket Restaurant).
Private medical and life insurance coverage
Additional employee benefits and corporate discounts
Opportunities for professional development and continuous learning.
If you are passionate about marketing, enjoy working in a dynamic and international environment, and are eager to contribute to the growth of our brands, we would love to hear from you!
