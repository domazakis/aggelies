Responsibilities:
Prepares publicity plans and contributes to their successful implementation, aiming to optimally promote the Company. Participates in maintaining positive relationships and further developing contacts with the media within the areas of responsibility, based on the communication strategy, to more effectively promote the Company to external audiences
Participates in the preparation of announcements, interviews (written, radio, television and New Media), and articles for national and local media, as well as in the co-development of additional communication materials (presentations, speeches, briefing notes, talking points, etc.). Supports the organization and implementation of media events, press conferences, corporate press trips, etc.
Monitors publicity concerning the Company, competitors, the energy sector, the digital and innovative services sector, as well as developments in the broader operational environment
Maintains steady collaborations with Company Departments to develop, coordinate, and facilitate strategic communication initiatives that serve business objectives. Works with internal and external partners to produce communication materials
Creates necessary online content and systematically monitors online dialogue
Requirements:
Bachelor’s degree from a University in Communication, Political Science, Public Administration, or a related field
Master’s degree in Communication or a related field
At least five (5) years of relevant experience in corporate communication on projects and matters related to energy, and/or technology, and/or telecommunications, and/or IT, and/or digital transformation, and/or advanced digital services
At least five (5) years of experience in Public Relations or Strategic Communication companies, focusing on corporate communication, media relations, and managing the communication needs of large corporate clients
Knowledge and use of Digital and Social Media will be considered an additional qualification
Good knowledge of the English language
Knowledge of Microsoft Office programs (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and Internet
Availability in case of emergency (upon phone contact)
Availability for urgent geographical relocations

Accountability and result orientation
Teamwork and collaboration
Problem-solving and decision-making
