Ogilvy seeks to recruit a
Performance Marketing Specialist
to join its award-winning team and one of its fastest growing units,
Ogilvy One
. The candidate will be responsible for designing, managing & reporting digital campaigns across all digital channels including Paid search, Social, Display & Programmatic advertising. Additionally, the candidate may be involved in SEO and Analytics projects.
You will be working in a fast paced environment and will be responsible for some of the greatest brands in the Greek Market.
Minimum 3 years prior experience (agency or client side) in managing complex media accounts (Google Ads, Social Media Platforms).
Google Ads & Analytics certified.
Meta certified (primarily media planning or buying).
Experience in programmatic advertising.
Good understanding & knowledge of Google Analytics 4 & Looker Studio.
Highly Proficient in MS-Excel (pivot tables, advanced formulas etc).
Excellent consulting, writing, presentation and communication skills in Greek and English.
Any SEO experience will be considered a plus.
Degree in a quantitative, test-driven field.
Responsibilities
Design, plan and execute digital campaigns via digital media platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Linkedin Ads, DV360, etc.).
Provide detailed analysis and reporting of each campaign including interpretation, commentary and recommendations.
Testing, analysis and optimization with tools like Excel, Google Sheets & Google Analytics.
Reporting to clients on weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual basis based on client needs and business objectives.
Collaborate closely with other business units and partners.
Coordinate with accounting dept. about billing and invoicing issues relevant to digital media campaigns.
Stay up-to-date on the latest industry trends & best practices and evaluate emerging performance marketing technologies.
