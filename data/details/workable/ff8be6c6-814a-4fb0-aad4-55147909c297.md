We are looking for a
Performance Marketing & MarTech Specialist
to join our Digital Acquisition and MarTech team.
This is a hybrid role combining performance marketing, marketing technology, data, tracking and optimisation. You will be responsible for helping us maximise the performance of our paid acquisition activity while ensuring that the technology and data infrastructure supporting our marketing channels is accurate, reliable and continuously improving.
You will work closely with the Digital Acquisition, SEO, BI, CRM, Product, Engineering and Marketing teams to turn data into actionable insights and measurable business growth.
The ideal candidate is comfortable working both inside advertising platforms and with the technology/data behind them.
Key Responsibilities
Performance Marketing
Plan, execute and optimise paid acquisition campaigns across channels such as Google Ads, Performance Max, Meta and other digital advertising platforms.
Monitor and analyse campaign performance against key KPIs such as ROAS, and identify opportunities to improve efficiency and growth.
Optimise budget allocation across campaigns, channels and audiences to maximise performance and ROI.
Manage and optimise campaign structures, targeting, bidding, audiences, creatives and landing pages.
Identify opportunities for automation and process improvements within paid media.
Provide regular performance reporting and actionable recommendations.
MarTech & Tracking
Own the day-to-day operation of key marketing technology and measurement platforms, including Google Tag Manager (GTM), GA4, Looker Studio, advertising pixels, conversion tracking and audience technologies.
Manage the day-to-day collaboration with the external analytics partner, ensuring effective communication, delivery and alignment with business requirements.
Troubleshoot tracking, attribution and data-quality issues in collaboration with the BI and Engineering teams.
Support the implementation of new marketing technologies, integrations and tools, working with developers and external technology partners.
Ensure marketing platforms are correctly connected and exchanging the required data, and identify opportunities to improve marketing automation, data flows and operational efficiency.
Cross-functional collaboration
Work closely with Digital Acquisition, SEO, Analytics, CRM, Product, Engineering, Finance and Creative teams.
Translate marketing requirements into clear technical requirements for developers and technology partners.
Coordinate with external agencies and technology vendors, including vendor operations, invoice validation and purchase order management.
Communicate performance insights and recommendations clearly to stakeholders.
Requirements
3–5 years of experience in performance marketing (Google Ads, Meta Ads).
3–5 years of experience with MarTech and analytics platforms, including Google Merchant Center, product feeds, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM) and Looker Studio.
Experience troubleshooting tracking and data-quality issues.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; comfortable working with large datasets and performance reports.
Ability to work effectively with both marketing and technical teams.
Strong attention to detail and ownership of results.
Excellent command of Greek and English, both written and oral.
Nice to have
Working knowledge of Supermetrics.
Experience in e-commerce or a high-volume digital business.
Experience working with APIs or marketing technology integrations.
