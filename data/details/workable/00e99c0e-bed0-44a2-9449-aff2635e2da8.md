At Uni Systems, we are working towards turning digital visions into reality. We are continuously growing and we are looking for a Senior Business Consultant to join our UniQue team.
What will you be brining to the team?
Our UniQue people come from a variety of different backgrounds and industries, with different skillsets and approaches, but they have one thing in common: passion for technology and innovation! For the Senior Business Consultant position we expect you to:
Meet with clients to determine requirements
Conduct and facilitate seminars and workshops based on clients’ needs
Analyze and document the clients’ requirements in a comprehensive way in order to provide solution that fulfills their needs
Collaborate with Data Engineers, Developers, and other stakeholders to make sure that requirements are clarified and well documented.
Oversee the project progress and ensure that all parties share the same view
Communicate with all involved parties in order to identify, monitor and resolve dependencies
Ensure high product quality
Provide continuous support on software solutions
What do you need to succeed in this position?
Your experience, skillset and qualifications are going to help you succeed in this position, moving forward. We would like you to have:
University Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology from a Greek or foreign institute
At least five years of experience as an IT Business Analyst in banking's payment solutions
In-depth knowledge of local and international payment systems (SEPA, SEPA Inst, Target2, SWIFT, etc.)
Excellent business know how of payments
Very good knowledge of English language
Very good knowledge of MS-Office applications
Outstanding communications skills, with strong customer service orientation
Career motivation and a willingness to continue to further knowledge and skills
Technical knowledge and experience will be a plus
