Elorus delivers cutting-edge online financial management solutions for project-driven teams and small businesses worldwide. Our all-in-one invoicing, time tracking, and expense management platform is trusted by more than 50,000 businesses across diverse industries.
With over a decade of experience, we combine the reliability of a mature company with the drive for continuous innovation. We're a team of passionate professionals committed to building great software and delivering exceptional service.
About this Position
We're looking for a creative and tech-savvy
Marketing Content Writer
to join our team and help us produce engaging, high-quality content for our Greek target audience.
Your mission will be to create content that is clear, to the point, and adds real value to the reader, whether that is a blog post that answers a question a business owner actually has, a product landing page, or an email campaign. You bring editorial judgment and a good understanding of our audience, small business owners and freelancers, and when a topic is new to you, you are comfortable researching it until you can explain it well.
In our team, AI tools are part of the daily workflow: we use them to research, plan, restructure, and brainstorm. We'd like to meet you if you can get the most out of them while still judging the results critically, so that every final piece is accurate, useful, well-written, and aligned with Elorus' brand voice.
Our ideal candidate is a team-spirited, detail-oriented content professional who combines excellent Greek writing skills with a good grasp of business topics and curiosity about AI, technology, and modern content workflows. Self-driven and keen to keep learning and improving.
Please share samples of your work in Greek by including either a portfolio or links with your application, so we can get an idea of your best work, preferably on business management, finance, technology, or small-business-related topics.
If you have a passion for technology, enjoy experimenting with new tools, and understand the pain points and needs of modern SMBs, you are at the right place.
What you’ll do
With a focus on prioritizing your day-to-day tasks, your core duties will include:
Creating content for our Greek blog, from guides on invoicing and myDATA to articles on the everyday questions of small businesses and freelancers. You will use AI tools to research topics, generate ideas, and identify content angles to help with the content production process.
Writing copy for product landing pages and email campaigns to promote Elorus.
Re-structuring, refreshing, and merging blog articles that are already published to enhance their performance.
Developing product-related content, including product descriptions, guides, onboarding emails, and in-app tooltips to support our existing users.
Proofreading and editing articles in our Greek help center, making them more comprehensive and easier to understand.
Additionally, you'll have numerous opportunities to further express your creativity by:
Creating long-form content, such as ebooks, reports, surveys, and guides, on industry-related topics to attract our target audience.
Writing contributed articles for third-party websites and media, on topics relevant to Greek SMBs.
Experimenting with AI tools and suggesting ways to improve content quality, speed, and scalability.
Collaborating with the marketing and product teams to transform product knowledge, customer insights, and SEO opportunities into useful content.
