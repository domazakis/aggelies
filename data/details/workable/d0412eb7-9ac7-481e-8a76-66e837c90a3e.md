Power Factors is a software and solutions provider leading the next generation of clean energy with Unity, one of the most extensive and widely deployed renewable energy management suites (REMS) in the market. The company’s suite of open, data-driven applications empowers renewable energy stakeholders to collaborate, automate critical workflows, and make more informed decisions to maximize asset returns. ABOUT THE ROLE
Currently, we are looking to strengthen our team with a passionate and dedicated mid level In-house Lawyer. The ideal candidate will be involved with several day-to-day legal matters, contracts and basic elements of cross border transactions.
This role is located in our offices in Chalandri, Greece
WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING
· Providing legal services and advice in Corporate and Business Law
· Reviewing commercial international contracts (NDAs, Framework agreements/ MSAs, Supply agreements, Maintenance agreements, Software License Agreement etc)
· Managing legal projects effectively and actively establishing internal and external relationships
· Drafting Board and General Assembly Minutes
· Handling multiple legal matters simultaneously and independently – possessing the ability to deliver high-quality work under deadlines
· Conducting legal research
· Participating in client meetings/phone conferences
WHAT YOU WILL NEED TO BE SUCCESSFUL
We believe you enjoy working in a purpose-driven organization and that you thrive in an environment where you need to find creative solutions to challenging problems in a fast-changing context. This also means that you are effective at managing multiple tasks of varying complexities and are driven by continuous learning and growth. Specifically, we are looking for someone with the following toolbox:
· University degree in Law from a distinguished university
· Master’s degree in Business Law, Corporate or Commercial Law, Energy or IT field (preferable, not required)
· At least 5 years of experience in relevant field at a law firm with a focus on international contracts or in the legal department of an IT/software or energy company (required) is desirable
· Experience in reviewing international commercial contracts (such as NDAs , Framework / Master Services agreements, Supply agreements, Maintenance agreements, Software License agreements etc) (required)
· Experience in corporate housekeeping/Drafting of Minutes (required)
· Excellent knowledge of written and spoken professional English as communications are mainly conducted in the English language (required)
· Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
· Business acumen with a solution-oriented approach
· Accountability and results orientation
· Professionalism and developed business acumen
