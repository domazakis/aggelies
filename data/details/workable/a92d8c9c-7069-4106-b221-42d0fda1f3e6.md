About Barilla
Barilla is an Italian family-owned food company. Established in 1877, it’s now an international Group present in more than 100 countries.
A world leader in the markets of pasta and ready–to–use sauces in continental Europe, bakery products in Italy and crispbread in Scandinavia, the Barilla Group is recognized worldwide as a symbol of Italian know–how.
“The joy of food for a better life” is Barilla’s purpose. Bringing people closer to the joy of good food and making quality the choice for a better life, from each individual to the planet. Because this is how we are nurturing the future, today. It's a commitment from field to fork, to bring to the world tasty, joyful, and wholesome products, made with selected raw materials from responsible supply chains. Because what we eat today can change our tomorrow. Because good food is a joy for the present and a choice for a better future.
At Barilla we empower people to live and work responsibly and ethically, embracing our value system of integrity, trust, courage, intellectual curiosity and passion. Key Accountabilities
Lead efforts to increase e-commerce sales and market share across all online customer segments.
Take charge of the annual agreement negotiations with e-tailers, to ensure successful implementation of business plans. Cultivate long-term customer engagement relationship with a value creation approach.
Build strategic partnerships with omni-channel customers retailers to drive business growth.
Develop and execute annual business plans, aligning them with shopper and brand priorities.
Promotional planning, co-ordination & evaluation for a group of customers.
Assortment priorities & Merchandising standards definition.
Collaborate closely with Global Centre of Excellence on tools, processes, portfolio, content and search optimization.
Work in coordination with Marketing, Sales, and Trade Marketing Teams to deploy the best content for e-commerce shoppers.
Optimize content and search placement on retailer websites and effectively utilize retailer digital media to support e-commerce shopper initiatives.
Analyze e-commerce activities data to support decision-making and enhance effectiveness.
