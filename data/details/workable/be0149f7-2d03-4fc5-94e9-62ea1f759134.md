As a
Project Manager (at PMO: Tech/Telecom Projects)
you will oversee the planning, execution, and delivery of
technical and infrastructure projects
, ensuring timely, cost-effective, and high-quality outcomes.
Initially, the focus will be on managing a
major OTDR rollout initiative
for fiber network testing and monitoring. Over time, the role will expand to cover a
broader portfolio of projects
involving
IoT systems, software integration, energy optimization, and smart infrastructure deployments
.
The position requires a
blend of technical understanding, organizational skills, and stakeholder coordination
, making it ideal for an engineer with a strong delivery mindset and passion for technology.
