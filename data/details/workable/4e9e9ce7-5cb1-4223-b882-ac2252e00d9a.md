About the role:
A Technical Product Manager (TPM) is a specialized product leader who bridges the gap between business objectives and complex technical execution, providing development specs and documentations, managing developer tools, APIs, data pipelines, and core platforms.
In this role, you will lead development efforts within core banking-related projects, guide engineering teams, and ensure delivery of high-performing and secure solutions. You will work closely with product, architecture, and client teams to design, build, and implement advanced banking platforms and services.
Key Responsibilities:
Guide the software development lifecycle, from architecture and design to delivery and maintenance.
Acts as a liaison between Product Solutions and Product Development, providing specs and documentations to development teams
Apply deep understanding of banking systems, regulatory needs, and financial workflows to drive solution design and quality.
Oversee technical decision making, ensuring scalability, performance, and maintainability of solutions.
Support QA and review processes to ensure compliance, security, and product excellence.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including business analysts, product owners, and DevOps.
Utilize hands-on technical proficiency across:
Microsoft .NET
MS SQL Server
Postgres
Angular
GitHub
Apply experience in cloud environments including AWS and Azure, supporting hybrid and cloud-native architectures.
Qualifications:
Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
10+ years of proven experience
in leading development teams and delivering enterprise-scale software systems.
Strong domain knowledge in banking, financial services, or core banking platforms.
Deep understanding of software engineering practices, system architecture, and lifecycle management.
Cloud experience with AWS and/or Azure environments.
Strong leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
A solutions-driven mindset with the ability to handle complex challenges.
