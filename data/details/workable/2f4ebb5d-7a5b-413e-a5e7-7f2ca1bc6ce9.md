The Role
We’re looking for a
CRM Solutions & Implementation Consultant
to help us turn business needs into well-designed CRM solutions, primarily in HubSpot.
You’ll work across discovery, solution design, implementation and enablement. That means understanding how a client’s teams operate, shaping the right approach, configuring the CRM and helping people use it with confidence.
This role combines thoughtful consulting with hands-on delivery. You’ll work closely with experienced consultants, solution architects and developers, with opportunities to take on more ownership as you grow.

Understand the business

Learn how existing processes work and where they can improve.

Design and implement CRM solutions

Build workflows for lead routing, handoffs, follow-ups and other business processes.
Create reports and dashboards that give teams useful visibility.
Support clean data, thoughtful CRM governance and scalable system design.
Troubleshoot issues and recommend clear, workable solutions.
Deliver work people can build on
Document CRM logic, workflows and implementation decisions.
Prepare materials that help clients understand and use their CRM.
Help ensure the systems we deliver are clear, maintainable and fit for purpose.
Work closely with clients and the team
Help train client teams and support adoption of new processes.
Explain solutions clearly and confidently, in writing and in conversation.
Collaborate with developers on integrations and cross-platform data flows.
Coordinate with colleagues and client stakeholders to keep delivery moving.
