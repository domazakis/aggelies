Are you passionate about creating meaningful employee experiences, fostering a positive workplace culture and bringing creative ideas to life? Do you have an interest in people development, internal communications and employer branding?
This is your opportunity to join
Goldair Handling
, one of the leading ground handling service providers in Southeast Europe, and become part of our Human Resources team at Athens International Airport.
🌟 About the Role
As an
Engagement & Development Specialist
, you will actively contribute to the design and implementation of initiatives that enhance employee experience, support people development and strengthen our employer brand across our domestic and international network.
You will have the opportunity to combine your HR knowledge, creativity and project management skills to deliver meaningful initiatives that reflect our company culture and values.
🧭 Key Responsibilities
Design, coordinate and implement employee engagement initiatives, fostering a positive and inclusive workplace culture.
Organize corporate events, employee recognition programs, well-being activities and other employee experience initiatives.
Coordinate employee engagement surveys and focus groups, contributing to the analysis of results and the development of action plans.
Develop engaging internal communications, including newsletters, announcements and employee campaigns.
Coordinate learning and development activities in collaboration with internal stakeholders and external partners.
Contribute to Talent Management, Leadership Development, Internal Mobility and Performance Management initiatives.
Develop and implement employer branding initiatives in collaboration with the Talent Acquisition team.
Create engaging content for social media, career platforms and corporate communication channels, showcasing our people, culture and career opportunities.
Participate in career events and initiatives that enhance the company's visibility as an employer.
Manage assigned projects from planning to implementation, ensuring adherence to timelines, budgets and objectives.
Monitor relevant KPIs, evaluate the effectiveness of initiatives and propose improvements.
Stay up to date with emerging trends and best practices in Employee Engagement, Learning & Development and Employer Branding.
🎓 Qualifications & Experience
Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, Marketing, Communications or a related field.
At least 2 years of relevant professional experience in Employee Engagement, Learning & Development, Employer Branding, Internal Communications or a related field.
Experience in coordinating projects, events and/or communication campaigns.
Good understanding of employer branding practices and digital communication channels.
Experience in content creation and social media platforms.
Excellent command of the Greek and English languages, both written and verbal.
Very good knowledge of MS Office.
Familiarity with Canva or other content creation and design tools will be considered an asset.
A postgraduate degree in a relevant field will be considered an asset.

Creativity and innovative thinking.
