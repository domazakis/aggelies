This position was mainly established for the purpose of supporting the Components Team of Asso.subsea. The position requires self-motivation and aspiration to further develop in a technology driven company that operates in the offshore industry. The successful candidate will assist and support the team with the maintenance of mechanical parts, instruments and for the Group's bespoke and novel equipment.
Main Responsibilities
Production of underwater cables, including preparation, soldering/termination, and resin moulding.
Inspection, maintenance, and troubleshooting of underwater cables.
Assembly and disassembly of underwater cable instruments and their integration with underwater machinery.
Execution of maintenance tasks on underwater equipment.
High School Diploma or Technical Education Degree
Up to 2 years of experience
Experience and familiarity with hand tools (wrenches, screwdrivers etc.) and electrical tools (drilling, grinding machine)
Read a simple electronic and electrical drawing
Be able to use electrician hand tools (Multimeter instrument, cutters, pliers, screwdrivers etc).
Private Health Insurance
Company Bus from central points
Fast-paced environment
Specialization in a niche market
Competitive compensation
Continuous development opportunities
