GRNET S.A. provides Internet connectivity, high-quality e-Infrastructures (HPC, cloud and storage resources), and advanced services to the Greek Educational, Academic and Research community, with strong links with European and international infrastructures and research community. GRNET is hosting the DAEDALUS supercomputer, ranked 31st  among the fastest supercomputers worldwide and will also serve the Greek AI factory - Pharos with special needs for AI workflows.
We are currently looking for a technically-oriented Project Manager with a track record in project management and wide technical background, preferably in Computer Engineering or Computer Science, such as parallel computing, computer architecture and machine learning,
to join our team working on the management and delivery of EC Projects for the European Infrastructures and Projects Directorate.
You will be part of a team of established Projects Managers and engineers that nourish technical competency, delivering  solutions/services for e-Infrastructures in a number of strategic
EC Projects in the areas of  EuroHPC JU, Global Connectivity, EuroQCI and European Open Science Cloud amongst others.
Your Tasks:
Develop project concept, scope and objectives, involving all relevant stakeholders and ensuring
technical feasibility
Preparing and writing proposals for projects, independently or in collaboration with other European partners
Manage running projects
Participate to and manage meetings, including administrative and technical ones
Contribute to deliverables of active projects
Create and maintain comprehensive project documentation
Contribute to designing technical solutions for scalable, fault-tolerant, highly available services targeting wide user base
Represent the company in project consortium meetings in Europe and beyond
