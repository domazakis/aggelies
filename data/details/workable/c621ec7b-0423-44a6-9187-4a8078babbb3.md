The
Product Manager - Energy Services & Platforms
drives the development of energy services and energy products’ presence in Volton’s digital platforms, while identifying new business opportunities and developing strategic partnerships. The role combines product management, commercial development & support of product operations across the energy product portfolio.
Responsibilities
Design and develop new energy products and services through partnerships, creating differentiated and commercially viable propositions
Manage and optimize the commercial presence of energy products, services and offers across Volton’s digital platforms, with emphasis on the digital app MyOn
Manage Salesforce CRM platform related to new packages & promos setup, UATs, configurations etc
Monitor the energy market, competition and market trends to identify opportunities for new products and services and optimize the existing portfolio
Monitor regulatory developments and cooperate with the relevant teams to ensure timely implementation of requirements
Define product and process requirements and translate business needs into clear business requirements
Coordinate go-to-market activities and commercial initiatives in collaboration with internal stakeholders (MarCom, Legal, Regulatory, Operations, Customer Support, Sales etc)
Analyze customer needs and develop strong customer value propositions, taking into consideration different customer segments
Prepare financial analyses and business cases for new and existing products, services and partnerships
Monitor product and partnership performance through KPIs, including commercial performance, profitability and P&L
Manage the full product lifecycle, making recommendations for optimization, repositioning, redesign or withdrawal based on performance and business needs
