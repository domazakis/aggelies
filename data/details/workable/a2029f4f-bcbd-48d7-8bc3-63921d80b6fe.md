PeopleCert
is looking for an experienced and strategic
Brand & Corporate Communications Manager
to lead the development and execution of brand-driven campaigns across corporate brand, employer brand, and CSR, as well as the end-to-end, in-house management and delivery of non-commercial corporate events.
Working cross-functionally, the role will focus on building brand awareness, strengthening our corporate identity, enhancing corporate reputation, and driving engagement across internal and external audiences, while ensuring a consistent and compelling brand presence across all touchpoints.
As a Brand & Corporate Communications Manager, your tasks will be the following:
Drive the execution and rollout of brand-focused campaigns, including corporate brand, employer brand, and CSR initiatives.
Manage the end-to-end planning and delivery of non-commercial corporate events, including company-hosted events, third-party event participation, sponsorships, and executive engagements, from initial concept and coordination through setup, on-site execution, and post-event reporting, across HR, ESG, and corporate brand initiatives.
Develop, refine and enforce brand guidelines to ensure consistency across all internal and external communications.
Shape and manage the brand narrative through storytelling and content aligned with business priorities and values, ensuring consistency, clarity, and impact across all communication channels.
Monitor brand perception and public sentiment, proactively identifying risks and opportunities.
Evaluate campaign performance and engagement metrics; report insights and recommend improvements.
Support internal communications that reinforce culture and brand advocacy.
What we look for:
Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Marketing, Public Relations, or a related field. A Master’s degree is a plus.
8+ years of experience in brand communications and corporate events, either in-house or within a creative/advertising/pr agency.
At least 5 years of hands-on experience managing company-hosted events, corporate participation in third-party events, sponsorships, and executive engagements, with responsibility for planning, budgets, suppliers, coordination, execution, and post-event reporting.
Proven track record of delivering successful brand campaigns, including employer brand and CSR efforts.
Strong storytelling, writing, and editing skills.
Strong project management skills, with the ability to manage high-impact, cross-functional projects in fast-paced environments.
Solid communication and stakeholder management skills.
Excellent command of the English language (C2 level certification desired; LanguageCert C2 LTE or C2 IESOL certificate would be a plus).
Advanced computer literacy is required; ECDL Advanced level certification is desirable.
