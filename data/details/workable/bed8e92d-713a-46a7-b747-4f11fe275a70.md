Support clarification requests, grant preparation and the handover of successful proposals to the project delivery team.

What We Are Looking For
Demonstrable experience writing and submitting European funding proposals, with clear personal responsibility for substantive sections or complete applications.
Excellent written English and the ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly and persuasively.
Working knowledge of cybersecurity, including areas such as cyber threat intelligence, application security, vulnerability management, security operations and critical infrastructure protection.
Understanding of AI and machine learning concepts, including generative AI, intelligent automation, trustworthy AI and the security of AI systems.
Familiarity with quantum technologies and their security implications, particularly quantum computing, post-quantum cryptography and quantum-safe communications.
Knowledge of the European Defence Fund, including its research and development focus, call documentation, proposal requirements, evaluation approach and consortium considerations.
Ability to connect technical objectives with measurable outcomes, implementation plans, budgets and exploitation opportunities.
Strong research, organisational and stakeholder coordination skills, with the ability to manage parallel deadlines and maintain document quality.
Confidence working independently while actively seeking input and validation from technical and business colleagues.
Additional Experience We Would Value
A substantive contribution to proposals selected for funding, particularly in cybersecurity, AI, quantum technologies or defence.
Direct involvement in EDF proposal writing or consortium development.
Experience with defence research, dual-use technologies or collaboration with industrial partners and research institutions.
Familiarity with the EU Funding & Tenders Portal and other funding submission platforms.
Experience developing exploitation strategies, business cases, intellectual property approaches and commercialisation plans.
Experience supporting grant preparation, project reporting or technical deliverables after a funding award.
You will work directly with the people developing our technologies and delivering our services. This will give you access to the technical evidence, practical use cases and business priorities needed to build well-grounded proposals and contribute to our research and innovation direction.
