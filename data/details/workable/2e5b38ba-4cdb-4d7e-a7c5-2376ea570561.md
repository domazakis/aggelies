The opportunity
Our Tier 1 ranked Restructuring team is growing!
We are currently seeking an experienced Legal Professional to join the Legal team of Platis – Anastassiadis & Associates Law Partnership. The successful candidate will be involved in a broad range of matters, including commercial litigation, dispute resolution, restructuring and insolvency proceedings, debt recovery, contract drafting and negotiation, as well as general corporate and commercial law projects across various industry sectors.
The role requires active involvement in court proceedings and contentious matters, as well as the preparation, review and negotiation of a wide spectrum of commercial agreements. You will work closely with our clients to identify legal and commercial risks, provide practical and business-oriented advice, and support the successful delivery of complex legal engagements.
You will be expected to build and maintain strong internal and external relationships, anticipate and identify risks within engagements, and proactively address issues as they arise. In addition, you will stay informed of applicable legal developments, professional standards, and firm policies and procedures, applying this knowledge effectively to diverse client situations.
In return, you can expect leading - class learning and development tailored to your unique interests and motivations. You will be given all the skills, knowledge and opportunities to progress and become ready to build a better working world for our people, our clients and the communities that support us.
