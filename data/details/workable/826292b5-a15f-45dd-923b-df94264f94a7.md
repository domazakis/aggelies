Role Purpose
We are looking for a
Performance Marketing Manager (Mid-Level)
with a strong data-driven mindset and passion for measurable results.
The primary purpose of this role is to design, manage, and optimize digital advertising campaigns to maximize efficiency (ROI / ROAS), aligned with each client’s strategy and business objectives.
The successful candidate will support and execute multi-channel performance campaigns while actively contributing to strategic planning and continuous optimization.
Direct Reporting Line:
Performance Marketing Director / Senior Performance Marketing Manager
Manage and optimize campaigns across: Google Ads (Search, Display, YouTube), Facebook Ads / Instagram Ads, TikTok Ads, Programmatic platforms
Develop and execute multi-channel performance strategies in collaboration with the Senior Performance Marketing Manager
Monitor, analyze, and optimize campaigns based on KPIs and client feedback
Prepare monthly and ad-hoc performance reports using: Google Analytics, Other data analytics platforms (e.g., Smart Pixel, Social Analytics, etc.), Set up and maintain goal tracking systems
Accurately log time spent on tasks in Wrike
Ensure deadlines are met and proactively inform the Performance Director / Senior Performance Marketing Manager / Account Lead if timelines are at risk
Present campaign performance results to the Account Lead
Communicate directly with clients to gather feedback and adjust strategies accordingly
Collaborate closely with Account and Creative teams to deliver integrated campaign results
Maintain and renew professional certifications (Google, Meta, Programmatic, Analytics, etc.)
Ideal Candidate Profile
4–6 years of experience in a performance marketing role
Strong knowledge of digital advertising platforms
Analytical mindset with the ability to translate data into actionable insights
Excellent organizational and time management skills
Client-focused approach
Google / Meta / Analytics certifications are considered a strong advantage
