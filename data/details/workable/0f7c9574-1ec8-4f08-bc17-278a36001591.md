We are seeking a
Project Manager
to join our Service Delivery and Operations Delivery team in Athens. The main duties will be to lead and deliver complex software projects end-to-end, ensuring alignment with business objectives, quality standards, timelines, and budgets. The role requires strong expertise in software development lifecycle (SDLC), Agile and DevOps practices, and the ability to manage cross-functional and distributed teams. Acting as the primary point of contact for clients and stakeholders, the Senior Project Manager oversees planning, execution, risk management, and reporting, while driving collaboration, continuous improvement, and successful project outcomes in dynamic and demanding environments.
Responsibilities:
Support the planning, execution, monitoring, and successful delivery of projects and service engagements;
Coordinate project activities, resources, timelines, and deliverables to ensure commitments are met;
Collaborate with project teams, business stakeholders, customers, partners, and vendors to drive project success;
Facilitate effective communication among project team members and stakeholders through regular meetings, status updates, and reporting;
Monitor project progress, milestones, dependencies, risks, and issues, ensuring timely escalation when necessary;
Support project planning activities, including schedules, resource allocation, workload management, and forecasting;
Track project budgets, effort consumption, resource utilization, and overall delivery performance;
Coordinate consultant onboarding, offboarding, and staffing activities for projects and service contracts;
Monitor timesheet submissions and effort reporting to ensure accuracy and compliance with project and contractual requirements;
Prepare and present project status reports, dashboards, KPIs, and progress updates to management and customers;
Maintain project documentation, action logs, risk registers, decision logs, and governance records;
Organize and support project governance activities, including status meetings, steering committees, service reviews, and working groups;
Ensure alignment between business requirements, customer expectations, and project execution;
Identify opportunities to improve processes, project delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction;
Ensuring that all activities and duties are carried out in full compliance with regulatory requirements and supporting the continued implementation of the Group Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy;
