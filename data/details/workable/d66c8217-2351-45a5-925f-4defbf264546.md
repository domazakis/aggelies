The role
We’re now looking for a Senior Performance Marketing Specialist to support and scale our paid acquisition efforts across key digital channels. This is a high-impact role within our Marketing team, responsible for executing, optimising, and expanding performance campaigns to drive measurable growth.
You will work closely with the Head of Performance Marketing to implement the paid media strategy, manage significant budgets, and continuously improve performance across channels.
Responsibilities:
As our Senior Performance Marketing Specialist, you will:
Plan, build, and optimise paid media campaigns across major channels (Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Ads, Apple Search Ads, Tik Tok, etc.), ensuring alignment with overall acquisition goals
Own end-to-end campaign execution, including account structure, keyword research, audience segmentation, ad creation, tracking validation, and launch
Manage day-to-day campaign performance, continuously optimising bids, budgets, targeting, placements, and creatives to maintain strong ROAS and POAS
Contribute to paid media budget planning and forecasting, ensuring effective spend distribution across channels.
Design and run structured A/B tests across creatives, copy, landing pages, and bidding strategies, documenting insights and scaling successful learnings
Collaborate with Design, Product, and BI teams to improve tracking, attribution, landing page performance, and overall conversion funnel efficiency
Identify and execute scaling opportunities within existing campaigns, as well as expansion into new segments, geographies, and audience types
Prepare regular performance reports and deliver actionable insights and optimisation recommendations to the Head of Performance Marketing and CMO
Stay up to date with platform updates, beta features, automation tools, and industry best practices to continuously improve execution and efficiency
4+ years of hands-on experience in performance marketing, ideally in a fast-paced B2C environment
Proven experience in managing and scaling multi-channel paid advertising campaigns
Experience managing substantial monthly budgets with clear efficiency and growth targets
Strong understanding of digital advertising platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Apple Search Ads, Universal App Campaigns, etc.)
Good understanding of attribution, tracking implementation, and performance measurement principles
Comfortable working with data, spreadsheets, and performance dashboards to extract insights and support decision-making
Experience running structured A/B tests and optimisation experiments
A proactive, hands-on approach with strong execution skills and attention to detail
Fluency in English (Greek, Italian, or Spanish is a plus)
Experience using AI-powered platforms is considered a plus
