About the role
You will coordinate the performance team and plan, manage and optimize digital advertising campaigns across Google, Meta, TikTok and programmatic platforms. You will design each client's performance strategy, support the team in setting up campaigns and improve efficiency and return on ad spend, in line with each client's goals.
You will report to the Performance Marketing Director.
What you'll do
Design the performance strategy for each client, and plan multi-channel campaigns.
Support the team in setting up and running campaigns on Google, Meta, TikTok, programmatic and every other platform we use.
Track KPIs, analyze data and optimize campaigns based on the data and client feedback.
Monitor each client's competitors, find new ways to grow the client's performance results and adjust the plan.
Review and sign off monthly and ad-hoc reports, and track key metrics such as CTR, conversion rate and ROAS.
Set up and maintain goal tracking in Google Analytics and other analytics platforms.
Work with the Account and Creative teams, and with clients, to understand their needs and solve issues as they arise.
Lead and develop your sub-team, from interns and executives to managers.
Keep your Google, Meta and programmatic certifications up to date, and suggest better ways to deliver the service.
Use AI tools in your daily work and bring them into the team's workflows, guiding mid-level and junior members in using them well.
You'll also contribute to
Shaping the client plan.
Correct invoicing of the department's services.
The profitability and revenue of the service and of each client.
New business proposals (specs) and client presentations.
How we measure success
ROI and ROAS.
CPA and conversion rate from campaigns.
Budget management accuracy.
AI adoption in the team's workflows.
Satisfaction of clients whose campaigns we run (NPS, CSAT).
What we're looking for
3+ years of experience in performance marketing, ideally within a creative, advertising or digital agency.
Proven experience planning and running multi-channel paid campaigns, from strategy to optimization.
A strong focus on results such as ROAS, CPA and conversion rate, and on managing budgets accurately.
Advanced knowledge of Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, programmatic platforms, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Looker Studio.
Google Ads and Meta certifications, or the willingness to get them.
Proven experience integrating AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude into team workflows and guiding others in using them well.
Experience guiding and giving feedback to junior team members.
Analytical thinking, organization and clear communication of results.
Professional-level Greek and fluent English (written and spoken).
