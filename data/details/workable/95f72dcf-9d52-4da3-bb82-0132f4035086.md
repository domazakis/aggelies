Are you a seasoned IT professional with expertise in project management? Join us in Athens or work remotely, and become part of our team as a
Senior IT Consultant / Project Manager
. You'll play a crucial role in the life-cycle of software implementation and maintenance projects, coordinating and communicating effectively to ensure successful project delivery.
What You'll Do:
Prepare and maintain the project's time plans and quality plans, coordinate the activities of the implementation/maintenance teams, and elaborate status reports;
Participate in business analysis and project management meetings and workshops;
Compile, review, and validate requirements and specifications, at both business and technical levels;
Carry out functional testing and prepare end-user documentation;
Liaise with clients and manage expectations.
Deliver Consulting, Training, and Coaching services to clients;
University degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field;
At minimum 2 years experience in Project Management of e-Government projects;
At minimum 3 years experience in Business Analysis of web development projects;
Hands-on experience in the development and maintenance of web development projects, in the government sector;
Good knowledge and experience in Scrum, Agile, Prince2, and ITIL;
Excellent command of English, both written and spoken.
