Role Purpose
The Marketing Specialist plays a key role in executing Noisis’ marketing initiatives, supporting the company’s growth, brand positioning, and digital presence.
The successful candidate will be expected not only to implement marketing activities but also to evaluate opportunities, influence decisions, and actively contribute to how marketing drives business growth.
This role operates as a central marketing function, requiring ownership across multiple areas rather than a narrow specialization. It offers high visibility and the opportunity to work closely with leadership and external partners.
The role requires a combination of hands-on execution, analytical thinking, and business judgment, ensuring that marketing initiatives deliver measurable impact.

Plan and execute digital marketing campaigns that support brand awareness, lead generation, and customer engagement.

Monitor and analyze campaign performance using tools such as Google Analytics, identifying clear, actionable opportunities for improvement
Take ownership of planning and prioritizing marketing initiatives, balancing business needs, available budget, and expected impact
Develop and continuously improve the end-to-end lead generation funnel, from awareness to conversion, identifying gaps and optimization opportunities
Evaluate marketing proposals from external agencies or partners and contribute to decisions regarding advertising investments, campaign strategies, and budget allocation
Coordinate and manage collaborations with external partners including advertising agencies, digital marketing providers, and other service partners, ensuring high-quality output and measurable results
Manage email marketing campaigns using Mailchimp (or similar platforms), including content creation, audience segmentation, and performance tracking
Support the management and continuous improvement of the company’s website (WordPress), ensuring content is relevant, accurate, and aligned with business priorities
Drive and coordinate the development of content initiatives (e.g. articles, insights, newsletters), strengthening the company’s market presence and credibility
Collaborate with internal teams to ensure consistent, professional, and effective brand communication across all marketing channels
Conduct market and competitor analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for differentiation
Identify new channels, tools, or approaches that can improve marketing effectiveness and efficiency
Contribute actively to marketing strategy discussions, bringing data-driven insights and practical ideas that influence decisions
Operate with a hands-on, problem-solving mindset, taking initiative in a fast-evolving and not fully structured environment
