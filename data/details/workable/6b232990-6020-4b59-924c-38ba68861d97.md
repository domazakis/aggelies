The Role
Reporting to the Marketing & Communications Manager, you will be the operational heart of our marketing team. You’ll connect our internal teams, hotel teams, regional sales & brand teams, external partners so that campaigns, collateral and content get delivered on time and on brand. This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys variety, works well with many stakeholders, and wants to grow in hospitality marketing.
We are seeking a Marketing Assistant / Executive to support the Marketing & Communications Manager and the wider marketing team. The successful candidate will provide operational, administrative and creative support, and will coordinate between internal departments, hotels and external partners to ensure the smooth delivery of marketing activities.
What You’ll Do
Support the coordination of digital marketing campaigns managed by external agencies, including asset delivery and timelines
Liaise with external designers on marketing projects, ensuring clear briefs, organised feedback and on-time delivery
Handle general inquiries and route them to the appropriate departments or hotels
Coordinate the production and printing of brochures, signage and promotional materials
Manage the distribution and inventory of marketing collateral across hotel properties
Update website and mobile application content via CMS platforms
Organise and archive digital assets and media files
Create and adapt marketing materials using Canva, in line with brand guidelines
Assist in reviewing influencer and media collaboration proposals in cooperation with the PR agency and Brand Strategist
Support the preparation of press trips, including rates, accommodation requests and itineraries
Maintain the marketing calendar, files and databases
Process marketing invoices and coordinate with suppliers
Support the preparation of submissions for industry awards and nominations
What We’re Looking For
A degree in Marketing, Communications, Tourism or a related field
1–3 years of experience in marketing, ideally in hospitality, travel or lifestyle
Excellent written and spoken English and Greek
Familiarity with digital marketing, design & emailing tools  (Meta, Google Analytics, Canva, Mailchimp, Moosend etc); familiarity with CMS platforms (e.g. WordPress) is a plus
Strong organisational skills and the ability to juggle multiple projects and deadlines
A collaborative, can-do attitude and a keen eye for detail and brand consistency
Good knowledge of MS Office / Google Workspace
