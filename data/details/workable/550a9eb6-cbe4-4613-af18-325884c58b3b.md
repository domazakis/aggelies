We currently have a vacancy for an
R&D Manager for Health software applications
, fluent in English, to offer his/her services as an expert based in Athens, Greece or Luxembourg, working remotely. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to be a part of a top-rated team of talented individuals with different expertise working collaboratively on common goals. We are looking for a person who is outstanding in responsiveness, integrity, and reliability, with excellent analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills, as well as a strong ability to work in teams for supporting projects funded by the European Commission and national research frameworks. You will be expected to show initiative, be flexible, and take responsibility. With experience in applications in Health software applications, and/or other related technologies, focusing on the software aspects. Additional expertise in the domain of European-funded R&D projects will be appreciated.
What You'll Do:
Management and implementation of EC-funded IT projects;
Elaboration of the full suite of document deliverables and reports for R&D projects and business development activities (requirements, conceptual models, specifications, implementation and integration plans, etc.);
Design, development, and documentation of novel applications, solutions, and technologies in Health including AI and Data, ethics, and personalized health services and other related projects;
Design and implement next-generation health, data, and AI technologies.
University degree in Electrical or Computing Engineering/ Data and AI sciences, preferably with postgraduate qualification at the PhD level, focusing on software development;
At least 4 years experience in working directly with technical teams with proven track records;
Ideally 2 years experience specifically in National or European research programs in ICT, Data Economy and/or Health;
Experience and strong background in software design and development, software development lifecycle, and ICT project management;
Experience in the Health, Data, and AI domains, with previous research or commercial projects, including patient engagement, interaction, gamification, virtual and augmented reality, UX, IoT, and mobile apps especially in terms of upcoming health requirements;
Excellent command of English and Greek, both written and oral;
Good interpersonal, organization, and communication skills, with the ability to work in a diverse team environment;
Good analytical skills, as well as a proactive attitude towards solving problems.
