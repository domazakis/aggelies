# One Group | One Power
We are Enerwave, member of HELLENiQ ENERGY and a leader in providing outstanding and innovative energy solutions. We operate with passion, being engaged in heart and mind to what we do, and we pride ourselves on offering our employees a place where they can excel, creating value. We are offering now a set of exciting positions in our headquarters in Athens, across multiple departments and areas of expertise.
We are currently looking for a Product Manager - Large Accounts is an important member of the Product & Portfolio Management squad. Along with the respective Senior Team Leader, they work on the development of Enerwave’s product portfolio for Large Accounts, the most demanding part of our customer base. Large Accounts, refer to big industrial & commercial enterprises that request for sophisticated energy products/ offers to cover their needs. Product Manager, Large Accounts is an experienced individual contributor that acts as a product expert & ambassador in the area of his expertise.
What will you do:
Development of customized pricing offers for new, existing Large Accounts for Electricity and Natural Gas products
New product development (research, design, evaluation)
Support the analysis of big energy data
Development of pricing strategies & tactics in order to achieve company objectives
Management and monitoring of all pricing offers requests
Support the load declaration forecasting model
Development and management of Large Accounts Electricity and Natural Gas standardized products and bundles
Weekly and monthly reporting
Ad-hoc analyses & business cases
Provide various forms of support to other collaborating teams & stakeholders
What you need to be successful:
A BSc in Business/ Economics, Engineering and related fields
A MSc or MBA will be considered a plus
At least 3 years experience in relevant experience in Energy & Utilities, Telcos, Management and Consulting industries
Excellent command of the English language
Advanced knowledge in Excel, Word, PowerPoint
Knowledge of the electricity market will be considered an asset
Excellent analytical skills and clear ability to deal with large datasets
Effective communication skills and the willingness to work well with others to achieve a common goal
Expert in using Excel and other Microsoft Office products
Strong attention to detail
