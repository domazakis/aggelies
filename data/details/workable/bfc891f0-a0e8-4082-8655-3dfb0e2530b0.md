Destsetters
, one of the leading hospitality consulting firms specializing in concept and strategy, is seeking a
Digital Content Creator
to join its creative and marketing operations team.
The role involves developing and overseeing digital content across multiple platforms — including social media, websites, and digital campaigns — ensuring that all outputs align with Destsetters’ premium communication standards.
The ideal candidate combines strong creative direction with operational consistency, handling both content creation and digital platform management with precision.
Role and Responsibilities:
Research:
Conduct thorough market research to identify trends, competitive offerings, and consumer preferences that inform the development of new hotel concepts.
Social Media Content Creation
:
Develop engaging and high-quality content (visuals, copy, video snippets) across all social media channels, maintaining brand consistency and strategic tone.
Website & Platform Content Management
:
Update, edit, and supervise website content, ensuring alignment with brand messaging and optimization for performance and user experience.
Video Editing & Supervision
:
Edit short-form videos for campaigns, reels, and digital platforms while supervising outsourced editing work to ensure quality and alignment with concept standards.
Online Marketing Support
:
Assist in planning and executing digital campaigns (email, social ads, Google campaigns), coordinating with internal and external teams for proper implementation.
Content Alignment & Consistency
:
Ensure that all digital materials — from social posts to landing pages — follow Destsetters’ certified branding methodology, maintaining both creative excellence and operational discipline.
