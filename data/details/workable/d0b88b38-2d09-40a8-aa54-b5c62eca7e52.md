We are seeking a
Project Manager/ Release Manager
to join our Service Delivery and Operations Delivery team in Athens.
Responsibilities:
Develop, maintain, and communicate integrated release plans, schedules, and deployment calendars.
Coordinate release activities across multiple development teams, applications, environments, and external vendors.
Manage release scope, milestones, dependencies, and resource requirements.
Ensure alignment between project delivery schedules, release priorities, and business objectives.
Coordinate release readiness assessments, including testing completion, deployment prerequisites, and operational preparedness.
Facilitate release planning meetings, readiness reviews, and Go/No-Go discussions.Ensure deployment, rollback, and contingency plans are prepared and validated.
Monitor release execution, coordinate issue resolution, and escalate critical blockers.
Coordinate post-deployment verification, stabilization, and transition to operational support.
Capture Risks and Issues and provide mitigation plans in cooperation with the relevant teams.
Adhere to and safeguard the process of all releases phases.
Ensuring that all activities and duties are carried out in full compliance with regulatory requirements and supporting the continued implementation of the Group Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy;
Αctively ensuring compliance with the Group's Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy and all applicable regulations, identifying and reporting potential risks related to bribery and corruption, and promoting ethical behavior by participating in and encouraging continuous training and awareness initiatives.
University degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related technical field;
Minimum 5 years of experience in handling IT projects;
Strong Technical background (e.g., prior software engineering experience) is highly desirable;
Strong understanding of SW Development Life-cycle (SDLC), Defect Management and/or Quality Assurance.
Proficiency with project and release management tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, ServiceNow, MS Project, or equivalent.
Ability to follow various SDLC models (mainly Waterfall but also Agile Framework).
Project Management, strong communication and interpersonal skills, problem-solving ability.
Ability to negotiate and collaborate with internal and external stakeholders.
Attention to the detail, ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.
Excellent communication and organizational skills.
Certification on Project Management Methodology (PMP, PMI-ACP, PRINCE 2) will be considered a plus.
Very good knowledge of written and spoken English is required
