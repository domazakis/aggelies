, a leading Integrated Marketing and Sales services company, , is seeking a talented and experienced
Digital Marketing Specialist

About the role:
As a
Digital Marketing Specialist
, you will design and deploy digital communication strategy across digital ecosystem for product, commercial and brand initiatives, by ensuring that they: are aligned with business needs and strategic priorities, serve brand's role, objectives and guidelines and meet consumer needs based on their Consumer Journey stage.
Responsibilities:
Develop content for digital communication campaigns across digital touchpoints and social media channels.
Handle the overall direction and integration of digital assets, such as website, e-partners, social media and content management.
Be responsible for website content design and development.
For all assigned projects:
Design and develop the digital communication toolbox across digital touchpoints.
Closely collaborate with Brand teams for digital developments.
Capitalize on Global toolboxes, adapting (where necessary) to meet local needs.
Promote collaboration with internal stakeholders and external partners (3rd party agencies & suppliers).
Monitor overall results, evaluate impact of digital communication and recommend next steps.
Ensure adherence to budget limitations.
Requirements:
University Degree in Marketing/Business Administration, Communications, Digital Marketing. MBA or MSc will be considered an asset.
Minimum 3-5 years of proven experience in a position which combines great track record in managing successfully brand digital marketing exposure. Experience working on an international brand/e-shop is considered as a plus.
Excellent knowledge of Digital Marketing and Social Media.
Strong analytical and communication skills (both in Greek and in English).
Great collaborative spirit.
Agility and ability to deal in a continuously changing environment.
