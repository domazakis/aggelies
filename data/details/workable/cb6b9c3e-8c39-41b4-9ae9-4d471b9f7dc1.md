Sleed
, a digital marketing agency with more than 10 years of field experience and a strategic partner of Google, is looking for a Digital Media Executive to join our Digital Department.
In this role you will be hands-on with the daily running of our clients’ paid media: you will set up and optimize campaigns on Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads, report on their results, and handle the day-to-day operational communication with the client.
You will report to a Digital Media Specialist, under the Digital Media Manager, and on every client account you will work alongside a senior Strategy and Media colleague. It is an operations-focused position for someone with 1–2 years of experience who is ready to take ownership of media execution, with continuous training to grow fast.
Responsibilities
Campaign Setup:
Set up campaigns on Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads, from structure, audiences, budgets and bidding to ad copy and creatives, following the strategy and media plan agreed for each client. Make sure conversion tracking and UTMs are in place before going live.
Optimization:
Monitor performance daily and optimize continuously (budgets, bids, audiences, placements, creative and copy testing) to reach each client’s KPIs such as ROAS, CPA or CPL. Flag risks, anomalies and opportunities to the team early.
Reporting:
Refresh data and maintain accurate, easy-to-read reports in Looker Studio or other internal platforms, combining data from the ad platforms and web analytics tools. Go beyond the numbers: explain what happened, why, and what we do next, to demonstrate the return on advertising spend to clients and company management.
Day-to-Day Communication:
Be the everyday contact for clients on practical media matters: request what you need to move forward (assets, approvals, access, information) and share routine updates. Work directly with the client’s team, including their creatives, on briefs and debriefs for campaign visuals. Strategic discussions stay with the senior Strategy and Media colleagues of the account.
AI-Powered Work:
Use AI tools in your daily work (for example for analysis, ad copy and creative ideas, reporting and repetitive tasks) and keep exploring how they can improve our work and our clients’ results. Share what works with the team.
Teamwork & Self-Driven:
Work closely with media specialists, strategists, designers and colleagues of every seniority. Freely share knowledge, tests and best practices, and keep up to date with platform changes and industry developments with a research &amp; development attitude.
Main KPIs:
Campaign performance against client goals, budget pacing, accuracy and timeliness of reports, responsiveness and quality of day-to-day communication, and good task and time management.
Be a Sleeder:
Take ownership, embrace adaptability, demonstrate resilience, spread positivity, and bring human passion to everything you do.
Experience:
1–2 years of experience in digital marketing or paid media.
Paid Media Platforms:
Hands-on contact with Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads: you have worked in the platforms and understand how campaigns are set up and optimized.
Reporting:
Experience viewing and building reports in Looker Studio.
AI User:
You already use AI tools in your daily work and want to keep growing in how you use them as they evolve.
Communication:
Excellent written and oral communication skills in Greek and English, and comfort communicating with clients on a daily basis.
