Required:
Bachelor’s degree in Electrical, Electronic, Telecommunications, Computer or Network Engineering, or a related discipline.
5–8 years of relevant experience in product management, pre-sales, solutions engineering, systems engineering or a similar technical/customer-facing role.
Strong knowledge of radio/RF communications and tactical communication systems.
Good understanding of IP networking, routing and network architectures.
Ability to analyse complex requirements and develop coherent system-level solutions.
