We introduce high technology in our state-of-the-art sterile manufacturing plant and create new production lines with cutting-edge technology and robotic equipment. About the role
Communications Specialist
RAFARM is looking for a creative and proactive
Communications Specialist
to support the development and implementation of corporate communication initiatives, reporting to the Head of Corporate Communications & ESG.
Key Responsibilities
Develop engaging corporate content in Greek and English for internal and external audiences.
Manage and enhance RAFARM’s corporate LinkedIn presence and support website updates.
Implement internal and external communication plans and campaigns.
Prepare corporate announcements, presentations and communication materials.
Support corporate events, employee engagement, wellbeing and volunteering initiatives.
Contribute to ESG and sustainability communications.
Monitor media coverage and pharmaceutical industry trends.
Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to ensure consistent and effective communication.
Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Public Relations, Marketing, Journalism or a related field.
Professional experience in corporate communications, content creation or a similar role.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Greek and English.
Familiarity with LinkedIn, digital communication channels and content management.
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities.
Creativity, initiative and excellent collaboration skills.
