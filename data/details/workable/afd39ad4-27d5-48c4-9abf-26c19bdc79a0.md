The Project Manager is responsible for managing defined projects or workstreams, ensuring clear planning, coordination and delivery across teams and stakeholders.
This role focuses on:
Maintaining clear project plans and timelines.
Driving alignment and coordination across functions.
Managing risks, dependencies and governance.
Providing visibility on progress, decisions and next steps.
Key Responsibilities
Project Planning, Delivery & Control
Own and maintain clear project plans, milestones, scope and timelines.
Track progress and ensure plans remain realistic and actionable.
Monitor delivery health, manage risks, dependencies and changes.
Support impact assessment and maintain control over scope and priorities.
Cross-Functional Coordination & Execution
Coordinate across Product, Engineering, Delivery and other stakeholders.
Identify dependencies, resolve blockers and drive actions to completion.
Ensure alignment across teams and maintain delivery momentum.
Mobilisation, Governance & Reporting
Support shaping and structuring work before execution.
Run governance, maintain RAID logs and ensure follow-through on actions.
Provide clear reporting on status, risks, decisions and next steps.
Stakeholder Management & Communication
Build strong relationships across teams and functions.
Facilitate meetings, workshops and planning sessions.
Communicate clearly and support informed decision-making.
Experience & Qualifications
5+ years of experience managing technology projects, workstreams or delivery initiatives across complex, multi-team environments, ideally within the lottery, gaming or wider technology sectors.
Proven experience managing project plans, governance, RAID and delivery reporting in technology-led environments.
Experience coordinating work across multiple teams, functions and stakeholders.
Strong track record of maintaining delivery control, momentum and follow-through across defined scopes of work.
Experience working across Agile, hybrid and phased delivery environments.
Good understanding of project governance, dependency management, milestone tracking and change control.
Strong working knowledge of project and delivery management tooling, including the ability to manage plans, dependencies, RAID and reporting using tools such as MS Project, Jira and Confluence.
Experience operating in regulated, high-accountability or enterprise environments is advantageous.
Relevant certifications such as PRINCE2, PMP, CAPM or equivalent are desirable.
Key Competencies
Strong planning and coordination skills.
Ability to manage complexity and competing priorities.
Effective stakeholder management and influencing skills.
Clear and structured communication.
Proactive, organised and results-oriented approach.
Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking.
