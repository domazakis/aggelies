Develop and maintain compelling content calendar across digital channels & social media channels aligned with marketing objectives and campaigns. Manage marketing agencies and third parties ensuring effective collaboration, clear briefs, high-quality deliverables and timely execution. Explore new content formats, digital trends and emerging platforms that can strengthen Edenred’s communication.
