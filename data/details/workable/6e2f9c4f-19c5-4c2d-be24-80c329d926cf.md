Key Responsibilities
Create and maintain consistent brand visuals across digital platforms, with occasional adaptations for print materials.
Develop key visuals and creative assets for 360° marketing campaigns across multiple digital touchpoints.
Ensure all designs align with brand guidelines and visual identity standards
Collaborate closely with the Graphic Design Manager, Marketing team and other internal stakeholders
Implement feedback efficiently and manage multiple design tasks in parallel, while working within clear deadlines
Participate in brainstorming sessions and contribute creative ideas
Stay up to date with design trends, tools and best practices, especially within the retail/sports industry
Translate business goals into clear and effective visual design solutions
Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design, Visual Arts or a related field.
1-2 years of professional experience in graphic design.
Strong portfolio showcasing digital-first design projects. Experience with print design will be considered a plus.
Understanding of digital design , brand identity & marketing needs
Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and Figma
Experience working with web layouts, landing pages or CMS platforms will be considered an asset.
Detail-oriented with good time management skills and a strong sense of ownership and accountability
Passion for design, creativity and visual storytelling
