TELCOCOM DATA HELLAS, a subsidiary of FiberHome, a global leader in optical networking and digital infrastructure solutions, is seeking an experienced Project Manager for our Fibre Optic Infrastructure (PPC Projects) team. Based in Greece, our company excels in the deployment of FTTH networks, optical fiber systems, and large-scale telecommunications projects nationwide. We pride ourselves on our commitment to high-quality engineering and reliable field operations while focusing on continuous development for our people within a safe, collaborative, and growth-oriented environment. As a Project Manager at Telcocom Data Hellas, you will play a pivotal role in overseeing complex projects in fibre optic infrastructure, ensuring that each project is completed on time, within scope, and on budget. You will work closely with cross-functional teams in a dynamic environment, managing resources, timelines, and all project-related communication. If you are passionate about telecommunications and looking for an opportunity to lead innovative projects that have a significant impact on our communities and the industry, we invite you to join our team and help shape the future of connectivity in Greece.
Responsibilities
Lead and manage fibre optic infrastructure projects from inception to completion.
Develop detailed project plans, including scope, timelines, resources, and budgets.
Coordinate and communicate with internal teams, subcontractors, and stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.
Monitor project progress and ensure adherence to timelines and quality standards.
Identify and mitigate risks associated with project execution.
Prepare status reports and presentations for stakeholders and management.
Ensure compliance with safety regulations and company policies during project implementation.
Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Telecommunications, or a related field.
Proven experience in project management within the telecommunications industry, specifically with fibre optic projects.
Strong understanding of FTTH network deployment and optical fibre systems.
Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
Proficiency in project management software and tools.
Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and meet deadlines.
Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to identifying and addressing challenges.
Competitive and rewarding salary package
Opportunities for continuous professional growth and career development

Ongoing training, education, and upskilling programs
