Are you passionate about building partnerships and winning new business?
Do you thrive in a fast-paced, target-driven and collaborative environment?
SpeakIt is looking for an ambitious Business Development Executive to join our team in Athens and help us grow our client base across Europe! 🌍
What you'll be doing
As a Business Development Executive, you will own the full sales cycle, from first conversation to signed contract, and become the commercial face of SpeakIt for your accounts. Your responsibilities will include:
Identifying new opportunities in business services, shared service centres, and international commercial and operational teams entering new markets or facing hiring challenges
Building your own pipeline through targeted calls, emails, LinkedIn, referrals, partnerships and industry events, while following up on enquiries and Marketing leads
Building relationships with HR and Talent Acquisition leaders, Operations directors, country managers, and Finance and Procurement decision-makers
Running discovery meetings to understand HR related needs hiring volumes, roles, languages, locations, deadlines, budgets and the client's decision process
Preparing tailored proposals and presentations that clearly show the value of our services
Working with our Recruitment team to confirm feasibility, resources and delivery commitments before closing
Acting as the commercial contact for your accounts, supporting client reviews and identifying repeat hiring, additional services and opportunities in new locations
Keeping CRM records and forecasts accurate, with clear next steps and expected revenue
What we're looking for
Must have:
2+ years of B2B sales or business development experience, with personal responsibility for finding opportunities, negotiating and closing new accounts
Proven ability to ask the right questions, understand client priorities and explain the value of a tailored service in clear terms
Experience engaging and negotiating with senior business buyers
An organised approach to prospecting, follow-up and CRM use
Professional fluency in English
Willingness to travel within Europe for client meetings and events
Nice to have:
Sales experience with HR software or other SaaS
Experience in recruitment, staffing or HR services
Why join SpeakIt?
At SpeakIt, we empower our people to learn, grow, and thrive in a collaborative environment. As part of our team you'll enjoy:
Hybrid working model
Competitive salary
Bonuses and incentives
Professional development: Support for ongoing learning and skill development
Work environment: A positive and inclusive work culture, collaborative teams, and a supportive management style
Private health insurance
Company laptop + mobile phone
