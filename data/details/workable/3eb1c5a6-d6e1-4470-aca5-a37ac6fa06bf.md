Responsibilities:
Takes responsibility for the overall planning, coordination, and delivery of corporate events, conferences, training initiatives, executive meetings, volunteering activities and other internal communication initiatives. At the same time, acts as a key point of contact for General Divisions and business units, identifying their needs, proposing appropriate solutions and ensuring the successful implementation of the relevant activities
Prepares briefs and coordinates the development of communication and branded materials (concepts, presentations, videos, talking points, etc.) in collaboration with advertising agencies and external partners, ensuring the consistent application of PPC’s corporate identity and communication standards
Oversees and coordinates suppliers, creative partners, production companies and other external partners, monitoring quality, timelines and compliance with agreed specifications. Additionally, proposes new ideas, event formats, technological solutions and best practices aimed at continuously enhancing employee experience and the quality of activities
Coordinates junior team members, providing guidance, transferring knowledge, supporting their professional development and monitoring the effective execution of assigned tasks. Also contributes to improving processes, tools and ways of working that enhance the team’s efficiency, quality and collaboration
Participates in the planning and monitoring of the budget for managed projects, coordinates the tender process, and ensures the proper completion of required corporate procedures and approvals
Bachelor’s degree from a University or College in Communication and Media, Humanities, Marketing, Advertising or Business Administration
Master’s degree in Communication and Media, Humanities, Marketing, Advertising, Business Administration
At least five (5) years of relevant experience in a Marketing Agency (ATL and/or BTL) or in communication or event management roles
Experience in roles involving management of internal events or project management will be considered an additional qualification
Excellent knowledge of the English language
Knowledge of Microsoft Office programs (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and Internet
Availability in case of emergency (upon phone contact)
Inclusive working environment
Continuous training and participation in development activities
