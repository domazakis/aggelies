Responsibilities:
Participates actively in the implementation and coordination of 360° communication campaigns as well as individual communication activities for brand communications, products, services and promotional actions
Designs, organizes and oversees Below the Line (BTL) activities and exhibitions, ensuring their execution, the proper promotion of the Company and their alignment with respective 360° campaigns
Prepares and drafts briefs for advertising agencies and external partners and collaborates closely with them for timely delivery of materials and completion of communication projects
Oversees promotional and communication materials, ensuring that their message is consistent and engaging, the creative idea is correctly conveyed across all media, and the corporate visual identity and tone of voice are faithfully applied
Monitors marketing developments to promote innovative ideas, systematically analyses competitors’ action and prepares reports and post-evaluations on campaign performance
Bachelor’s degree from a University or College or Technological Educational Institute in Marketing and Communication, Advertising or a related field
At least two (2) years of relevant experience in the management and implementation of 360° communication campaigns (experience in Offline/Above-the-Line media is required, beyond Digital)
Experience in Social Media channel management and digital advertising will be considered an additional qualification
Good knowledge of the English language
Knowledge of Microsoft Office programs (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and Internet
Availability for urgent geographical relocations
