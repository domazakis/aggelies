The Role
You will own the day-to-day delivery framework for WebOps projects, including website builds, redesigns, CMS implementations, migrations, integrations, and optimization work. You will work closely with clients, designers, developers, content specialists, and marketing or CRM consultants. You are accountable for the project plan and delivery process; technical and creative decisions remain with the relevant specialists.
The right candidate is organized, clear in communication, comfortable managing several workstreams, and able to turn business goals into a realistic plan that a team can deliver.
What You’ll Do
HubSpot CMS & WebOps Project Management
Lead website development projects from initial strategy through implementation.
Oversee HubSpot CMS website builds, ensuring seamless execution of design, content, and development.
Manage website migrations and replatforming projects, ensuring minimal downtime and SEO impact.
Collaborate with designers, developers, and marketers to ensure alignment between website goals and business objectives.
Ensure that HubSpot CMS websites are optimized for SEO, AEO, performance, and conversions.
Cross-Functional Team Coordination
Act as the bridge between developers, marketers, and clients, ensuring smooth project workflows.
Facilitate team stand-ups, sprint planning, and project status meetings to keep projects on track.
Apply Growth-Driven Design (GDD) principles to support continuous website improvement.
Use a structured project management approach, such as Agile, Scrum, or Kanban, to support collaboration and efficient delivery.
Process Optimization & Automation
Implement automation tools and workflows to streamline website management and updates.
Ensure HubSpot websites integrate with CRM, marketing automation, and analytics platforms.
Optimize website performance, speed, and accessibility to support a positive user experience.
Identify bottlenecks in website production workflows and implement solutions to improve scalability.
Client & Stakeholder Communication
Act as the main point of contact for clients, ensuring project expectations are met.
Conduct website training sessions for clients, empowering them to manage and optimize their HubSpot CMS.
Provide ongoing guidance on best practices for website governance, data security, and lead conversion tracking.
Translate technical concepts into clear, actionable insights for non-technical stakeholders.
