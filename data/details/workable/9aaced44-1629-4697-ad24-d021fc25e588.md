The Opportunity
We are seeking a motivated
SAP Project Management Coordinator
to support our SAP Technology Consulting team. This role is ideal for candidates ranging from recent graduates to those with a few years of experience in Project Management or in SAP Consulting. You will play a crucial role in assisting with SAP implementation projects, ensuring that our clients achieve their business transformation goals.
Your key responsibilities
Assist in managing project team roster and onboarding process for new team members
Maintain team on-site plan and vacation calendar
Assist in defining project management processes and quality standards.
Contribute to planning major phases, milestones, and deliverables in the system implementation lifecycle.
Ensure adherence to project methodologies and tools.
Maintain workshops plan & schedule workshops according to participants’ availability.
Maintain project deliverables, including status reports, minutes of meetings, timesheets, and project documentation.
Support project planning, monitor project execution and communicate with stakeholders to track activity and deliverables completion against planned dates.
Assist in monitoring project risks, actions, issues, and mitigation actions.
Assist in supporting team members on requests related to project management tools (e.g. JIRA)
To qualify for the Role, you must have
A university degree in information technology, engineering, business administration, or a related field.
Experience in SAP or Project Management is a plus, but not mandatory; we welcome candidates with varying levels of experience.
Strong organizational and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Greek and English.
Familiarity with MS-Project and MS-Office tools is desirable.
A passion for learning and developing skills in a fast-paced, multinational environment.
