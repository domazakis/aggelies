Πλήρης απασχόληση
Περιγραφή
We are the market leader in the electrical equipment sector, with dedicated Lighting, Photovoltaics, Industrial Equipment and Building Solutions divisions, as well as three state-of-the-art electrical panel manufacturing units.
Our combination of high-quality products, highly skilled people and an organised, agile structure makes us the preferred choice for our customers. We select and develop talented professionals whose outstanding performance enables us to seize every opportunity.
We continuously invest in our people, creating a dynamic, highly professional and innovative environment that promotes integrity, teamwork, passion and exceptional results. At the same time, we provide meaningful learning opportunities that support our employees’ personal and professional growth.
About the Role:
We are looking for an ICT (structure cabling and network physical infrastructure) Project Administrator to join our team and play a key role in coordinating and monitoring ICT projects and related customer orders.
The role will contribute to monitoring the performance of customer and digital journeys, evaluating processes and measuring the adoption of new digital solutions.
The position combines data analysis, business intelligence and transformation project support, turning operational and customer data into actionable insights that improve customer experience, process efficiency and business outcomes.
Working closely with the Customer Experience, Digital Transformation, Customer Service, Sales, Operations and IT teams, the role will support data-driven decision-making, continuous improvement and the successful delivery of digital initiatives.
Key Responsibilities:
Develop and maintain
reports and dashboards in Excel and Power BI, providing a consolidated view of Customer Experience, Customer Service, omnichannel operations and Digital Transformation project progress
Analyse
order, customer service, Voice of Customer and digital channel data to identify trends, pain points, root causes and improvement opportunities
Monitor
KPIs such as NPS, CSAT, CES, First Contact Resolution, service levels, conversion, adoption and process performance, highlighting deviations and recommending corrective actions
Support
Digital Transformation projects through data validation, requirements analysis, testing, UAT, adoption tracking and assessment of business outcomes
Map and evaluate
customer journeys and business processes, helping to identify friction points and design improved solutions
Prepare
presentations, executive reports and evidence-based recommendations for team meetings and business decision-making
Collaborate
with all company departments to ensure a consistent customer experience, resolve operational issues and track agreed improvement actions.
