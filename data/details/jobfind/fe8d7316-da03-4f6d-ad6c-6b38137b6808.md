GINI GROUP
, leader in fashion jewels and accessories is looking for Social Media Specialist-Content Creator!
Join our team for a unique working experience!
Do you have passion for Social Media and you want to take your next step in your career as a Social Media Specialist?
Do you enjoy planning, implementing and monitoring Social Media strategy? Do you enjoy increasing sales, brand awareness and improve Marketing efforts, by using Web analytics, e-mail marketing and SEO? If yes, then a very well-known commercial company is looking just for you!
You are responsible for:
Develop and implement social media strategies to increase brand awareness and engagement
Create, curate, and manage published content across various social media platforms
Monitor, analyze, and report on social media performance using analytics tools
Engage with followers and respond to inquiries in a timely manner
Ensure brand consistency and synergy across campaigns
Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities to refine strategies
Assist in the development of promotional materials and advertisements for social media campaigns
Απαραίτητα Προσόντα
Your Education & Qualifications are:
Proven experience 2 years min, in managing social media platforms for businesses or organizations
Strong understanding of social media trends, tools, and best practices
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both Greek and English
Experience with social media analytics tools to track performance metrics
You are a professional with:
Creative mindset with a passion for storytelling and engaging content creation
Ability to work independently as well as part of a team, managing multiple projects effectively
