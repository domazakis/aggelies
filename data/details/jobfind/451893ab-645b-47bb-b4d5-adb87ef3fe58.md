GEOSYSTEMS HELLAS S.A.,
a Geoinformatics company based in Pangrati (Athens), is seeking a
Dissemination, Communication & Exploitation Specialist (EU Funding / Horizon Europe)
to join our dynamic team.
About the Role
As a
Dissemination, Communication & Exploitation Specialist (EU Funding / Horizon Europe),
you will play a key role in maximizing the visibility, impact, and market potential of our EU-funded research projects. You will contribute to the successful delivery of projects by overseeing the design and implementation of strategic communication plans, ensuring the effective dissemination of scientific outcomes to diverse audiences, and driving exploitation activities to facilitate commercial or institutional adoption. Working closely with multi-partner European consortia and internal technical teams, you will support strategic decision-making regarding Intellectual Property and market uptake, ensure compliance with EU funding requirements, and foster stakeholder engagement to secure the long-term sustainability of project results.
About Geosystems Hellas
GEOSYSTEMS HELLAS S.A., is a leading provider in earth observation and downstream space application solutions.
About the team
At GEOSYSTEMS HELLAS S.A., we foster a collaborative and innovative work environment that encourages personal growth and professional development. Join our team and contribute to cutting-edge projects that have a positive impact on industries related to Downstream Space Applications, Security, Environmental Monitoring, Urban Planning, Cultural Heritage and more on subjects connected to the built environment and infrastructures.
