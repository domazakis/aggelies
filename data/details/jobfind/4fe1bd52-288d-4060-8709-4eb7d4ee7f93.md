Marketing Manager (6309/MM/ESS/GR)
Role Overview:
A highly hands-on, creative, and execution-focused role, covering marketing communications, digital marketing, content creation, CRM, social media, and campaign management. You will work closely with Brand Managers, Product Managers, internal stakeholders, and external agencies to strengthen brand presence and create engaging customer experiences across multiple channels.
Responsibilities:
Plan and execute integrated marketing communication campaigns across digital and offline channels.
Develop and coordinate the production of marketing content and assets, including brochures, videos, presentations, POS materials, digital content, and advertising materials.
Create engaging content for websites, social media, newsletters, campaigns, and promotional activities in both Greek and English.
Manage and update website content.
Coordinate social media activities.
Support product launches, events, exhibitions, promotional activities, and other marketing initiatives.
Manage CRM and email marketing campaigns, supporting customer engagement, retention, and lead nurturing.
Ensure consistent brand identity and high-quality execution across all communication touchpoints.
Monitor campaign performance, prepare post-campaign reports, and provide recommendations for improvement.
Stay informed about digital marketing trends, consumer behavior, and emerging communication channels.
Coordinate multiple projects simultaneously, ensuring deadlines, budgets, and quality standards are met.
Requirements:
Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration, or a related field.
5–8+ years of experience in Marketing, Communications, Digital Marketing, Content Marketing, or a similar role.
Strong content creation and copywriting skills in both Greek and English.
Proven experience in digital marketing, social media, and integrated campaign execution.
Good knowledge of SEO, GEO, email marketing, and paid digital campaigns.
Experience with CRM and email marketing platforms.
Previous experience working with creative, media, digital, or production agencies.
Strong project management and coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
