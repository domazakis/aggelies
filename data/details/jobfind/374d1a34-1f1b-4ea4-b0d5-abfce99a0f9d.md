THE OPPORTUNITY
Harris Kyriakides is currently seeking a motivated and experienced individual based in Greece to join our firm as a Digital Marketing Officer. This is a
fully remote position
, offering the opportunity to work from Greece while collaborating closely with our team in Cyprus.
MAIN DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
The successful candidate will be responsible for assisting with all business development needs of the firm and be a part of our Practice and Client Development Department. He/She will gain exposure to the full array of day-to-day firm activities and contribute to the overall success of the firm’s performance.
Responsibilities include:
Expertise in managing platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube, etc.
Creation and development of digital content (videos, articles and press releases) and audience specific materials focused on increasing engagement for various channels.
Write, edit and manage content for the website and social media of the Firm (e.g. news items, newsletters).
Coordinating and executing the submission of legal directories.
Undertake client and business development initiatives and projects of the Firm.
Create social media posts other branding materials (firm profile, brochures, presentations etc.).
Work and collaborate closely with other departments.
Identifying the latest trends and developments and provide key insights and suggestions.
Making suggestions and proposals to Senior Management for the improvement of the Department.
ABOUT YOU
You will need the following:
University degree in Marketing, Communication, Business or Digital Marketing, or in any other relevant field.
Previous experience in similar position.
Design and video editing skills.
Strong knowledge of business use of social media.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills in Greek and English.
Ability to multitask and perform effectively under pressure and tight deadlines.
Strong team orientation, ability to work collaboratively in a team setting.
Self-driven and forward-thinking individual with a genuine interest in the legal field.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office tools and excellent computer literacy.
Project management and organisational skills.
REMUNERATION
An attractive remuneration package will be offered to the successful candidate depending on experience and qualifications.
APPLICATION PROCESS
Interested candidates can send their CV.
We accept applications until December 12, 2026, but we may hire a suitable candidate before the lapse of this deadline.
Only applications that fulfill the above requested qualifications will be considered.
