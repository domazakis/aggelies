Περιγραφή
The Secret Hotels family continues to grow, extending its heritage and presence in Santorini.
Secret Hotels create exceptional experiences that go above and beyond guests’ expectations in luxury hospitality, combining unparalleled standards of excellence and elegance with an authentic connection to local traditions.
As part of our continued growth, Santorini Secret Hotels is seeking to recruit a:
Marketing Executive – Piraeus Head offices
We are looking for a dynamic, creative, and highly motivated marketing professional to join our team and contribute to the development and implementation of our brand and marketing strategy.
Key Responsibilities :
Develop and implement the annual marketing and communications plan.
Ensure that all external communications are accurate, brand-compliant, and aligned with the hotel’s positioning and brand identity.
Establish and maintain strong relationships with local, regional, and international media to maximize brand exposure.
Manage content creation, including photography, videography, and brand storytelling.
Oversee all digital marketing channels, including websites, SEO, SEM, email marketing, CRM, and social media.
Manage and maintain hotel media kits.
Develop, compile, and distribute weekly and monthly social media performance reports in collaboration with outsourced partners.
Build and maintain strong relationships with influencers, bloggers, and other relevant content creators.
Ensure clear and effective communication and collaboration with the Sales Department and Senior Management.
Coordinate photo selection, copy preparation, and follow-up for hotel brochures and promotional materials targeting travel agents, in line with Sales Department guidelines.
Create and manage newsletters in accordance with Sales Department guidelines.
Manage the hotels’ online reputation and monitor online reviews and guest feedback.
Audit and update hotel and OTA websites in collaboration with the E-commerce Manager.
Update and maintain hotel fact sheets, presentations, and other marketing materials.
Coordinate and manage social media content and blog posts.
Απαραίτητα Προσόντα
Position Requirements :
2+ years of marketing experience, preferably within luxury hospitality, premium lifestyle brands, and/or the hotel industry.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both Greek and English; additional languages will be considered an asset.
Highly energetic, proactive, creative, and team oriented.
Proven experience in website management and online advertising.
Strong knowledge of digital marketing, CRM, SEO/SEM, paid media, and analytics.
Demonstrated ability to develop and maintain long-term relationships with high-profile and strategic accounts, including international media, tour operators, airlines, and other industry partners.
Results-oriented, with strong organizational and project management skills.
Availability to travel when required is essential.
Excellent communication, interpersonal, and presentation skills.
Previous experience in the travel industry will be considered an advantage.
Experience in offline marketing will be considered an advantage.
Qualifications
Education :
Bachelor’s degree in marketing, Business Administration, Communications, or a related field.
A master’s degree will be considered an advantage.

Competitive salary based on qualifications and experience.
Permanent, all-year employment.
Position based in Piraeus.
A friendly and professional working environment.
Training and professional development opportunities.
Opportunities for career growth and advancement.
