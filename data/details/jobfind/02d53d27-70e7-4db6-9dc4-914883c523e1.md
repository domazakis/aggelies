About the Role:
We are looking for a creative, proactive
Content Creator & Social Media Specialist
to join our team. If you have an eye for design, enjoy shooting and editing short-form videos, and know how to engage audiences on social media, we would love to hear from you.
In this role, you will be the creative voice and visual storyteller of our brand—handling everything from raw footage and photo editing to drafting copy and publishing content across our channels.
Key Responsibilities:
Video & Photo Editing: Edit dynamic short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, Shorts) and format still photos to match our brand’s aesthetic and visual standards.
Graphic Design: Design engaging social media graphics, carousels, stories, promotional banners, and marketing collaterals using standard design tools.
Content Planning & Creation: Brainstorm creative concepts, assist in capturing content (behind-the-scenes, product/service highlights), and build an organized monthly content calendar.
Publishing & Channel Management: Schedule and publish daily/weekly posts across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube).
Copywriting: Write compelling captions, headlines, and calls-to-action (CTAs) that align with our brand tone.
Basic Marketing & Reporting: Track key performance metrics (views, engagement, reach), stay ahead of social media trends/audio tracks, and optimize content based on what resonates with our audience.
Requirements:
Hands-on experience with basic graphic design and video editing software (e.g., Canva, CapCut, Adobe Premiere Rush/Pro, Photoshop, or similar).
Strong understanding of social media platforms, algorithms, and trending formats (especially Instagram Reels & TikTok).
Creative mindset with attention to detail, typography, color balance, and pacing in video edits.
Good written and verbal communication skills in English (and Greek).
Ability to work independently, manage deadlines, and take initiative.
A portfolio or links to active accounts, past video edits, or design work is required.
Nice to Have:
Basic knowledge of digital marketing concepts (audience targeting, hashtags, paid social ads).
Basic photography or smartphone videography skills.
What we offer:
A friendly and supportive working environment
Private health insurance
Opportunities for professional growth and development
