Sandblu Resort is currently in search of:
Marketing Executive
Crafting the Future of Luxury Hospitality in Santorini, we are the team behind one of Greece's most exciting and rapidly growing luxury hospitality group. Our expanding collection includes the award-winning Sandblu Resort, the first LXR Hotels & Resorts property by Hilton in Greece, Blu Syllogi, a carefully curated handpicked fashion collection, the iconic Blu Restaurant overlooking Santorini's breathtaking Caldera, Inspire Luxury Villas, and the upcoming luxury developments Heliotopos Luxury Caldera Suites & Restaurant, and a new beachfront destination.
At Sandblu, we are redefining ultra-luxury hospitality through excellence, innovation, and genuine Greek hospitality. As one of the few LXR Hotels & Resorts in Europe, we are proud to introduce a new benchmark of luxury to Greece while creating exceptional destinations where unforgettable guest experiences begin with outstanding people.
Discover more: Sandblu Restaurant Gallery, Nectar Restaurant Gallery and Blu Restaurant Gallery or find us on social media at Sandblu, Nectar Restaurant and Blu Restaurant.
Job responsibilities:
Develop and execute the annual marketing plan aligned with commercial objectives.
Manage and oversee all digital marketing channels, including website, SEO, SEM, email marketing, CRM, and social media.
Coordinate brand campaigns, seasonal launches, and promotional activities.
Collaborate closely with Sales, Revenue, Reservations, Operations, and external agencies.
Manage PR initiatives, media relations, influencer partnerships, and brand collaborations.
Oversee content creation, photography, videography, and brand storytelling.
Monitor campaign performance, budgets, ROI, and analytics, providing actionable insights.
Ensure brand consistency across all online and offline communications.
Identify market trends, competitor activity, and emerging luxury hospitality opportunities.
Support the Director in developing long-term brand positioning and marketing strategy.
Απαραίτητα Προσόντα
Job requirements:
Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Communications, or a related field (Master's degree is a plus).
5+ years of marketing experience, preferably in luxury hospitality or premium lifestyle brands.
Proven experience managing integrated marketing campaigns.
Strong knowledge of digital marketing, CRM, SEO/SEM, paid media, and analytics.
Experience working with creative agencies, PR agencies, and content production teams.
Excellent project management and organizational skills.
Strong analytical mindset with a commercial approach.
Excellent communication and presentation skills.
Fluency in English (written and spoken); additional languages are considered an asset.
Experience with hospitality systems and marketing platforms is a plus.
