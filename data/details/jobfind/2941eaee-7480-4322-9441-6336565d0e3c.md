Eurotraining, a leading educational organization, is seeking experienced and motivated team members to work in the field of European-funded projects. The roles focus on three key specific areas:
Implementation
of approved projects, ensuring timelines are met, actions are coordinated, and deliverables are completed.
Porposal writing
for new projects under programmes such as Horizon Europe, Erasmus+, Interreg, etc.
PR Networking
and partnership building at an European level, through participation in events and conferences.
Responsibilities by role
:
EU Project Manager
:
Managing day-to-day project activities and liaising with partners
EU Proposal Writer
:
Drafting and preparing proposals in line with program requirements
EU PR Networking Officer
:
Organising and participating in networking activities such as conferences and events.
Monitoring developments in EU funding and identifying new opportunities
Required Qualifications
:
Bachelor or Master's degree in a relevant field, such as International Relations, Business Economics or Public Policy
Proven experienece in proposal writing and/or implementation of European projects
Good knowledge and understanding of EU procedures, project design and management methodologies
Benefits
:
Competitive remuneration package
Opportunity for professional developement in a dynamic and constantly evolving environment
Pleasant and modern working environment, which encourages team spirit, cooperation and continuous learning
