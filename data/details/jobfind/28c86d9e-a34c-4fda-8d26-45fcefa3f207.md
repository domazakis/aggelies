Περιγραφή
PRIMARY
is a leading scheduling software company operating internationally since 2008 with offices in
Athens
and
Milano
. We design & develop Planning & Scheduling Software using state-of-the-art technology. Our clientele consists of more than 100 large-scale companies in Retail, Services, Hospitality, and Manufacturing in several countries around the world.
PRIMARY
is looking for a
Project Management Executive
to join our operations team in
Athens
and contribute to the adoption of our scheduling systems by our clients.
You will…
Provide Project Management Services
Analyze customer needs
Perform system configuration according to needs
Deliver end-user training
Setup staging environments
Contribute to technical documentation
Collaborate with our sales & software development departments
Απαραίτητα Προσόντα
You must have…
A University Degree in Mathematics, Physics, Information Technology or Engineering
Strong communication skills
Excellent command of the English language (C2 Level)
Good command of MS Office Tools (Word, Excel, Powerpoint)
An aftersales background
Document writing skills
You may have (one or more…)
Previous experience in a software firm
Knowledge of the Italian language
Παροχές
Our Offer:
A competitive remuneration package
Opportunity to gain valuable experience from working with large-scale clients
Collaboration with highly qualified professionals
Additional benefits based on Level & Performance
Career development opportunities
Comfortable workplace
Private Health Insurance Program
If you are a results-oriented person with good communication skills, willing to become a member of a strong team of qualified professionals, please send us your CV.
