Job requirements:
Minimum 2 years of experience in Night Audit, Front Office, or a similar role, preferably within a luxury hotel or boutique hospitality environment.
Strong knowledge of Opera PMS and relevant F&B systems such as MICROS / RVC.
Previous experience with cashier operations, daily reconciliations, financial controls, and resolving discrepancies.
Good knowledge of End-of-Day procedures, night audit processes, room charges, billing, and departmental closures.
Strong Excel and reporting skills, with excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
Ability to work independently during night shifts, prioritize tasks, and remain calm when handling unexpected situations.
High level of confidentiality, integrity, and responsibility when handling financial and guest information.
Fluent English required, Greek preferred, with additional languages considered an asset.
Professional appearance and a strong understanding of luxury hospitality standards.
Job responsibilities:
Conduct the nightly Front Office and financial audit, ensuring all transactions, postings, and departmental revenues are accurate and properly recorded.
Review the Logbook and Guest Feedback and ensure all relevant follow-ups, wake-up calls, and breakfast boxes for early departures are arranged.
Open and verify the cashier, check pending arrivals and traces, and ensure all outstanding items are properly addressed.
Review room rates, billing instructions, credit limits, and next day reservations to identify and resolve discrepancies.
Ensure all departments submit their daily reports and required supporting documentation before the End-of-Day process is completed.
Run, review, and distribute required Opera and financial reports to the appropriate recipients.
Verify departmental closures, signatures, supporting documents, and daily revenue reports, following up on any discrepancies.
Prepare the next day's departures and ensure all relevant information, billing instructions, and guest requirements are updated.
Maintain accurate records and update the Logbook with outstanding matters, incidents, and priorities for the morning shift.
Respond to overnight guest requests and assist with Front Office operations when required, ensuring a professional and discreet service at all times.
Communicate effectively with the Front Office, F&B, Housekeeping, and management teams to ensure operational continuity.
Ensure a clear and comprehensive handover to the morning team, highlighting any unresolved issues or required follow ups.
Maintain strict confidentiality regarding guest information, financial data, and hotel operations
