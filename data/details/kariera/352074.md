Job requirements:
Minimum 3-5 years of experience in professional kitchens, with at least 1-2 years in a Chef de Partie position.
Previous experience in luxury hotels, resorts, fine-dining, or high-end restaurants is highly desirable.
Strong culinary and technical skills within the assigned kitchen section.
Good knowledge of Mediterranean and contemporary cuisine; knowledge of Greek and local Santorini products is an advantage.
Good understanding of HACCP, food safety, hygiene, allergen management, and kitchen safety standards.
Ability to consistently deliver high standards of quality, presentation, taste, and timing.
Ability to work efficiently under pressure during busy service periods.
Strong teamwork, communication, organization, and time-management skills.
Professional attitude, attention to detail, reliability, and willingness to learn and develop.
Previous pre-opening or luxury hospitality experience is an advantage.
HACCP certification and good English communication skills are preferred.
Job responsibilities:
Take full responsibility for the daily operation of the assigned kitchen section.
Prepare, cook, and present dishes according to approved recipes, specifications, and presentation standards.
Ensure all mise en place and preparation is completed on time and to the required quality.
Maintain consistent standards of taste, portioning, presentation, and food quality throughout service.
Monitor the freshness, quality, labeling, storage, and correct handling of all ingredients.
Follow all HACCP, food safety, hygiene, and sanitation procedures at all times.
Assist the Sous Chef with daily preparation, ordering, stock control, and inventory.
Control portion sizes and help minimize food waste and unnecessary costs.
Support and guide Demi Chefs de Partie and Commis Chefs, helping to maintain smooth kitchen operations.
Maintain excellent cleanliness, organization, and discipline within the assigned section.
Ensure the correct and safe use of kitchen equipment and tools.
Communicate effectively with the Sous Chef and other kitchen sections during service.
Assist with menu preparation, new dishes, seasonal products, and special events when required.
Support the culinary team during the pre-opening and launch of the new restaurant.
Contribute to a positive team environment based on teamwork, professionalism, respect, and authentic hospitality.
