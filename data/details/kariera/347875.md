The Role:
We’re looking for a passionate and execution-focused Product Manager Crypto to support the delivery of our new crypto trading platform. This role sits at the heart of our crypto ecosystem, spanning wallets, custody, funding, transaction lifecycles and trading infrastructure.
This is a hands-on role focused on turning the product strategy of some of the most critical building blocks of our crypto platform into execution. From wallets and custody to transaction processing and crypto spot trading you’ll work on the products and systems that securely move customer assets, power seamless trading experiences and ensure our platform is resilient, scalable and operationally ready as we continue to expand our digital asset offering.
Success in this role requires a genuine passion for crypto and an interest in how digital asset platforms operate behind the scenes. The ideal candidate will have strong analytical and systems-thinking capabilities and a deep interest in designing best in class user experiences.
The key responsibilities of the position include:
Support the execution of the crypto product roadmap by translating product strategy into detailed product requirements, user stories, and functional specifications
Collaborate cross-functionally throughout the product development lifecycle to ensure high-quality, timely delivery of product initiatives
Define and document end-to-end product flows across wallets, crypto funding, trading, and transaction lifecycle management
Support integrations with custody providers and other crypto infrastructure partners to deliver secure and scalable platform capabilities
Define operational workflows, edge cases and failure scenarios for key platform capabilities, including deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and settlement
Coordinate the launch of new offerings, ensuring go-live readiness through internal approvals and controls, operational runbooks, product validation, pilot client phasing, and marketing through to final launch
Collaborate with Design, Customer Support, and Operations to continuously improve user journeys, product quality, and operational efficiency
Balance customer needs, technical constraints, and regulatory requirements when defining product solutions
Initiate product adjustments as necessary to ensure that the product continues to meet business goals
Main requirements:
At least 3–5 years of Product Management experience, preferably with 1-3 years of experience in crypto, fintech, trading, payments, or other financial technology environments
Experience delivering customer-facing digital products through the full product lifecycle
Experience working within Agile product development teams and collaborating closely with engineering teams
Strong understanding of crypto platform fundamentals, including:
Crypto spot trading and the differences between spot trading and leveraged/CFD products like Blockchain transaction lifecycles and confirmations/ Crypto deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and settlement/ Crypto wallet infrastructure and custody concepts
Strong analytical and systems-thinking skills, with the ability to break down complex product challenges into scalable solutions
Experience writing clear product requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and functional specifications
Comfortable defining product flows, operational scenarios, and system behaviours
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across cross-functional teams
Ability to manage multiple initiatives within a fast-paced and evolving environment
Comfortable leveraging AI tools to accelerate product discovery, documentation, analysis, and execution
The following will be considered an advantage:
Experience working with crypto custody providers or wallet infrastructure platforms
Familiarity with blockchain analytics, AML/KYT processes and Travel Rule requirements
Experience working on crypto exchanges, brokerages, trading platforms, or payment products
Exposure to reconciliation, settlement, treasury, or operational workflows within financial systems
Experience working with APIs and third-party platform integrations
Personal involvement in crypto through trading, wallets, DeFi, or blockchain projects
Benefit from:
Attractive remuneration package
Private health insurance
Corporate pension fund
Intellectually stimulating work environment
Continuous personal development and international training opportunities
Type of employment: Full time
