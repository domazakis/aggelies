Job requirements:
Minimum 2-3 years of proven experience in bartending, preferably within a luxury hotel, fine dining restaurant, upscale bar, resort, or high end hospitality environment.
Good knowledge of classic and contemporary cocktails, spirits, wines, beers, and non-alcoholic beverages.
Knowledge of cocktail preparation, garnishing, presentation techniques, and beverage service standards.
Previous experience in a luxury hospitality or high volume environment will be considered an advantage.
Passion for hospitality and a genuine interest in creating memorable guest experiences.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a warm, polished, and guest-oriented approach.
Good organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to remain composed during busy service periods.
Confident and professional approach to suggestive selling and beverage recommendations.
Fluency in English is required; additional languages are considered an asset.
Basic understanding of beverage stock, product handling, storage, and waste control.
Positive attitude, professional appearance, and willingness to learn and develop within a luxury hospitality environment.
Ability to work in a fast paced environment while consistently maintaining service and quality standards.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking and navigating stairs between the bar, service areas, and different levels of the property.
Strong understanding of hygiene, cleanliness, and responsible alcohol service standards.
Job responsibilities:
Provide professional, warm, and attentive bar service to all guests, ensuring consistently high quality experience.
Prepare and serve cocktails, wines, spirits, beers, soft drinks, coffees, and other beverages according to established recipes and service standards.
Ensure consistency in taste, quality, presentation, and portioning of all beverages.
Maintain excellent knowledge of the beverage menu, cocktails, spirits, wines, ingredients, and available products.
Engage with guests in a professional and friendly manner, anticipating their needs and responding to individual preferences and requests.
Provide informed beverage recommendations and support suggestive selling to enhance the guest experience and beverage sales.
Ensure all orders are prepared accurately and served in a timely and professional manner.
Maintain the bar, workstations, glassware, equipment, and service areas to impeccable standards throughout the shift.
Assist with opening, service, and closing procedures according to company standards.
Assist with stock replenishment, inventory counts, and proper storage of beverages and bar supplies.
Support effective beverage cost control by minimizing waste and using products, equipment, and supplies responsibly.
Communicate effectively with the Head Bartender, Restaurant Manager, Floor Manager, Head Waiter, Kitchen, and wider F&B team to ensure smooth service.
Support the Head Bartender and wider bar team during busy service periods and assist colleagues whenever required.
Maintain a clean, organized, and safe working environment at all times.
Handle guest feedback and service requests with professionalism, empathy, and discretion, involving the Head Bartender or management when appropriate.
Ensure compliance with responsible alcohol service, health, safety, hygiene, and company policies at all times.
Contribute to a positive, collaborative, and professional team culture that reflects the values and standards of the property and the group.
Participate in training and development activities and continuously develop knowledge of cocktails, spirits, wines, service techniques, and current beverage trends.
