Rooms Division:
Night Auditor
Locations:
Greece Corfu, Kos, Halkidiki, Crete
Duration:
7 months, between March-November
​At Sani/Ikos Group, the
Front Office department
plays a pivotal role in guest experiences. Our
Front Office Team
orchestrates unforgettable holiday experiences with seamless check-ins, personalized recommendations, and world-class service.
Whether as a Front Office and Guest Relations Agent or an e-Concierge team member, guest interaction is at the heart of what we do.
Responsibilities:
Front Desk Operations:
Manage check-ins, check-outs, and guest inquiries during the night shift, ensuring smooth service.
Audit & Reporting:
Reconcile daily financial transactions, prepare night audit reports, and ensure accurate posting of all accounts.
Guest Support:
Respond to guest requests and issues promptly, maintaining high service standards.
Safety & Security:
Monitor hotel premises during night hours, ensuring the safety of guests and employees.
Collaboration:
Coordinate with other departments for room status updates, special requests, and any overnight operational needs.
About you
You have proven experience in front office, accounting, or auditing within the hospitality industry. You possess strong numerical, analytical, and reporting skills, along with proficiency in hotel management systems and MS Office. You demonstrate excellent communication and problem-solving abilities, and you are able to work independently, responsibly, and overnight shifts.
