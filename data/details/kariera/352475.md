What You Will Do
Support the Restaurant Manager in overseeing daily service, ensuring seamless operations and consistently elevated guest experiences.
Supervise service briefings, floor assignments and service execution across all meal periods.
Coach team members on refined service etiquette, product knowledge and personalized guest recognition.
Resolve guest requests and concerns discreetly, taking ownership of timely follow-up.
Coordinate with Culinary, Bar and other departments to deliver accurate, well-paced service.
Assist with rosters, inventory, cost controls and operational reporting.
Maintain hygiene, safety and brand standards, supporting pre-opening procedures and training.
