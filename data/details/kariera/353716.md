Hemmersbach
provides IT infrastructure services in more than 190 countries with 50 own subsidiaries. We deliver exclusively for the leading companies in the IT industry. We go the extra mile – we not only simply enthuse our customers, but also make the world a better place: 20% of our profits go into our Direct Actions Hemmersbach Rhino Force and Hemmersbach Kids’ Family. That’s why Hemmersbach is The Social Purpose IT Company.
Role:
Maintain and regularly update vendor databases and company records.
Collect, compare, and input vendor pricing and product information into internal systems.
Research and source new vendors to support business development and expand the company’s supplier network.
Communicate with existing and potential vendors to gather information and clarify requirements.
Review order specifications to ensure accuracy and compliance with company standards.
Enter and organize data accurately while keeping records up to date
Requirements:
min. 1 year experience (or equivalent) in purchasing/procurement
B2 English
Basic knowledge of Microsoft Excel
Strong attention to detail and accuracy
Good organizational and time-management skills
Good communication and interpersonal skills

Kids-family.org: taking care of children without parents in Poland and India, turning their shelters into homes.
