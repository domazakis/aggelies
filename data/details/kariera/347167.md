Αποθήκευση
With a leading presence in the sector of small domestic appliances and homeware,
Benrubi
is passionate about offering products that stand out for their exceptional quality, innovation and design.
Benrubi is a member of
Quest Group
, one of the leading business groups in Greece, offering our people the opportunity to grow within a strong and diverse business environment.
If you would like to become a member of the Marketing Department of the top company in the field, and you are passionate about your job, this is a position for you.
Your Role:
Participate in the development of new products
Daily communication with foreign suppliers for developing and expanding the product range
Effectively handle sales forecast, stock levels, and purchases
Managing the product range
Generate and reporting sales analysis
Working closely with the Supply chain department to ensure timely importing and product launching
Assist with the execution of marketing plans/campaigns and coordination of all related activities.
Create Marketing, Product Briefs and Presentations.
Execute all Marketing activities under the Marketing Director
Your Skills:
Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Economics or Business. A Master’s degree will be considered an asset.
4 years minimum of previous experience in similar positions
Fluent in written and spoken English
Ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment
Outstanding writing, verbal communication and presentation skills
Excellent computer skills, including Excel, Powerpoint and ERP systems
What we offer:
A modern and friendly work environment
Competitive remuneration package
Opportunities for career growth
Private insurance
