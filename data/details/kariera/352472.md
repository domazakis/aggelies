Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What You Will Do:
Supervise daily restaurant service to ensure smooth operations and exceptional guest care.
Guide waitstaff in refined table service, menu knowledge and service recovery.
Welcome guests, anticipate preferences and respond promptly to requests or concerns.
Coordinate orders and service timing with the kitchen and bar teams.
Monitor mise en place, cleanliness, stock levels and adherence to procedures.
Support training and provide constructive feedback to the service team.
What You Need to Bring:
Previous supervisory experience in an upscale restaurant, preferably within a luxury hotel or fine dining setting.
Strong knowledge of table service, food and beverage products and service sequence.
Excellent English communication skills; additional languages are an advantage.
Ability to lead by example and coach team members during busy service periods.
Guest-focused approach with tact, discretion and confident complaint handling.
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail and teamwork.
