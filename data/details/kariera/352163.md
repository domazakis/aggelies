Job requirements:
Minimum 2-3 years of previous experience as a Hostess, Receptionist, Guest Relations Agent, or in a similar customer facing hospitality role.
Previous experience within a luxury hotel, fine dining restaurant, or high end hospitality environment is considered an advantage.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a warm, polished, and guest oriented approach.
Confident, courteous, and professional manner with a genuine passion for creating exceptional first impressions.
Strong organizational skills and the ability to multitask while remaining calm and composed under pressure.
Ability to manage reservations, guest arrivals, seating arrangements, and waiting times efficiently.
Strong attention to detail and the ability to anticipate guest needs and special requests.
Ability to communicate and collaborate effectively with the F&B team, Kitchen, and other departments.
Fluency in English is required, additional languages are considered an asset.
Well groomed, professional appearance and a strong understanding of luxury hospitality standards.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking and navigating stairs between the entrance, host stand, dining areas, and different levels of the property.
Job responsibilities:
Welcome guests in a warm, friendly, professional, and courteous manner, creating an exceptional first impression.
Manage reservations, table allocations, walk in guests, and guest flow efficiently throughout the service.
Guide guests to their tables and present menus with a polished and attentive approach.
Coordinate table assignments closely with the service team to optimize seating, service flow, and overall guest experience.
Monitor dining room activity, reservations, table status, and waiting times to ensure smooth operations.
Handle guest inquiries, special requests, preferences, and occasions with care, discretion, and professionalism.
Maintain a thorough awareness of VIP guests, special occasions, and specific guest requirements.
Communicate effectively with Waiters, Head Waiters, Assistant Waiters, Kitchen, and management to ensure seamless coordination.
Maintain an impeccable, clean, organized, and welcoming host stand and entrance area at all times.
Navigate the property's unique Caldera layout efficiently and safely, coordinating guest arrivals and seating across different levels and dining areas when required.
Assist in maintaining smooth guest flow during peak service periods and manage waiting guests with professionalism and courtesy.
Maintain strong knowledge of the restaurant's menu, concept, operating hours, reservations, and key guest information.
Ensure all interactions and procedures are consistent with the property's luxury service standards and brand guidelines.
Handle guest concerns with empathy and discretion, escalating matters to the Restaurant Floor Manager or Captain when appropriate.
Adhere to all health, safety, hygiene, and company standards at all times.
Perform opening, daily, and closing duties related to the host area and guest arrival experience.
