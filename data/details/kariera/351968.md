APHI AURA located in Kolymbia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the
Sous Chef.
POSITION SUMMARY:
The Sous Chef will assist with menu planning and development, kitchen organization, cost control, inventory management, and staff training, while ensuring smooth and efficient operations across all culinary outlets. This role requires strong leadership, creativity, attention to detail, and the ability to perform effectively in a fast-paced, luxury hotel environment.
Responsibilities:
Preparation and control of food for buffet and A La Carte
Ensure the culinary section adheres to all safety, health & quality regulations
Delegation of work to cooks
Adhere to standardized recipes and portion sizes.
Adjust recipes as needed to meet dietary restrictions or special requests.
Assist in inventory management, including monitoring stock levels and restocking as needed.
Stay updated on culinary techniques and industry trends through continuous learning.
Coordinate timing with other cooks and kitchen staff to ensure timely delivery of all menu items.
Requirements:
Minimum of 3 years of experience in a similar position
Certificate in culinary arts
Strong sense of culinary creativity with attention to detail
Ability to multitask in a fast-paced and dynamic environment
In-depth knowledge of sanitation principles and food preparation standards
Familiarity with a range of cooking and other kitchen equipment
Excellent communication skills and an enthusiasm for teamwork
Non-EU applicants must possess a valid and active residence permit with the legal right to work in Greece at the time of application and throughout the employment period.
Benefits:
Salary package and other benefits (as per local legislation)
Advanced opportunities for professional growth
Accommodation (if necessary) and board
Participation in educational programs & trainings
Diverse and multicultural environments
Professional skills development
