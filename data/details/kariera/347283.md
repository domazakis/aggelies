ABOUT US
LPP S.A. is one of the largest fashion companies in Central and Eastern Europe. The company has been operating steadily in Poland and abroad for more than 30 years, successfully meeting market demands in the field of clothing.
LPP S.A. manages 5 fashion brands:
Reserved, Cropp, House, Mohito,
and
Sinsay,
each of which addresses a different audience.
The company lists over 3,700 stores in its network and additionally has online stores for each of its Brands. It has a presence in 41 countries.
We are ambitious, which means that we are constantly looking for new challenges and striving to improve.
Our biggest strength is our team, which today consists of more than 63,000 talented people in its offices and stores. Their passion motivates us to still have the energy and courage to reach for more.
ROLE OVERVIEW:
We are looking for a
Loss Prevention Coordinator
to protect the company’s inventory, money, assets, employees, and customers, by identifying risks, strengthening controls and coordinating Loss Prevention activities across the assigned region.
MAIN ACTIVITIES:
Coordinate the Loss Prevention strategy and action plans for the assigned region.
Visit stores regularly to assess risks, conduct audits, and monitor compliance with Loss Prevention procedures.
Identify and address risks related to theft, fraud, inventory loss, safety, and operational risks.
Investigate incidents using all available security systems and resources.
Monitor security systems, including CCTV and alarm systems, and ensure proper functioning.
Analyze loss data, identify trends, and recommend preventive actions.
Deliver Loss Prevention training and support the implementation of relevant policies and procedures.
Follow up on audit findings and corrective actions and prepare relevant reports.
Develop Loss Prevention Action Plans for stores requiring targeted intervention.
Cooperate with the central Loss Prevention team, other departments, and authorities when required.
Requirements
Previous experience in Loss Prevention, Security, Internal Audit, or Risk Management, preferably in retail.
Previous experience as a Store Manager will be an advantage.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify risks and develop practical solutions.
Flexibility, Adaptability & Team Spirit.
Very good organizational and communication skills.
Strong interpersonal and training skills.
Proactive team player with strong work ethic, committed to continuous self-improvement
Availability for frequent business travel.
Valid Category B driving licence.
Excellent knowledge of the English language (oral & written communication).
Excellent PC knowledge (Ms Office) & experience using applications.
