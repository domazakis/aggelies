About Us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new international ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality
is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
The Role
We are looking for a meticulous, guest-focused, and systems-savvy
Reservations Manager
to join the pre-opening team of our ultra-luxury resort. This is a rare opportunity to build the hotel's reservations function from the ground up, setting the standards, systems, and service culture that will shape every guest's first impression of the brand.
Reporting to the Cluster Revenue Director, the Reservations Manager will be responsible for the setup, execution, and ongoing management of the hotel's reservations operations, working closely with the commercial, revenue, front office, and operations teams, as well as key distribution and technology partners.
What you will do
Oversee the reservations team in Mykonos on-site and Athens during winter, ensuring seamless guest booking experiences
Lead the setup and configuration of the PMS (Opera), CRS (SynXis), and GDS systems, including rate codes, market segments, packages, and source of business structures — working closely with the Revenue team
Develop and maintain SOPs for reservations processes and guest communication
Manage day-to-day reservations operations once live: individual and group bookings, rate loading, availability control, and guest communication
Ensure accuracy and consistency of rates, packages, and inventory across all distribution channels (direct, GDS, OTA, wholesale, trade)
Collaborate with Sales, Revenue, and Operations to optimize room inventory and pricing
Identify and drive cross-selling and upselling opportunities across spa, F&B, and experiences to grow ancillary revenue
Act as the guardian of guest data integrity within the PMS/CRS, supporting CRM and personalization efforts
Coordinate with Front Office and Guest Experience teams to ensure smooth handover of pre-arrival details, preferences, and special requests
Handle VIP, high-touch, and complex bookings (multi-room, villa, long-stay, bespoke packages) with a white-glove approach
Support Luxury consortia, affiliations, and other luxury network reservation protocols and reporting requirements
Ensure all guest interactions reflect the resort's luxury standards and personalized service
Monitor booking trends, denial/regret tracking, and system performance; prepare forecasts and contribute to strategic planning
Support pre-opening activities including system setup, rate loading, testing, and training
Train and onboard reservations agents ahead of opening, embedding brand voice and service standards into every guest interaction
Liaise with IT and system vendors on integration, testing, and troubleshooting
What you need to bring
Minimum 3 years of experience in a reservation’s leadership role, ideally in a luxury resort
Experience in hotel pre-opening/opening is a plus
Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Hotel Management, Tourism, or Economic Studies
Proficient in Microsoft Office and hotel systems (PMS, SynXis, Extranets, GDS)
Availability to travel/stay within Greece during opening season
Excellent command of the English language
Excellent communication and guest service skills
Strong leadership and team management abilities
Commercial mindset with cross-selling and upselling capabilities
Detail-oriented and organized
Ability to collaborate across departments and drive results
Analytical thinking and reporting skills
