Job requirements
Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration, or a related field.
2–3 years of experience in marketing, digital marketing, communications, or a related role, preferably within luxury hospitality, tourism, or premium lifestyle brands.
Good understanding of digital marketing channels, including social media, email marketing, website content, and online advertising.
Experience supporting marketing campaigns and promotional activities.
Strong content coordination and copywriting skills, with excellent attention to detail.
Experience working with creative agencies, photographers, videographers, or other external partners is an advantage.
Strong organizational and project coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines.
Creative mindset with a strong interest in luxury hospitality, lifestyle, and brand storytelling.
Good analytical and reporting skills.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Fluency in English, both written and spoken; additional languages are considered an asset.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office and familiarity with digital marketing and social media tools.
Job responsibilities
Develop and execute the annual marketing plan aligned with commercial objectives.
Manage and oversee all digital marketing channels, including website, email marketing and social media.
Coordinate brand campaigns, seasonal launches, and promotional activities.
Collaborate closely with Sales, Revenue, Reservations, Operations, and external agencies.
Manage PR initiatives, media relations, influencer partnerships, and brand collaborations.
Oversee content creation, photography, videography, and brand storytelling.
Monitor campaign performance, budgets, ROI, and analytics, providing actionable insights.
Ensure brand consistency across all online and offline communications.
Identify market trends, competitor activity, and emerging luxury hospitality opportunities.
