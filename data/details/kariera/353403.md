About us:
Hemmersbach provides IT infrastructure services in more than 190 countries with 50 own subsidiaries. We deliver exclusively for the leading companies in the IT industry. We go the extra mile – we not only simply enthuse our customers, but also make the world a better place: 20% of our profits go into our Direct Actions Hemmersbach Rhino Force and Hemmersbach Kids’ Family. That’s why Hemmersbach is The Social Purpose IT Company.
Role:
Drive HR projects and initiatives across different teams and countries, working closely with HR and business functions
Support strategic topics such as retention, succession planning, skills development and workforce planning
Translate HR data, KPIs and trends into structural and organizational recommendations — not just reporting
Prepare reports and presentations to support business and HR decision-making
Develop and track key HR metrics and help identify areas for improvement
Build strong, trust-based relationships with HR teams and business leaders globally
Support and guide HR colleagues through complex challenges
Coordinate HR topics across countries and work with local and central HR teams to find smart, practical and cost-effective solutions
Requirements:
8+ years of experience in HR Business Partnering, Project Management or Consulting within an international, matrix organization
Track record designing or re-engineering HR operating models across a matrix
Navigating competing executive priorities
Exceptional communication and networking skills, coupled with a strategic mindset and analytical approach
High level of business acumen and assertiveness paired with a focus on strategic thinking and execution
Fluent in English (C1 level)
Structured & data-driven approach
Benefits:
Buddy program
International environment

Mobile working
Staff discounts
Unlimited employment contract
Private Life and Health Insurance
Joining Hemmersbach, you will support our commitment to social responsibility. We prioritize 20% of our profits into our company owned organizations such as:
Rhino-force.org: providing environmental protection for an endangered species
