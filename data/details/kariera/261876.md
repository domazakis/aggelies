Job's Purpose
We are looking for a motivated Loading Assets Management Associate to join our Supply Chain team.
In this role, you will actively support the department's daily operations, playing a key part in data analysis, monitoring accounting balances and participating in projects of processes improvement.
What you will focus on
Create, organize, and maintain both physical and digital filing systems.
Collect, process, and accurately record data related to the movement of fixed assets.
Monitor and reconcile accounting balances.
Generate and distribute reports to distribution centers to ensure transparency and operational alignment.
Utilize warehouse management systems (e.g., SAP, WMS) to track asset movements and inventory data.
Support product management throughout the design and execution phases of internal projects.
Track project progress and provide regular updates using operational metrics and reports.
Conduct weekly and monthly reviews of current processes to identify areas for improvement.
Act as a liaison between technical and non-technical teams, translating complex data into clear insights.
Who we are looking for
• Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Logistics, Business Administration, or relevant field.
• Minimum 1 year of experience in a supply chain or logistics environment.
• Verry good knowledge of Microsoft Office (especially Excel).
• Experience in ERP Systems, WMS will be considered as an asset.
• Very good knowledge of English language (both written and verbal)
