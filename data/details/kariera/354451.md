A leading healthcare services provider is looking for a Digital Marketing Specialist to join its team in Athens, reporting directly to the Marketing Director.
This is a hands-on role supporting the growth and performance of our digital presence across multiple brands. You will work across websites, digital content, SEO, GEO and online campaigns, collaborating with internal teams and external partners to improve user experience, maintain brand consistency and deliver measurable results.
Key Responsibilities
Website Management & Digital Presence
· Coordinate the maintenance and continuous improvement of websites and landing pages.
· Manage content updates through WordPress or similar content management systems, ensuring accuracy, consistency and usability.
· Collaborate with developers and external partners to improve website functionality and optimise user journeys.
· Support SEO and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) initiatives to improve visibility across search engines and AI-powered search experiences.
Digital Campaigns & Content
· Support the planning, execution and optimisation of digital campaigns, working with agencies on paid media activity.
· Coordinate email marketing campaigns, including audience segmentation, scheduling and performance monitoring.
· Use AI tools to support research, content development and optimisation, reviewing outputs for accuracy, relevance and brand alignment.
Social Media & Community Management
· Coordinate social media content calendars and publishing with external partners.
· Support content creation, ensuring consistency with brand guidelines and communication priorities.
· Coordinate community management and maintain Google Business Profiles across the network, ensuring accurate information, timely responses to enquiries and reviews, and internal follow-up on feedback.
Performance Monitoring & Reporting
· Monitor website, campaign and social media performance using GA4, Google Search Console and platform insights.
· Track relevant KPIs, including engagement, qualified enquiries, appointment requests and conversion rates where available.
· Analyse results and recommend improvements to campaign effectiveness, digital visibility and user experience.
· Monitor competitors, audience behaviour and emerging digital trends to identify opportunities for improvement.
Requirements
· 2–4 years of experience in digital marketing or a similar role.
· Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration or a related field.
· Experience managing website content through WordPress or a similar CMS.
· Good understanding of SEO principles and familiarity with GEO and AI-powered search.
· Experience coordinating digital campaigns, content production and external agencies.
· Working knowledge of GA4, Google Search Console and digital performance reporting.
· Familiarity with Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager and email marketing platforms.
· Ability to use AI tools effectively and critically.
· Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Greek and English, including copywriting and copy-editing.
Skills & Attributes
· Strong organizational and project coordination skills.
· Analytical thinking and the ability to turn data into actionable insights.
· Attention to detail and a strong sense of ownership.
· Creativity, curiosity and a proactive approach to problem-solving.
· Ability to prioritize, manage multiple stakeholders and meet deadlines.
· Strong communication skills and a collaborative, hands-on approach.
Experience in healthcare or another regulated sector, as well as relevant digital marketing certifications, will be considered an advantage.
