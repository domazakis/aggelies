Head of HR Systems & Analytics
15/10/2026
Job Description:
The challenge
As METLEN continues to grow internationally, having reliable HR systems is only part of the equation. The bigger challenge is connecting people, processes, and data across markets to give leaders the visibility they need to make better decisions.
We are strengthening our HR technology and analytics capabilities to meet that challenge.
Your mandate
Lead our HR Systems & Data function, shaping how we manage HR technology, govern workforce data, and turn information into business insights.
You will lead and develop a team, influence technology priorities, and build capabilities that support an increasingly complex international organization.
Who you'll work with
Work closely with HR leadership, HR Transformation, People Services, and IT, partnering with teams across the business and external technology providers to turn operational needs into effective solutions.
The outcomes you'll drive
Build and develop a strong HR Systems & Data team, with the expertise and capabilities to support our growing international operations.
Create a reliable, connected HR technology environment, making the most of SAP SuccessFactors and its integrations across HR processes.
Give business and HR leaders a clearer picture of their workforce through consistent global reporting, executive dashboards, and meaningful people analytics.
Strengthen confidence in workforce data through common standards, clear ownership, and effective governance across markets.
Simplify how HR operates, reducing manual work, improving processes, and making technology easier and more effective to use.
What you bring
10+ years of experience across HR systems, HR technology, people analytics, or data management, including team leadership.
Strong understanding of enterprise HRIS platforms, ideally SAP SuccessFactors, and experience with implementations, integrations, and enhancements.
Experience in people analytics, executive reporting, and data governance within a complex organization.
The ability to lead teams, influence stakeholders, and translate business needs into effective technology and data solutions.
A degree in Information Systems, HR, Business, Data Analytics, or a related field.

Preferential household electricity plan
