The ideal candidate will support the planning and execution of digital marketing activities, contribute to engaging content creation and help strengthen our online presence across different digital channels.
Responsibilities
Manage and create content for the company’s social media channels
Support the planning and execution of digital marketing campaigns
Monitor and report on digital campaign and social media performance
Support SEO, Google Ads and other online marketing activities
Create and coordinate email and newsletter campaigns
Support website content updates and digital projects through the company’s CMS
Prepare copy and content for digital and printed communication materials
Contribute to the creation of marketing materials, including digital assets and videos
Support the organization of PR, CSR and other marketing activities
Collaborate with internal teams and external partners on marketing initiatives
Stay up to date with digital marketing trends and best practices
Reports to Marketing & Communications Supervisor
Requirements
BSc in Marketing, Communication, Business Administration or a relevant field
1–2 years of professional experience in Digital Marketing or a similar role
Hands-on experience with social media management and content creation
Basic knowledge of Google Ads and Google Analytics
Knowledge of SEO and digital marketing tools
Familiarity with CMS platforms
Good knowledge of MS Office & Canva
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Very good command of English
Strong organizational and interpersonal skills
Creative mindset, attention to detail and willingness to learn
What we offer
Competitive remuneration package
Private medical insurance
Laptop & mobile phone
Opportunities for learning and professional development
A dynamic and collaborative working environment
The opportunity to be part of a leading international organization
