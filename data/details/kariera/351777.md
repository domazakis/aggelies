Room Attendant (Maid)
Season 2027 | Location Mykonos
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Clean and service guest rooms in accordance with the hotel’s luxury standards.
Make beds, change linens, clean bathrooms and replenish guest amenities and supplies.
Ensure each room is perfectly presented, clean and ready for the guest.
Identify and report maintenance issues, safety concerns or room deficiencies.
Handle lost and found items in accordance with hotel procedures.
Maintain housekeeping carts, pantries and storage areas clean, organized and fully stocked.
Respond to guest requests promptly, professionally and with genuine care.
Work closely with Housekeeping Supervisors and other departments to ensure timely room readiness.
Participate in daily briefings, training sessions and team activities.
Contribute to an exceptional guest experience through attention to detail, professionalism and high standards of service.
What you need to bring:
Previous experience as a Room Attendant or in a similar Housekeeping role, preferably in a luxury hotel.
Good knowledge of housekeeping procedures, cleaning techniques and hygiene standards.
Strong attention to detail and commitment to maintaining high standards of cleanliness.
Guest-focused mindset with a warm, discreet and professional approach.
Good communication and interpersonal skills.
Positive attitude, reliability and a genuine passion for delivering an exceptional guest experience.
