Job requirements:
Previous experience as a Head Waiter, Senior Waiter, or in a similar supervisory role within fine dining, luxury hospitality, or a high-end restaurant environment is required.
Strong knowledge of fine dining service standards, food and beverage offerings, table service, and luxury guest expectations.
Fluency in English is required; additional languages are highly valued.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a warm, polished, and professional approach to guests and colleagues.
Strong organizational, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to remain composed and professional under pressure.
A proactive and guest centric mindset, with the ability to anticipate guest needs and ensure personalized service.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking and navigating stairs between different levels, dining areas, and service points.
Professional appearance and a strong understanding of the standards expected within an ultra luxury dining environment.
Job responsibilities:
Ensure seamless, timely, and exceptional service throughout each shift.
Oversee the smooth operation of the assigned section and ensure that all service standards are consistently maintained.
Welcome and engage with guests in a warm, discreet, and professional manner, creating a memorable and personalized dining experience.
Anticipate guest preferences and needs and ensure they are addressed promptly and appropriately.
Coordinate closely with the Restaurant Floor Manager, Captain, Waiters, Assistant Waiters, Bartenders, Kitchen, and other departments to ensure seamless service.
Support the service team before and throughout each shift, ensuring everyone is informed of daily operations, reservations, special occasions, VIP guests, menu changes, and specific guest preferences.
Maintain a thorough knowledge of the menu, ingredients, preparation methods, wine and beverage offerings, and relevant service standards.
Assist guests with menu selections and provide knowledgeable recommendations when required.
Monitor table presentation, service timing, cleanliness, and overall guest satisfaction throughout the dining experience.
Efficiently coordinate service across the different levels and areas of the Caldera property, ensuring smooth communication and movement despite the property's unique layout and stair access.
Handle guest requests, feedback, and minor service issues with discretion and professionalism, escalating matters to the Restaurant Manager when appropriate.
Support in training and development of junior members of the service team and lead by example in maintaining company standards.
Ensure compliance with all health, safety, hygiene, and company policies at all times.
Assist with opening and closing procedures and contribute to the smooth day to day operation of the restaurant.
Perform any additional duties and responsibilities assigned by the Restaurant Floor Manager or management team.
