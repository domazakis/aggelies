Hotel - Manager
About the Role
At ZEUS Hotels & Resorts, we are looking for a dedicated and inspiring Hotel Manager to lead day-to-day hotel operations, deliver exceptional guest experiences, and support strong team performance.
You will be responsible for ensuring smooth operations, maintaining high service standards, and creating a positive and engaging environment for both guests and employees.
Working closely with the hotel departments, you will balance people management, operational efficiency, and commercial objectives, with a strong focus on memorable guest experiences.
Life Inside the Hotel Group
Every day brings new challenges and opportunities. From welcoming guests and overseeing daily operations to supporting your team and resolving unexpected situations, your role will be dynamic and engaging.
You will be the central point of coordination, ensuring consistency, quality, and attention to detail across all departments while fostering a positive and professional working environment.
What You'll Do
Lead, motivate, and support the hotel team, promoting a positive and high-performance culture.
Plan and implement operational strategies aligned with the hotel’s goals.
Oversee all operational departments, including Front Office, Food & Beverage, Housekeeping, and Maintenance.
Ensure excellent guest satisfaction through effective communication, feedback handling, and service recovery.
Monitor maintenance needs, repairs, and the general upkeep of the property.
Track budgets, financial performance, and key operational metrics to support profitability.
Collaborate with Sales and Marketing to increase occupancy, revenue, and guest loyalty.
Build and maintain strong relationships with guests, suppliers, and external partners.
Ensure compliance with health and safety standards, legal regulations, and internal policies.
Lead by example, demonstrating professionalism, integrity, and accountability.
Encourage continuous improvement, staff development, and innovative ideas.
Your Uniqueness is Welcome
We believe that successful Hotel Managers stand out not only for their organizational skills, but also for their leadership style, emotional intelligence, and ability to inspire others.
If you enjoy motivating teams, solving problems, and creating outstanding guest experiences, this role will be a great fit for you.
We're looking for someone who:
Holds a degree in Hospitality Management, Business Administration, or a related field (postgraduate studies are a plus).
Has previous experience in a supervisory or management role within the hospitality industry.
Demonstrates strong leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
Has a solid understanding of hospitality operations and service standards.
Is confident in decision-making and handling multiple priorities.
Speaks English fluently (additional languages are an advantage).
Has experience with hotel management systems (knowledge of a Property Management System is a plus).
What We Offer
Competitive remuneration package.
