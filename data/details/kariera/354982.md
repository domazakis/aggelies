About us
ORA Hospitality is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Oversee the daily operations of the hotel’s wellness facilities, including spa, fitness, and wellness programs, ensuring exceptional guest experiences.
Lead, train, and motivate the wellness team, including therapists, pool and spa attendants, gym and kids club team, maintaining high performance and luxury service standards.
Develop and implement wellness programs, treatments, and experiences tailored to the needs of the hotel’s clientele.
Monitor facility operations, hygiene, and safety standards, ensuring compliance with brand policies and local regulations.
Coordinate with Front Office, Guest Relations, and F&B to enhance wellness offerings and guest satisfaction.
Manage inventory, equipment, and supplies, ensuring cost efficiency and quality standards.
Handle guest feedback and service recovery promptly, maintaining the highest level of professionalism.
Prepare operational reports, analyse performance metrics, and propose improvements to enhance guest experiences.
What you need to bring:
Minimum 5 years of experience managing wellness or spa operations within a 5-star or luxury hotel environment.
Excellent command of the English language; additional languages are a plus.
Strong leadership, team management, and motivational skills.
In-depth knowledge of spa treatments, wellness programs, fitness, and guest service standards.
Exceptional organizational, communication, and problem-solving abilities.
Ability to create a luxurious and personalized experience for every guest.
Familiarity with wellness management software, inventory control, and safety protocols.
