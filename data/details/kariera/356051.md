About the role
DMA Academy is an Australian digital marketing training business. We’re looking for an experienced Social Media Manager to take ownership of our Instagram and Facebook accounts and maintain a consistent, engaging brand presence.
We want someone who understands the brands they work with, takes initiative and can independently manage our day-to-day social media activity. This role involves more than scheduling posts: you should understand how content, design and audience engagement work together to strengthen a brand.
Your responsibilities
Manage our Instagram and Facebook profiles, keeping content and profile information current and on-brand.
Plan and maintain a content calendar, including scheduling and publishing posts.
Create and publish Instagram Stories that keep our audience engaged.
Design branded graphics using Canva, Photoshop or similar tools.
Write clear, engaging captions that reflect our brand voice.
Maintain visual consistency across posts, Stories and profile elements.
Monitor comments and messages and engage professionally with our audience.
Review content performance and suggest improvements to reach and engagement.
Work with our team to turn supplied footage, student stories and program updates into social content.
What we’re looking for
Demonstrated experience managing social media accounts for other businesses or brands.
A strong portfolio showing the profiles you’ve managed and the content you’ve created.
A deep understanding of branding, visual identity, tone of voice and social media profile management.
Basic graphic design skills using Canva or Photoshop.
Strong written English, as our content is aimed at an Australian audience.
Strong organisation and attention to detail, with the ability to manage your workload independently.
A genuine interest in the brands you represent and a proactive approach to audience engagement.
Availability to commit four hours per day to the role.
How To Apply
Please include:
A brief introduction and overview of your relevant experience.
Links to Instagram and Facebook accounts you have managed, explaining your responsibilities for each.
Examples of posts, Instagram Stories and graphics you have created.
Any relevant results or improvements you achieved, such as stronger engagement or audience growth.
We’re particularly interested in seeing practical examples of your work and understanding your personal contribution to each brand.
