Αποθήκευση
This role is designed to strengthen our Quality Assurance Team and actively support the company’s Continuous Improvement Plan. Based at our Production Plant in Schimatari, Greece, the QA Compliance Specialist supports product quality and regulatory compliance through the management of quality and regulatory documentation, batch record review activities, product dossier compliance, audit support, and quality systems.
The role focuses on ensuring alignment with GMP, GDP, registered dossiers, and applicable regulatory requirements, while supporting new product implementation, change control, CAPA, risk assessments, validation activities, Continued Process Verification (CPV), Product Quality Reviews (PQRs), and continuous improvement initiatives. The position works cross-functionally with QA Systems, QA Operations, QC, customers, suppliers, and external service providers.
Role and Responsibilities
Maintains up-to-date product dossiers and supports applications for new product licenses, formulations, and market introductions, including provision of relevant documentation to health authorities and customers.
Ensures compliance with regulatory requirements for product approvals and manages changes with regulatory or dossier impact, including CEP updates, analytical method validations, and dossier modifications.
Reviews analytical methods and QC analysis practices against the registered dossier to ensure alignment and identify potential discrepancies.
Prepares risk assessments for nitrosamines, and other quality-related projects in accordance with applicable guidelines, regulatory expectations, and internal requirements.
Supports customer and regulatory audits, including audit preparation, responses to findings, and completion of assigned actions, also participates in internal audits and supplier/manufacturer audits.
Conducts induction and refresher training on assigned procedures.
Works with QA Systems and QA Operations Supervisors to harmonize and implement quality policies, manuals, and procedures, ensuring alignment with current practices.
Contributes to the review of analytical method validation protocols and reports and participates in the preparation of validation protocols and reports assigned to the position.
Participates in the preparation of Manufacturing Instructions for Feasibility and NPI batches and supports feasibility and validation batches for process optimization.
Participates in the preparation and/or review of Quality Technical Agreements (QTAs) with suppliers, customers, and external service providers, as assigned by the QA Manager.
Oversee the tracking and resolution of corrective and preventive actions (CAPAs) assigned to the position.
Handles change controls related to new products or regulatory requirements and leads assigned new product projects, coordinating cross-functional activities for timely and compliant implementation.
Leads and manages Continued Process Verification (CPV) activities, including monitoring of Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs) and Critical Process Parameters (CPPs) to identify trends and mitigate risks.
Collaborates on the preparation and review of Product Quality Reviews (PQRs) assigned by the QA Manager, ensuring accurate and comprehensive documentation in accordance with regulatory and internal standards.
Monitors changes in applicable pharmaceutical legislation, regulatory requirements, and relevant guidelines, and communicates applicable updates to the responsible departments and personnel, supporting the timely implementation of necessary actions to ensure that procedures, systems, and facilities remain aligned with current regulatory and GMP requirements.
Participates in batch record review activities, ensuring compliance with applicable quality and regulatory requirements.
Qualification profile
Bachelor’s or higher degree in Chemistry or a related scientific field.
Extensive experience in pharmaceutical Quality Assurance and regulatory compliance.
In-depth knowledge of GMP, GDP, and relevant pharmaceutical regulations.
Strong Greek and English oral communication and scientific writing skills.
Strong communication, organizational, and leadership skills, with the ability to work effectively across functions.
Attention to detail, commitment to product quality and safety, and ability to make critical decisions and work under pressure.
Competency in Microsoft Office, particularly Microsoft Word and Microsoft Excel.
