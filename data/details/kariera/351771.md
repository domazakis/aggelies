Season 2027 Location : Mykonos
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do
Oversee the daily operations of the laundry department, ensuring the highest standards of cleanliness, presentation, and efficiency.
Manage and train the laundry team to handle guest garments, linens, and staff uniforms with utmost care and precision.
Implement and monitor quality control procedures to maintain consistency and meet the hotel’s luxury brand standards.
Coordinate with Housekeeping, Engineering, and other departments to ensure timely delivery and collection of laundry items.
Supervise inventory levels, including detergents, equipment, and linen stock, ensuring proper maintenance and cost control.
Develop and optimize schedules, workflows, and productivity targets.
Ensure compliance with all health, hygiene, and safety regulations.
Handle guest requests and special garment care with discretion and professionalism.
What you will bring
Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar role within a 5-star hotel or luxury resort.
Proven leadership skills and the ability to motivate and guide a team.
Excellent command of the English language.
Strong knowledge of laundry operations, fabrics, cleaning techniques, and finishing standards.
Familiarity with laundry machinery and preventive maintenance procedures.
Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
Commitment to sustainability and efficient use of resources.
