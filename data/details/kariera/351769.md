Season 2027 - Location: Mykonos
About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What You Will Do:
Lead the Front Office team in delivering warm, intuitive, and highly personalized guest experiences, ensuring every arrival, stay, and departure feels effortless and memorable.
Inspire, coach, and develop a passionate team of hospitality professionals who consistently exceed guest expectations and embody the resort's service culture.
Create an environment where genuine guest connections, attention to detail, and anticipatory service are at the heart of every interaction.
Act as a visible and approachable host, handling guest feedback, special requests, and service recovery opportunities with confidence, empathy, and professionalism.
Collaborate closely with Reservations, Concierge, Housekeeping, Guest Experience, and all operational departments to deliver a seamless and personalized guest journey.
Drive service excellence by monitoring guest satisfaction trends, identifying opportunities for enhancement, and implementing meaningful improvements that elevate the overall experience.
Champion the resort's luxury standards, ensuring consistency, quality, and emotional engagement across every touchpoint of the guest journey.
Lead recruitment, onboarding, scheduling, and performance development initiatives, fostering a culture of accountability, growth, and genuine care for both guests and colleagues.
Use operational and guest insight data to support strategic decision-making while maintaining a strong focus on service quality, team engagement, and commercial success.
Serve as a role model for the resort's values, creating memorable moments that transform first-time visitors into loyal advocates of the brand.
What you need to bring
Minimum 3 years of experience in Front Office operations, preferably in a luxury 5-star hotel environment.
Relevant educational background in Hotel Management, Tourism or related field.
Excellent command of the English language; additional languages are an asset.
Proven leadership and team management skills.
Strong knowledge of PMS, reservations systems, and Microsoft Office.
Exceptional communication, problem-solving, and organizational abilities.
Strong guest service mindset with attention to detail and a proactive approach.
Ability to work under pressure, multitask, and maintain composure in a fast-paced environment.
