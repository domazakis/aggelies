About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Clean and maintain guest areas, corridors and public spaces according to the hotel’s luxury standards.
Support the Housekeeping team with the movement and handling of linen, supplies and cleaning equipment.
Clean balconies, windows and other designated areas as required.
Drive hotel buggies safely and responsibly when performing operational duties.
Respond promptly and professionally to guest requests and operational needs.
Assist Room Attendants and Housekeeping colleagues, particularly during peak periods.
Maintain Housekeeping supplies and report any shortages or replenishment needs.
Keep work and storage areas clean, organized and safe at all times.
Follow all hotel standards related to safety, hygiene, grooming and professional conduct.
Contribute to a seamless guest experience through teamwork, efficiency and attention to detail.
What you need to bring:
Previous experience in Housekeeping or a similar operational role, preferably in a luxury hotel.
Good understanding of cleaning, hygiene and safety standards.
Strong attention to detail and commitment to high standards of cleanliness.
Ability to perform physically demanding tasks and work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
Guest-focused mindset with a professional, discreet and helpful approach.
Good communication and teamwork skills.
Valid driving license and ability to operate hotel buggies safely will be considered an asset.
Positive attitude, reliability and a hands-on approach.
