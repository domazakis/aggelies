Job requirements:
Minimum 2–3 years of experience in sales, business development, luxury hospitality, fine dining, hotels, travel, events, or a similar high-end environment.
Proven experience in sales, business development, networking, account management, or relationship management, preferably within the luxury hospitality or lifestyle sector.
Strong existing network within luxury hotels, travel, tourism, events, concierge services, or premium lifestyle sectors is highly desirable.
Strong commercial mindset with the ability to identify new business opportunities, develop partnerships, and generate repeat business.
Excellent communication, negotiation, presentation, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build strong relationships with clients and industry partners.
Excellent command of English; additional languages are a strong advantage.
Impeccable presentation, confidence, professional etiquette, and the ability to represent the restaurant and hotel at external meetings and industry events.
Highly proactive, outgoing, self-motivated, and results-oriented, with a natural ability to network and develop new contacts.
Strong knowledge of the local luxury hospitality market, clientele, and key industry players.
Excellent organizational skills, attention to detail, and strong follow-up skills.
Ability to work independently, manage multiple business opportunities, and take initiative while collaborating closely with the Reservations, Service, Marketing, and Management teams.
Genuine passion for luxury hospitality, sales, relationship building, and delivering exceptional guest experiences.
Job responsibilities:
Develop and maintain strong relationships with luxury hotels, concierges, travel advisors, event planners, private clients, corporate accounts, and other key partners.
Proactively promote the restaurant and hotel and generate new business through external sales, networking, hotel visits, client meetings, and relationship building.
Identify and develop opportunities with corporate, international, luxury, and high-net-worth clientele.
Build and maintain a strong network of local and international contacts within the luxury hospitality, travel, tourism, events, and premium lifestyle sectors.
Represent the restaurant and hotel at relevant events, luxury venues, hotels, and industry functions, identifying opportunities for new partnerships and business.
Develop partnerships and referral opportunities that contribute to the visibility and revenue of the restaurant.
Follow up with clients and partners to convert opportunities into confirmed business, repeat business, and long-term relationships.
Manage guest requests, VIP arrangements, special occasions, and personalized experiences, ensuring a warm and highly personalized level of service.
Welcome and engage with guests and key clients, representing the restaurant and hotel with confidence, professionalism, and impeccable etiquette.
Maintain accurate records of contacts, leads, guest preferences, and business opportunities, and provide regular updates on sales activity and business development.
Support the restaurant team during service, events, and busy periods when required.
Handle all guest and client information with the highest level of discretion and confidentiality.
