Housekeeping:
Housekeeper
Locations:

Duration:

At Sani/Ikos, the
Housekeeping department
is the art of transforming spaces into havens of comfort and serenity. Every neatly made bed, polished surface, and tidied corner reflects our commitment to excellence.
Our
Housekeeping Team
plays a vital role in maintaining cleanliness in guest rooms and public areas, guided by our values of responsibility and excellence.
If you have a true passion for meticulous care and dedication, join us and be part of a team that takes pride in crafting exceptional guest experiences!
Responsibilities:
Cleaning & Maintenance:
Clean and maintain guest rooms, corridors, and public areas to high standards of hygiene and presentation.
Room Preparation:
Ensure rooms are properly prepared with fresh linens, amenities, and guest supplies.
Inspection & Reporting:
Report any maintenance issues, damages, or lost items to supervisors promptly.
Guest Assistance:
Respond politely to guest requests related to room cleanliness or additional amenities.
Compliance & Safety:
Follow all hygiene, safety, and hotel policies during cleaning and handling of equipment or chemicals.
About you
You have proven experience in housekeeping, preferably in hotels or resorts. You demonstrate attention to detail and the ability to maintain high cleanliness standards, along with good organizational and time-management skills. You also have a professional and guest-focused attitude.
