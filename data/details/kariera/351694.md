Creative Producer, Performance Ads, Fine Jewelry Brand (Athens, full-time)
Hybrid: shoot days at our workshop and on location in Athens, editing remote. The weekly split is flexible and follows the production schedule.
Location: our workshop is currently in Piraeus and moves to central Athens, near Syntagma, within the next 3 to 6 months.
Salary: €1,900 to €2,200 gross per month, based on experience.
Start: December 2026 or January 2027.
We are a fine jewelry brand selling solid gold and diamond jewelry direct to consumers online across English-speaking markets, first and foremost the United States. We have more than doubled year over year, and we plan to double again in 2027.
Your main job is simple to say and hard to do: study what our competitors run on Meta, and soon on TikTok, then shoot and edit ads that beat them and help us scale. You will be the person who knows the DTC ad landscape better than anyone on our team, and who turns that knowledge into new creative every week.
Your priorities, in order: ads first, organic social second.
What you will do
Track the ads leading DTC jewelry and fashion brands run on Meta and TikTok, and spot the hooks, formats and angles that are winning
Turn those insights into new ad concepts built around our pieces
Shoot and edit 5 to 7 new ad concepts every week, plus variations of winning ads: short-form video, statics, on-model close-ups, still life
Write ad hooks, scripts, on-screen text and captions in English
Write clear briefs for creators and models, and direct them on set
Produce every shoot end to end: concepts, props and styling, product prep, booking models and creators, managing shoot budgets
Read ad results every week (hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA, ROAS), report what won, and iterate on it
Plan a monthly content calendar with the founder, aligned with launches and promotions
Run our organic social channels: Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest, repurposing our ad creative for each platform
Help us launch and grow TikTok Ads and TikTok Shop as we expand there: creative, product listings, creator collaborations and live content
What you bring
3+ years creating content for e-commerce or DTC brands (jewelry or fashion experience is a strong plus)
Proven performance creative for DTC brands: you know creative testing, iteration and creative fatigue, and your ads helped scale spend and revenue profitably
You know how to tell a brand story on social, and how to turn that story into ads that sell
Deep knowledge of hooks, formats and pacing: what stops the scroll and what converts in a crowded market
You follow the Meta Ad Library and TikTok Creative Center and know what top jewelry and fashion brands are running right now
You shoot and edit yourself, comfortable with lighting and camera work (equipment provided)
Strong jewelry or small-product photography and video, including close-up on-model work
Strong retouching and color correction for jewelry: metal tones, diamonds, skin
Video editing in Premiere, DaVinci or CapCut
Fluent written English for an American audience
A quiet, refined eye: nothing loud, nothing that looks digital
You judge your work by results, not likes
Nice to have (a strong plus)
Experience with TikTok Ads or TikTok Shop, with results you can show
Experience with Klaviyo email campaigns and flows
Light motion graphics for ads
Smart use of AI tools to speed up editing and variations, never at the cost of how real our pieces look
Experience with UGC, creators or models in Athens
What we offer
A competitive salary, well above the market for this role
Creative ownership of a growing brand
Real budget behind your work: your best ads reach tens of thousands of people every week
Professional equipment provided
Flexible hybrid schedule
Room to grow into leading a creative team as we scale
How we work
Clear weekly deliverables and clear goals. We judge results, not hours.
How to apply
Send us your portfolio and a few examples of your work: ads you made, social content you are proud of, and any results you can share. A few lines on why this role excites you are welcome, plus one jewelry or fashion ad you saw recently that you would have made differently, and why.
We will invite shortlisted candidates to a conversation, followed by a short paid test project with one of our pieces.
