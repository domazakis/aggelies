Job requirements:
Previous experience in a porter, bell attendant, guest service, or similar hospitality position is considered an advantage.
Previous experience within a luxury hotel, resort, or high-end hospitality environment will be considered an asset.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a warm, polite, and guest-oriented approach.
Professional appearance and a positive, welcoming attitude.
Ability to provide discreet, attentive, and personalized service to guests.
Good organizational skills and attention to detail.
Ability to work effectively as part of a professional and collaborative team.
Ability to remain calm and professional during busy periods and adapt to changing guest needs.
Fluency in English is required; additional languages are considered an asset.
Physically fit and comfortable handling luggage and assisting guests across different areas of the property.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking and navigating stairs between guest areas, rooms, and service areas.
Valid driving license is considered an advantage.
Strong understanding of luxury hospitality and guest service standards. Physically fit and comfortable handling luggage and assisting guests across different areas of the property.
Comfortable working in the unique Santorini Caldera environment, including frequent walking, navigating stairs, uneven paths, and different levels of the property while assisting guests and handling luggage.
Job responsibilities:
Welcome and assist guests upon arrival and departure, providing a warm, professional, and personalized experience.
Assist guests with luggage during arrival, departure, room changes, and transfers within the property.
Escort guests to their rooms and provide assistance with luggage, directions, and general information about the property.
Handle guest luggage carefully and ensure it is securely stored and delivered in a timely manner.
Assist the Front Office and Guest Relations teams with guest requests and daily operational needs.
Provide information about hotel facilities, services, restaurants, and local points of interest when required.
Assist with transportation arrangements and coordinate with drivers or other departments when required.
Maintain the entrance, lobby, luggage areas, and guest arrival areas to impeccable standards.
Assist with the movement and delivery of guest items, amenities, packages, and other hotel materials as required.
Support the team with special guest requests, VIP arrivals, and personalized services.
Communicate guest preferences, requests, or concerns promptly to the relevant department.
Ensure a smooth and professional flow of guests and luggage throughout the property.
Handle guest feedback and service requests with professionalism, empathy, and discretion, escalating matters to the appropriate manager when necessary.
Ensure compliance with company standards, health and safety procedures, and security policies at all times.
Maintain a professional appearance and consistently represent the values and standards of the property.
Work closely with Front Office, Guest Relations, Housekeeping, Engineering, and other departments to ensure seamless guest service.
Contribute to a positive, collaborative, and professional team culture.
Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned by the Front Office Manager, Guest Relations Manager, or relevant department leadership
