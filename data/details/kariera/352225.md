Job requirements:
0–2 years of experience in a similar role (hotel or restaurant experience preferred).
Basic understanding of hygiene and sanitation standards (HACCP awareness preferred).
Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
Strong teamwork skills, reliability, and professionalism.
Flexibility to work various shifts (morning / afternoon / evening).
Positive attitude and willingness to learn and grow within the team.
Job responsibilities:
Wash and sanitize dishes, glassware, utensils, pots, pans, and kitchen equipment.
Maintain cleanliness and organization in dishwashing areas, storage rooms, and all auxiliary kitchen spaces.
Operate and maintain commercial dishwashing machines and related cleaning equipment.
Properly store clean kitchenware and equipment in designated areas.
Follow all hygiene, sanitation, and safety regulations in accordance with HACCP guidelines.
Remove waste, handle recycling, and ensure proper disposal of kitchen refuse.
Support the kitchen team with basic operational tasks when required.
Report any malfunctioning equipment or maintenance needs to the supervisor.
Ensure all workstations and support areas remain clean and safe during and after each shift.
