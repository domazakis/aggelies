About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in
Mykonos, Greece
, scheduled for
May 2027,
and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the
debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand
, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality
is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What You Will Do:
Support the management of in-room dining operations, ensuring discreet, timely and personalized service.
Supervise order taking, preparation, delivery, setup and collection across guest rooms, suites and villas.
Lead shift briefings, coordinate staffing and monitor service quality and response times.
Collaborate with Culinary, Bar and Guest Experience teams to accommodate preferences and special requests.
Train and coach colleagues in refined service etiquette, product knowledge and guest privacy.
Assist with cost control, inventory, upselling and performance reporting.
Support pre-opening procedures and ensure compliance with hygiene and safety standards.
What You Need to Bring:
Previous experience in In-Room Dining or Room Service supervision or management in a luxury hotel or resort.
Experience serving suites, villas or highly personalized guest experiences is an advantage.
Pre-opening experience will be considered an advantage.
Excellent command of English; additional languages are an advantage.
Strong leadership, team development and guest relations skills.
Commercial awareness, operational organization and attention to detail.
Discreet, hands-on approach and ability to deliver exceptional service .
