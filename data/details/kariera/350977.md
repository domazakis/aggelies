About SCL Mellon
SCL Mellon is a multi-award-winning, digital-first boutique agency based in Athens, specializing in strategy, content, and performance marketing to build brands and drive measurable growth. The team combines data-driven insight with creative execution to help clients stay ahead in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.
The agency is known for a curious mindset and a commitment to testing emerging technologies and new approaches. SCL Mellon offers a collaborative environment where professionals can work closely with diverse clients and contribute to innovative digital initiatives.
The Role
The
Performance Marketing Specialist
is a full-time role based in Vrilissia, responsible for planning, managing and optimizing paid media campaigns across a portfolio of clients.
We are looking for someone who can translate business objectives into campaigns that deliver measurable results, understand the data behind performance, and turn insights into clear actions and recommendations.
This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys campaign management, testing and optimization, has strong experience in
e-commerce performance
, and sees reporting as a tool for growth rather than simply a monthly obligation.
You will:
Plan, set up, manage and optimize paid campaigns across
Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads
, as well as LinkedIn Ads where relevant.
Manage media budgets, bidding strategies, pacing and overall ad account performance.
Develop and optimize
e-commerce campaigns
with a strong focus on conversions, revenue, ROAS and customer acquisition.
Run structured A/B tests across audiences, creatives, copy, campaign structures and landing pages.
Monitor performance closely and identify opportunities to improve results and budget efficiency.
Prepare performance reports with clear insights, conclusions and next-step recommendations.
Present results to clients and provide recommendations on campaign optimization and budget allocation.
Work closely with creative and content teams to develop and refine performance-driven creatives.
Monitor tracking, conversion data and attribution to ensure campaign decisions are based on reliable data.
Stay up to date with platform developments, new ad formats, automation and AI tools, and test them where they can add value.
Must have:
3+ years of hands-on experience
in paid media or performance marketing, preferably within an agency environment.
Proven hands-on experience managing e-commerce accounts and campaigns with revenue and ROAS targets is required.
Strong experience with
Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads and TikTok Ads Manager
.
Hands-on experience with Google Ads across
Search, Performance Max, Display and YouTube
.
Experience with
LinkedIn Campaign Manager
will be considered a plus.
Relevant Google Ads and Meta certifications are required.
Strong understanding of e-commerce KPIs including
ROAS, CPA, conversion rate, revenue and customer acquisition
.
Good working knowledge of
GA4, Google Tag Manager, conversion tracking and attribution
.
Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn performance data into clear recommendations.
Good client communication skills and confidence presenting results and recommendations.
Excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple accounts and budgets simultaneously.
Strong written and verbal communication skills in both Greek and English.
Curiosity about platforms, consumer behaviour, performance trends and emerging technologies.
