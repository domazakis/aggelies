About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Supervise Room Attendants and Public Area Attendants, ensuring high standards of cleanliness and service.
Support the Executive Housekeeper in the smooth daily operation of the department.
Conduct daily briefings, allocate tasks and support team training and development.
Inspect guest rooms, public areas and Housekeeping facilities to ensure luxury standards are consistently met.
Provide hands-on operational support during peak periods.
Monitor linen, amenities and supplies, reporting shortages and operational needs.
Provide feedback on operational, team and guest-related matters to Housekeeping Management.
Maintain a clean, organized and safe working environment.
Engage with guests and colleagues with warmth, professionalism and genuine care.
Promote hotel services and contribute to an exceptional overall guest experience.
What you need to bring:
Previous experience in a Housekeeping Supervisor role, preferably in a luxury hotel.
Degree or diploma in Hospitality, Tourism or a related field will be considered an asset.
Good command of English; additional languages are an advantage.
Strong leadership and team management skills.
Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills with a guest-focused mindset.
Ability to multitask, prioritize and work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
Good knowledge of housekeeping procedures, cleanliness standards and hotel operations.
