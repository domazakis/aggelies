About the job
Food and Beverage:
Waiter​
Locations:
Greece
: Corfu, Kos, Halkidiki, Crete
Duration:
7 months, between March-November
At Sani/Ikos Group, the
F&B department
is about more than just serving food; it's about creating unforgettable gastronomical experiences. From anticipating guests' preferences to suggesting exquisite culinary delights, every interaction is a chance to make each meal memorable.
Our
F&B Team
focuses on the finest details and ensures a seamless flow of service, taking pride in the smiles of satisfaction we witness every day.
Responsibilities:
Guest Interaction:
Provide attentive and friendly service, taking orders and ensuring guest satisfaction throughout the meal.
Service Delivery:
Serve food and beverages accurately and efficiently, following service standards.
Wine & Beverage Support:
Offer basic wine and drink recommendations; coordinate with the sommelier for specialized service.
Team Collaboration:
Work closely with kitchen and service teams to ensure smooth and timely operations.
Cleanliness & Standards:
Maintain a clean dining area and adhere to hygiene and safety protocols.
About you
You have previous experience working in a restaurant, ideally within a hotel or fine dining environment. You are fluent in English, while knowledge of an additional language is considered an advantage. You possess solid knowledge of food and beverage service, and familiarity with wine service is considered a plus. You demonstrate excellent communication and customer service skills, delivering attentive, polished, and guest-focused service at all times. A degree or diploma in Hospitality or Tourism would further support your professional profile.
* Not sure if you meet all the role requirements? We still welcome your application and a chance to learn more about why you think you could be a valuable addition to the Sani / Ikos team.
