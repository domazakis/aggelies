Ready to build a career at the intersection of insight, influence, and impact?
Do you thrive in fast-paced environments, enjoy tackling complex business questions, and excel at connecting people and ideas? If you’re eager to accelerate your consulting career in a globally connected, high-performance setting, this opportunity may be the right next step.
Dialectica—a global consulting firm connecting clients with industry experts—is seeking driven
Market Research Associates
to join a rapidly expanding team. This role is well-suited for ambitious, intellectually curious professionals who enjoy problem-solving, client interaction, and exposure to diverse industries and markets.
As a
Market Research Associate
, you will play a critical role in supporting strategic decision-making for leading organizations. You will work at the frontline of client engagements—identifying the right experts, facilitating high-impact insight exchanges, and ensuring clients get the insights they need to make informed business decisions.
What You’ll Do
Assess and identify the most suitable industry experts based on client objectives and project requirements
Act as the primary liaison between clients and market specialists
Conduct targeted research across industries, functions, and geographies
Engage and recruit experts through structured outreach and relationship-building
Develop core consulting capabilities, including stakeholder management, negotiation, time management, and professional communication
What We’re Looking For
0–3 years of professional experience
Undergraduate degree in any discipline
Fluency in English
Nice to Have
Strong academic performance
A natural aptitude for negotiation and persuasion
Leadership experience through academic, extracurricular, or professional activities
A strong interest in business, strategy, and global markets
A growth mindset with a commitment to continuous learning
A client-centric approach with high standards for quality and delivery
What’s in It for You

Comprehensive onboarding and ongoing training programs
Dynamic, entrepreneurial culture grounded in respect, ownership, teamwork, and continuous improvement
Comprehensive benefits including private medical insurance and savings plans

Global mobility opportunities across international office locations
