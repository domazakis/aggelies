About the role
As a Barman / Barmaid, you will be at the heart of the guest experience, bringing energy, personality, and craftsmanship to every drink you serve. With each cocktail, conversation, and interaction, you help create moments of genuine hospitality that guests remember long after their stay.
This role blends technical skill with creativity and human connection by balancing quality, speed, and warm service in a vibrant bar environment.
Life Behind the Bar
Every shift has its own rhythm. Some moments flow seamlessly, orders align, the bar runs smoothly, and teamwork is effortless. Other times are more dynamic and lively conversations, diverse guests, and spontaneous interactions shape the atmosphere.
Within this rhythm, you are the one who sets the tone, keeps the bar organized, and ensures every guest feels welcome and relaxed.
What you’ll do
Welcome guests with a friendly, professional, and attentive approach
Prepare and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages according to established recipes and guest preferences
Take and process orders accurately through the point-of-sale system
Recommend cocktails, wines, beers, and other beverages based on guest tastes and preferences
Ensure the bar area is clean, organized, and well-stocked at all times
Adhere to all health, safety, sanitation, and responsible alcohol service guidelines
Handle cash and credit card transactions accurately and efficiently
Collaborate closely with the wider team to ensure smooth operations and exceptional guest experiences
Your Uniqueness is Welcome
We believe the best bartenders are defined not only by recipes and techniques, but by their ability to read the room, connect with guests, and create atmosphere.
If you enjoy working with people, delivering high-quality service, and growing within a hospitality-driven environment, you’ll feel right at home with us.
We’re looking for someone who:
Has previous experience as a Barman / Barmaid (preferred but not required)
Possesses good knowledge of cocktails, wines, beers, and non-alcoholic beverages
Demonstrates excellent customer service skills and a positive, outgoing personality
Can multitask and perform efficiently in a fast-paced environment
Pay attention to detail and take pride in quality service
Is flexible to work evenings, weekends, and holidays
Holds responsible alcohol service certification (preferred)
What we offer
Continuous training and professional development
A dynamic, international, and data-driven work environment
The opportunity to work with a leading hospitality group
Accommodation (for resort properties) and meals provided
