President Hotel is one of the largest hotels in Athens: 513 rooms, the Penthouse 21 rooftop restaurant and pool, and conference and event spaces. We are looking for a
Revenue & Reservations Manager
to lead our pricing & yield strategy and distribution.
Key Responsibilities
Revenue Management & Reservations
Lead the hotel's overall Revenue Management strategy, maximising ADR, occupancy, RevPAR and total revenue.
Develop and implement pricing and yield strategies by day, room type, market segment and distribution channel.
Manage room availability, restrictions and inventory to maximise revenue opportunities.
Prepare and maintain daily, monthly and annual forecasts and contribute to the annual budgeting process.
Analyse historical performance, booking pace, pick-up, demand patterns, market trends and future demand to drive commercial decisions.
Monitor competitors and market positioning through rate shopping, STR and other relevant market intelligence tools.
Manage and optimise distribution across OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia, etc.), GDS and direct booking channels, with a strong focus on profitability and reducing distribution costs.
Work closely with Sales on group, corporate and MICE business, including pricing strategy, profitability assessment and displacement analysis.
Evaluate and optimise channel mix, segmentation and business source performance.
Identify opportunities to maximise revenue during periods of high and low demand through proactive pricing and inventory strategies.
Prepare and present weekly and monthly revenue performance reports to Management, providing clear insights, recommendations and action plans.
Work closely with Management and Sales to align commercial strategy, pricing and business objectives.
Provide leadership and direction to the Reservations team, ensuring efficient operations and high service standards.
Oversee FIT allotments, contracts and travel agent agreements in collaboration with the Sales Department.
Ensure accurate rates, availability and inventory across all relevant systems and distribution channels.
Qualifications
5+ years of experience in Revenue Management, ideally within a large-scale city hotel with 200+ rooms.
Previous experience in Reservations.
Strong commercial understanding of pricing, forecasting, yield management, distribution and profitability.
Degree in Hospitality/Tourism Management, Economics, Business Administration or a related field.
Strong knowledge of PMS, RMS, Channel Manager, BI systems, OTA extranets, GDS and payment gateway platforms.
Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including pivot tables, lookups and forecasting models.
Excellent command of Greek and English; a third language will be considered an advantage.
Strong analytical and data-driven mindset, with the ability to translate complex data into clear commercial decisions.
Excellent leadership, communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to work strategically while maintaining strong attention to detail and execution.
Revenue Management certification (e.g. eCornell, HSMAI, or equivalent).
Experience with major hotel PMS, RMS, Channel Manager and BI platforms.
Experience working in a high-volume, large-scale city hotel environment.
Benefits
Attractive compensation package, based on experience and qualifications.
Performance-based bonus linked to agreed commercial targets.
Group health insurance.
Ongoing training and professional development opportunities.
The opportunity to join one of Athens' established large-scale hotels and play a key role in driving its commercial performance with direct exposure to Management.
