Job requirements:
Previous experience in a similar position within a 5-star hotel, luxury resort, or high-end hospitality environment is required.
Strong attention to detail and a genuine commitment to maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness, hygiene, and presentation.
Professional, discreet, and well groomed appearance.
Good communication skills in English, additional languages are considered an asset.
Ability to work effectively as part of a team and maintain a positive attitude in a fast paced environment.
Reliable, organized, flexible, and consistent, with a strong sense of responsibility.
Ability to work independently and efficiently while following established procedures, cleaning sequences, schedules, and quality standards.
Respect for guest privacy, confidentiality, and discretion at all times.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking, navigating stairs, and carrying housekeeping and cleaning supplies between different levels and areas of the property.
Willingness and flexibility to support both guest room and public area cleaning operations according to the daily needs of the property.
Job responsibilities:
Clean, service, and maintain guest rooms, bathrooms, and assigned areas to the highest standards of cleanliness, hygiene, and presentation.
Clean and maintain public areas, including lobbies, corridors, stairways, terraces, common spaces, restrooms, and other guest facing areas.
Ensure all public areas remain immaculate, organized, and presentable throughout the day, paying particular attention to high traffic areas and guest touchpoints.
Make beds and change linens, towels, and other room textiles according to established hospitality standards.
Replenish guest amenities, stationery, and other in-room supplies, ensuring rooms are fully prepared for guest arrival.
Handle and organize guests' personal belongings with the utmost care, respect, and discretion.
Inspect guest rooms and public areas for damages, maintenance issues, missing items, or shortages and promptly report them to the Housekeeping Supervisor.
Respect and correctly implement “Do Not Disturb” signs and established guest privacy procedures.
Anticipate guest needs and identify opportunities to enhance the guest experience through attention to detail and proactive service.
Clean and maintain housekeeping equipment, tools, storage areas, housekeeping offices, and service areas.
Prepare, organize, and replenish cleaning supplies, equipment, linens, amenities, and other daily operational materials.
Prepare and arrange daily linens, amenities, stationery, and other items required for the smooth operation of the Housekeeping Department.
Store and properly organize unused supplies and materials at the end of each shift.
Follow daily instructions, established cleaning sequences, schedules, and timeframes to ensure efficient completion of assigned duties.
Navigate the property's unique Caldera layout safely and efficiently, including frequent movement between different levels, stairways, guest rooms, and public areas.
Maintain the impeccable presentation of all guest-facing areas throughout the operation, responding promptly to spills, litter, or any cleanliness issues.
Communicate and cooperate effectively with the Housekeeping Supervisor and all members of the Housekeeping and hotel team.
Support the team with additional housekeeping duties as required according to occupancy levels and operational priorities.
Maintain professional behavior, appearance, and conduct at all times.
