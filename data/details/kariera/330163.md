Job Description
Corporate Communication Officer
09/10/2026
Job Description:
We are currently looking for a
Corporate Communication Specialist
to join our
Corporate Communication
team in
Athens, Greece
.
By joining this role, you will play a key role in planning, coordinating, and delivering 360° corporate communication initiatives that strengthen the company's reputation and brand presence. You will act as a bridge between internal and external communications, creating impactful content, managing corporate events, and supporting strategic communication activities across digital and traditional channels.
Your role will encompass:
Plan and execute the annual Corporate Communication plan, ensuring alignment with business objectives and brand guidelines.
Support internal communication initiatives, including key corporate announcements, intranet content, and newsletters.
Coordinate and support internal events that promote the company's vision, culture, and values.
Collaborate with various business functions to develop and distribute engaging communication content across internal channels.
Work closely with IT teams to support the implementation of new technologies that enhance internal communication capabilities.
Support branding initiatives and ensure consistent application of corporate identity guidelines.
Assist in the planning and execution of corporate events, conferences, and ESG-related activities.
Oversee the development of high-quality multimedia content, including videos, photography, presentations, and infographics.
Create and manage content for social media, internal communications, and corporate communication campaigns.
Manage relationships with external partners, agencies, event organizers, and communication vendors.
Support contract administration processes, including specifications, supplier management, invoice verification, and payment tracking.
Monitor communication activities, track KPIs, and prepare reports and insights for management.
What you bring to the role:
Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, Public Relations, or a related field.
4+ years of proven experience in Corporate Communications, Brand Management, Marketing, or related areas.
Experience in B2B, industrial, manufacturing, or energy environments will be considered an asset.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both Greek and English.
Proven experience in planning and executing integrated communication campaigns and corporate events.
Experience in content creation, storytelling, and multimedia communication.
Strong knowledge of digital communication tools, social media management platforms, content management systems, and analytics tools.
