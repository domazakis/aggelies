Job requirements:
Previous experience as an Assistant Waiter, Runner, or in a similar role within fine dining, luxury hospitality, or a high-volume restaurant environment is preferred.
High school diploma or equivalent; hospitality education or relevant training is considered an advantage.
Fluency in English is required; additional languages are highly valued.
Genuine passion for luxury hospitality, guest service, and creating memorable dining experiences.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with a warm, polished, and professional approach to guests.
Ability to work collaboratively with the service team, kitchen, and other departments in a fast-paced environment.
Well-organized, attentive to detail, reliable, and proactive, with the ability to anticipate and respond to guest and team needs.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, which involves frequent walking and navigating stairs throughout the property and dining areas.
Physically capable of remaining active and on their feet for extended periods and carrying service items as required.
Professional appearance and a strong understanding of the standards expected within a luxury dining environment.
Job responsibilities:
Support the service team in delivering seamless, timely, and refined service throughout each shift.
Assist in preparing and maintaining tables, service stations, and dining areas to the highest standards.
Assist with the delivery of food and beverages, ensuring accuracy, presentation, and efficiency.
Move efficiently and safely between the different levels, dining areas, and service points of the Caldera property, navigating stairs as an integral part of daily operations.
Anticipate guest needs and respond promptly with a warm, discreet, and guest-centric approach.
Work closely with Waiters, Bartenders, Kitchen staff, and other team members to ensure a smooth and exceptional dining experience.
Maintain the cleanliness, organization, and impeccable presentation of the restaurant and service areas.
Familiarize yourself with the daily menu, key ingredients, and beverage offerings to confidently assist the service team when required.
Support the team in maintaining the high service standards and attention to detail expected of a luxury Caldera dining destination.
Adhere to all health, safety, hygiene, and company standards at all times.
Assist with opening and closing procedures and other operational tasks as required.
Perform any additional duties and responsibilities assigned by the Restaurant Manager or Supervisor.
