Αποθήκευση
We are looking for an experienced tourism professional who enjoys building relationships, spotting new opportunities and taking an active role in the day-to-day development of a growing tourism business. As our Tourism Business Development & Growth Lead, you will develop and manage B2B partnerships with hotels, DMCs, travel agencies and other tourism partners, manage the sales pipeline and contribute directly to commercial growth. At the same time, you will play an important role in operational coordination, helping ensure that partnerships, bookings and experiences run smoothly and efficiently. You will also contribute to process improvements, new product development and new sales channels. We are looking for someone proactive, commercially minded, organised and hands-on, who can combine business development with strong operational follow-through, work independently and take ownership of results. An existing professional network within the tourism industry will be a strong advantage.
