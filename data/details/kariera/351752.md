Guest Services Manager
Season 2027 Location : Mykonos
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do
Oversee the daily operations of the Guest Services department, ensuring exceptional and personalized experiences for all guests.
Lead, train, and motivate the Guest Services team, including Front Office and Concierge staff, to maintain luxury brand standards.
Handle guest inquiries, complaints, and special requests promptly, professionally, and with discretion.
Coordinate closely with F&B, Housekeeping, Concierge, and other departments to ensure seamless service delivery.
Monitor workflows, staff schedules, and departmental procedures to optimize efficiency and guest satisfaction.
Prepare reports on guest feedback, service metrics, and operational performance for senior management.
Implement service standards and best practices, ensuring consistent excellence across all guest touchpoints.
Act as the hotel’s ambassador for guest experience, proactively anticipating and exceeding guest needs.
What you will bring
Minimum 3 years of experience in Guest Services, Front Office, or Guest Relations within a 5-star or luxury hotel environment.
Excellent command of the English language; additional languages are an asset.
Proven leadership and team management skills.
Strong communication, organizational, and problem-solving abilities.
High guest service orientation, with attention to detail and a proactive mindset.
Ability to handle high-pressure situations calmly and professionally.
Familiarity with PMS and guest management systems.
Flexibility to work shifts, weekends, and public holidays as required.
