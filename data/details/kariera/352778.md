Are you looking to launch your career in a fast-paced international business environment? Do you enjoy connecting people, solving problems, and learning about different industries?
Optimal, on behalf of its client, a leading
insights-on-demand company
, is seeking motivated
Business Client Associates
to join their growing team. This role offers a unique opportunity to work with global clients, develop commercial skills, and gain exposure to a wide range of industries.
As a Business Client Associate, you will play a key role in connecting organizations with industry experts who can provide valuable insights to support important business decisions.

Understand client needs and project requirements
Identify and evaluate relevant industry experts

Conduct research across different industries and markets
Reach out to potential experts through various communication channels
Build strong skills in
sales, negotiation, time management, and professional communication
