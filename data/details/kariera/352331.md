Your role:
Guarantee smooth day-to-day hotel operations and coordinate activities across all departments.
Develop strategies to guarantee exceptional guest experience
Manage the annual budget, monitor expenses and maximize the hotel revenue
Analyze operational data and guest feedback to identify business opportunities
Develop, oversee and implement in coordination with the Hotel operations managers an annual action plan to achieve the business goals.
Prepare operational reports and present hotel performance KPIs to senior management
Collaborate closely with the department heads to achieve alignment with the business goals
Monitor staff performance, fostering a culture of continuous improvement, ownership and positive working environment
Ensure safety and security of all guests and staff
Guarantee compliance with all legal regulations during daily operations
Your profile:
Strong leadership capability, ability to create connecting teams and develop others
Ability to solve problems and manage crisis
Good communicator and Customer oriented
Financial and business acumen
Ability to set priorities, break down complexity, and monitor progress
Proven experience as an Operations Manager or similar role in hospitality
Degree in Hospitality/Tourism or Business Administration or a related field
Good knowledge of hotel management software & PMS Systems and MS Office
Excellent knowledge of English
