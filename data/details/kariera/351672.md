APHI MIRA (ex Miramare) located in Ixia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the
Guest Experience Manager
.
POSITION SUMMARY:
We are looking for a confident, service-driven Guest Experience Manager to lead the guest journey and ensure every stay is memorable. The successful candidate will combine hands-on hospitality, strong leadership and attention to detail to deliver personalised service, resolve concerns effectively and inspire a high-performing team.
Key job responsibilities:
Deliver an exceptional, personalised guest experience throughout every stage of the stay, maintaining a visible and welcoming presence across the hotel.
Recognise and anticipate the needs of loyalty members, repeat guests, VIPs and guests requiring additional assistance, ensuring all benefits and special requests are fulfilled.
Handle guest feedback and complaints promptly and professionally, coordinating with the relevant departments and following through to resolution.
Lead, train and motivate the Guest Experience team, conduct daily briefings, assign priorities and ensure consistent compliance with hotel’s standards procedures.
Work closely with Front Office, Housekeeping, Food & Beverage and other departments to coordinate arrivals, special occasions, events and tailored guest experiences.
Monitor arrivals and guest profiles in the hotel’s systems, manage pre-arrival communication and maintain accurate, up-to-date guest information.
Build strong relationships with guests and tour operator representatives, respond to online reviews and support the hotel’s reputation and social media activity.
Promote hotel services and facilities, support loyalty programme enrolment and contribute to guest satisfaction, quality and commercial targets.
Ensure compliance with health, safety, quality and company standards, and undertake Duty Manager responsibilities when required.
Requirements:
At least three years’ experience in guest relations or guest experience within a luxury hotel.
Fluency in Greek and English; additional languages will be considered an asset.
A degree in Hospitality Management, Tourism, Business Administration or a related field is desirable.
Strong leadership, team-development and supervisory skills, with the ability to motivate others and maintain high service standards.
Outstanding communication, active listening, problem-solving, organisational and multitasking abilities.
Confident user of Microsoft Office, hotel systems and digital communication tools.
Commercial awareness, including promotional, sales and basic budgeting knowledge.
Flexibility to work according to hotel operational requirements.
Benefits:
Salary and benefits package in line with local legislation.
Accommodation, where required, and meals.
Training, professional development and career-growth opportunities.
A diverse, multicultural and supportive working environment.
