What You Will Work On
Reporting to the
CRM, CMP & Sustainability Leader Greece
of the Marketing Department you are responsible and accountable to:
Support the development and implementation of the CRM strategy, aligned with the company's commercial and marketing objectives.
Plan and coordinate CRM marketing activities and campaigns based on the commercial calendar and marketing plan.
Deliver CRM campaigns and events across in-store and outdoor channels for IKEA Greece.
Execute additional CRM campaigns and initiatives based on business priorities and customer needs.
Ensure that the IKEA brand identity, values and key benefits are consistently communicated across all CRM communications.
Ensure compliance with IKEA Family and IKEA for Business guidelines across all relevant communication platforms and touchpoints.
Work with CRM platforms and tools to support campaign implementation, monitoring and optimization.
Prepare CRM reports and performance analysis, providing insights and recommendations for improvement.
Support the implementation of Marketing projectsand initiatives as required
What You Bring
Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration or a related field.
Approximately 4 years of professional experience, preferably in a Marketing or CRM department.
Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
Strong analytical and synthesis skills, with the ability to interpret information and translate it into actionable insights.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Digital literacy and confidence in using digital tools and platforms
Strong organizational and project management skills
Ability to work effectively with multiple stakeholders and manage multiple priorities and deadlines
