About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in
Mykonos, Greece
, scheduled for
May 2027,
and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the
debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand
, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality
is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What You Will Do:
Welcome guests warmly and manage arrivals with discretion and genuine hospitality.
Coordinate reservations, seating plans and table allocation to optimize guest flow.
Recognize returning guests and record preferences to support personalized service.
Communicate waiting times and manage special requests with professionalism.
Liaise with the Restaurant Manager and service team to ensure seamless seating and service.
Handle telephone and digital reservation enquiries accurately and courteously.
Maintain a polished reception area and uphold guest privacy and service standards.
What You Need to Bring:
Previous experience in a hostess, reservations or guest-facing role in luxury hospitality or fine dining.
Excellent command of English; additional languages are a strong advantage.
Confident communication, elegant presentation and excellent interpersonal skills.
Familiarity with restaurant reservation systems is an advantage.
Ability to remain organized, composed and welcoming during peak service.

Attractive salary package aligned with industry standards.

High-quality, comfortable living arrangements provided.
