Job requirements:
Minimum 5 years of proven culinary experience, with previous experience as a Chef de Cuisine, Sous Chef, or in a similar senior culinary leadership position within a luxury hotel, fine dining restaurant, upscale resort, or high end culinary environment.
Strong culinary background with excellent knowledge of modern gastronomy, classical techniques, contemporary cooking methods, and international culinary trends.
Demonstrated experience in developing, executing, and maintaining high-quality menus within a premium or luxury dining environment.
Strong knowledge of Greek and Mediterranean cuisine, seasonal ingredients, and the ability to incorporate local products and culinary identity into a contemporary offering.
Proven ability to lead, motivate, train, and develop a professional kitchen team while maintaining high culinary and operational standards.
Excellent understanding of food preparation, presentation, portion control, consistency, and quality assurance.
Strong knowledge of HACCP, food safety, hygiene, sanitation, and health & safety standards.
Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a demanding and fast paced kitchen environment.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a professional, collaborative, and respectful leadership approach.
Strong commercial awareness, including knowledge of food cost, menu engineering, stock control, purchasing, inventory management, yield management, and waste reduction.
Creative mindset with a passion for culinary innovation, seasonality, and continuous improvement.
Ability to work closely with Restaurant Management and F&B leadership to ensure seamless coordination between kitchen and front of house operations.
Fluency in English is required, additional languages are considered an asset.
Positive attitude, strong leadership presence, professional demeanor, and a genuine team player mentality.
Ability to remain composed and lead effectively during high-volume and high-pressure service periods.
Professional appearance and a strong understanding of ultra-luxury hospitality standards.
Job responsibilities:
Lead the kitchen operation during daily service, ensuring consistently high standards of food quality, preparation, presentation, timing, and guest experience.
Oversee the preparation and execution of all dishes, ensuring consistency with recipes, specifications, portion sizes, and presentation standards.
Maintain a strong presence in the kitchen, leading by example and actively supporting the team during service.
Ensure all culinary operations reflect the restaurant's concept, identity, and commitment to excellence.
Contribute to the development and seasonal evolution of menus, creating innovative dishes inspired by Greek and Mediterranean ingredients and the unique character of Santorini.
Conduct daily kitchen briefings, communicate VIP guests, reservations, special requests, allergies, menu changes, and service priorities.
Organize and coordinate the kitchen team across all sections to ensure smooth and timely service.
Train, coach, and develop kitchen team members, promoting professional growth and maintaining a culture of excellence.
Monitor food quality, preparation, cooking techniques, presentation, service timing, and consistency throughout each service.
Work closely with the Restaurant Manager, Floor Manager, Head Waiter, Sommelier, and wider F&B team to ensure seamless coordination between kitchen and front of house.
Collaborate with the service team regarding menu descriptions, ingredients, allergens, dietary requirements, and guest preferences.
Monitor food stock, product quality, ordering, inventory, food costs, portion control, yields, and waste.
Support supplier relationships, product selection, and quality checks when required.
Ensure proper storage, labeling, rotation, and handling of all food products in accordance with food safety standards.
Ensure full compliance with HACCP, health, safety, hygiene, sanitation, and company policies at all times.
Handle guest feedback related to food with professionalism and discretion, involving management when appropriate.
Support recruitment, onboarding, training, and development of kitchen team members.
Ensure opening, service, and closing procedures are completed correctly and that the kitchen is fully prepared for each service.
Continuously follow culinary trends, techniques, and industry developments to support innovation and the evolution of the restaurant's offering.
