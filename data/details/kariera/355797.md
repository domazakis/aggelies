Marketing Executive
VEESION - The New Era Advertising Agency
Great ideas need creative minds who make things happen.
At Veesion, we’re looking for an organized, proactive, and creative thinker who can connect ideas, people, and projects to join our team in Athens.
👉What You’ll Do
Project Management: Coordinate projects, timelines, deliverables, and workflows using ClickUp.
Client Service: Manage client communication, briefs, meetings, and follow-ups.
Marketing Planning: Support marketing plans, research, campaign planning, and KPIs.
Strategy: Contribute fresh ideas and creative thinking to marketing and communication strategies.
👉What We’re Looking For
1–3 years of experience, preferably in an advertising, creative, or digital agency.
A creative mind with fresh ideas, curiosity, and an unconventional way of thinking.
Strong organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills.
Fluency in both Greek and English, written and spoken.
Good understanding of marketing and digital communication.
A proactive attitude, attention to detail, and a collaborative spirit.
👉What We Offer
A creative and collaborative working environment.
Diverse brands and exciting projects.
Opportunities to learn, contribute and grow.
Veesion benefits.
Location: Athens, Greece
Eligibility: Applicants must currently reside in Greece. Remote applications will not be considered.
