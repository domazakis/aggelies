Conrad Corfu
Conrad Corfu, opening in 2027, is the latest addition to the Numo Hotels & Resorts portfolio. More than a place to stay, it’s a destination where authentic Corfiot heritage meets stylish modern living, designed to connect people with culture, nature, and each other.
Key Responsibilities
Oversee and effectively manage all Food and Beverage (F&B) operations along with the daily operational activities, ensuring that they align with budgeted financial guidelines and meet the highest standards of quality and service excellence.
Maintain and preserve exceptional levels of customer service, fostering a welcoming and professional environment.
Collaborate with chefs to design innovative, cost-effective menus aligned with brand standards and customer preferences.
Proactively identify and address customers’ needs, ensuring that all concerns and inquiries are handled in a timely and satisfactory manner.
Lead, inspire, and develop a high-performing F&B team by actively attracting, recruiting, training, and evaluating skilled personnel, fostering a culture of excellence and continuous improvement.
Ensure strict compliance with all Health and Safety regulations to provide a safe and secure environment for both staff and customers.
Establish and monitor clear performance targets, Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), policies, and operational procedures for the department, ensuring alignment with overall organizational goals.
Provide regular and detailed reports to management regarding sales results, operating costs, and overall productivity to inform strategic decision-making.
Ensure that all operations are in compliance with ISO and HACCP quality management standards, maintaining the integrity and safety of food and beverage offerings.
Requirements
Degree or Diploma in Hospitality Management, Business or related field
Experience of at least 3 years in a similar position in 5-star hotels
Excellent knowledge of English
Very good knowledge of a second language will be an advantage
