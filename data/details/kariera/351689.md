Introduction about the Organization
Elval is the aluminium rolling division of ElvalHalcor S.A., one of the leading flat rolled aluminium manufacturers worldwide and the only one in Greece. For more than 40 years, Elval has been recognized as a trusted partner with a broad portfolio of quality products for the packaging, construction, shipbuilding, automotive, energy, industrial and HVAC markets. Through an established global commercial network, Elval exports approximately 80% of its production to more than 60 countries and offers reliable and competitive solutions that meet the requirements of the most demanding global customers.
