About us
AEGEAN, based in Athens, Greece, is a member of the Star Alliance Group since 2010 and a proud winner of 15 SKYTRAX World Airline awards for ‘Best Regional Airline in Europe’. Our employees have been instrumental in our success, through seeking to channel their energy and motivation into the challenging industry of aviation, providing services of high standards across all travel stages and contributing to the continuous improvement of our products. We are looking for enthusiastic, talented and dedicated individuals who can drive our organization forward.
If you wish to explore the exciting world of aviation, join our team!
About the role
There is currently a job opening
Cabin Crew Member based in Athens
