Hemmersbach
provides IT infrastructure services in more than 190 countries with 50 own subsidiaries. We deliver exclusively for the leading companies in the IT industry. We go the extra mile – we not only simply enthuse our customers, but also make the world a better place: 20% of our profits go into our Direct Actions Hemmersbach Rhino Force and Hemmersbach Kids’ Family. That’s why Hemmersbach is The Social Purpose IT Company.
Role:
Process orders for BAU projects and support new projects’ implementation
Ensure the goods are procured and delivered at best possible price, within deadline and from most reliable vendors to maximize the margin of a project
Negotiate prices, terms, and conditions of contracts with strategic vendors and establish relationships with them
Be responsible for selecting the best vendors
Requirements:
3+ in purchasing/procurement
Previous experience (or equivalent) in commercial role
C1 English
Ability to work in ERP/WMS
IT background is huge asset
Project management knowledge

Rhino-force.org: providing environmental protection for an endangered species
Kids-family.org: taking care of children without parents in Poland and India, turning their shelters into homes.
