Job requirements
A minimum educational requirement, although additional education or training in hospitality can be beneficial.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills to provide exceptional service and address guest needs effectively.
Meticulousness in order-taking, ensuring accuracy in deliveries, and setting up in-room dining presentations.
Ability to manage orders, payments, and service setups efficiently, maintaining order in a fast-paced environment.
Proficiency in basic mathematics for order calculations, bill preparation, and payment processing.
Physical fitness to carry trays, walk, and stand for extended periods while delivering orders to guest rooms.
A polished appearance and professional behavior when interacting with guests.
Ability to collaborate with kitchen staff, colleagues, and management to ensure smooth service operations.
Job responsibilities
Receive and accurately record guest orders for food and beverages, ensuring all special requests are noted.
Promptly and courteously deliver orders to guest rooms, maintaining the presentation and quality of items.
Accurately prepare and present guest bills, handle payments, and address any billing inquiries.
Arrange in-room dining tables, ensure proper table settings, and create a pleasant dining atmosphere.
Provide exceptional customer service, addressing guest needs, inquiries, and special requests.
Maintain cleanliness and organization in the service area, ensuring all equipment and supplies are well-maintained and stocked.
