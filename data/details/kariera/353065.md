Responsibilities:
Oversee daily operations and quality assurance across all Phaedrus Living properties in Athens, ensuring consistent service and operational excellence.
Direct all aspects of property readiness — including cleaning, maintenance scheduling, and pre-arrival inspections — to ensure seamless guest experiences.
Establish and enforce detailed cleaning and quality assurance protocols across all properties.
Conduct regular quality audits using digital checklists, photo verification, and reporting tools to monitor compliance and performance trends.
Manage the cleaning services division, optimizing labor allocation, turnaround efficiency, and real-time communication through task management platforms.
Utilize property management systems (PMS), scheduling software, and data dashboards to monitor occupancy, work orders, and service response times.
Implement technology-driven process improvements, including automation of inspection reports, vendor tracking, and issue resolution workflows.
Collaborate with maintenance and operations teams to proactively address recurring issues using data insights.
Analyze performance metrics to drive continuous improvement, reduce operational costs, and increase guest satisfaction scores.
Partner with leadership to align operational strategy with growth objectives and maintain brand consistency.
Perform other relevant assignments as requested.
Requirements:
Proven experience in hospitality operations, housekeeping supervision, or quality assurance (short-term rentals or hotels preferred).
Excellent command of Greek and English, both verbal and written.
Proficiency in using property management systems (PMS), scheduling software, and digital reporting tools.
Strong organizational and leadership skills with attention to detail.
Excellent communication, problem-solving, and time management skills.
Commitment to maintaining high operational and service standards.
EU driving license required.
We offer a competitive salary and benefits package, a dynamic work environment, and opportunities for growth and advancement.
