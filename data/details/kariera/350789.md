Inter Cars Greece Single Member Liability Company
is a subsidiary of Inter Cars S.A., the biggest importer and distributor of automotive spare parts and garage equipment in the Middle and Eastern Europe, with over 200 branches in Poland and more than 190 branches in Europe. Inter Cars Greece values passion and involvement, partnership, reliability, loyalty and leadership by example.
Inter Cars Greece
is interested in appointing a
Logistics Manager
for the Logistic Department, based in
Larisa
.
📚Responsibilities
✔️
Oversee daily logistics operations, including warehousing, transportation, and inventory management.
✔️
Plan and optimize delivery routes to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and ensure timely deliveries.
✔️
Conduct Transportation / Logistics Analysis to identify cost-saving opportunities, optimize routes, improve fleet utilization, and enhance delivery performance.
✔️
Manage logistics KPIs, prepare reports, and provide insights to support strategic decision-making.
✔️
Coordinate with internal teams (Sales, Warehouse, HR) and external partners to ensure smooth operations.
✔️
Develop and implement logistics policies, procedures, and best practices.
✔️
Manage budgets, forecast transportation costs, and optimize resource allocation.
✔️
Lead and develop a high-performing logistics team, fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
✔️
Ensure compliance with health, safety, and legal regulations in logistics operations.
👤Requirements
✅BSc degree in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, or related field.
✅5+ years of experience in logistics management or supply chain leadership.
✅Strong knowledge of transportation planning, warehouse operations, and inventory management.
✅Experience with ERP, WMS, or TMS systems and advanced Excel / BI tools.
✅Excellent command of English and Greek (written and verbal)
✅Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
✅Strong leadership and team management abilities.
