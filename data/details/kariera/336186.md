Inter Cars Greece Single Member Liability Company
is a subsidiary of Inter Cars S.A., the biggest importer and distributor of automotive spare parts and garage equipment in the Middle and Eastern Europe, with over 200 branches in Poland and more than 190 branches in Europe. At Inter Cars Greece, we are driven by our core values of passion and commitment, partnership, reliability, loyalty, and leadership by example.
Inter Cars Greece is interested in appointing an
Accounting Assistant
in the
Athens Metropolitan Area.
📚Main Tasks
✔️
Accounting entries and posting of invoices (suppliers & expenses)
✔️Support in accounts payable process
✔️Reconciliation of suppliers and general ledger accounts
✔️Monitoring and filing of accounting documents in accordance with company procedures
✔️Communication with internal departments and external partners (suppliers, auditors)
👤 Requirements of qualified candidate
✅Degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics or related field
✅Up to 2 years of experience or strong interest in starting a career in accounting
✅Analytical mindset with attention to detail and accuracy
✅Computer literate, with ability to quickly learn ERP systems (knowledge of ERP Navision will be considered a plus)
✅Very good command of verbal and written English
✅Ability to work both independently and as part of a team
✅Willingness to learn, develop and grow within the organization
🎁The Company provides
💰Competitive salary package
🏢Work in a dynamic multinational environment
👩‍⚕️Private medical health insurance program
📩 Expression of Interest
If you would like to become part of a dynamic team in the automotive aftermarket industry, we would be pleased to receive your CV.
