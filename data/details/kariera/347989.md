Development Senior Associate_Handover
We are looking for a Development Senior Associate to join our Development team.
The successful candidate will provide technical expertise and advisory support to the Development Handover team across residential developments within The Ellinikon Project. On a project-by-project basis, he/she will support the team's handover preparation activities, including technical reviews, buyer documentation, utility application strategies, and engneering-related deliverables, contributing to the successful transition of building systems from construction completion to homeowner occupancy.
The Development Senior Associate - Handover will report to the Senior Manager and will provide technical support across multiple development projects as required.
Key responsibilities:
Core competencies, knowledge & experience:
Technical Advisory Support: Provides technical expertise and guidance to the Development Handover team during project handover preparation, ensuring alignment with project requirements and best practices.
Buyer Documentation & Technical Deliverables: Supports the preparation and technical review of buyer documentation, including O&M manuals, warranties, certifications, as-built drawings, utility records, and homeowner technical documentation.
Utility Applications & Technical Coordination: Supports the development of utility application strategies and provides technical input throughout utility application, inspection, approval, and service activation processes.
Commissioning & Technical Verification: Reviews commissioning, testing, inspections, and MEP system readiness, providing technical recommendations to support successful handover.
Technical Support During Handover: Provides technical guidance to the handover team during homeowner inspections, defect resolution, operational readiness, and systems transition where required.
Reporting & Technical Risk Assessment: Supports the identification of technical risks, outstanding MEP issues, and recommends mitigation measures during handover preparation.
Academic Qualifications:
• A Bachelor's degree in Engineering (Civil, Architect, MEP), Business Administration, Management, or another relevant discipline is preferred.
• Excellent command of the English language; any additional language will be considered an asset.
• Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
• Experience with SAP or other enterprise management systems will be considered an advantage.
Professional Experience:
Minimum 10 years of professional experience in engineering, design management, construction, project delivery, or real estate development.
Strong experience in large-scale residential, mixed-use, hospitality, or masterplan developments.
Demonstrates experience in the design, coordination, delivery, and technical oversight of systems throughout the project lifecycle.
Experience working with developers, consultants, contractors, utility providers, and authorities during design, construction, commissioning, and project close-out.
Good understanding of building services systems, utility infrastructure, commissioning processes, and statutory requirements
Experience reviewing technical documentation, specifications, as-built drawings, O&M manuals, and other project deliverables.
Knowledge of local construction practices, applicable regulations, and development best practices.
Strong technical judgement with the ability to provide practical engineering solutions and support multidisciplinary project teams.
Knowledge of development best practices, project close-out requirements, and building systems operation.
On a personal side, the ideal candidate:
Has excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to communicate technical matters clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Demonstrates strong technical judgement, analytical thinking, and a solution-oriented mindset.
Possesses strong coordination, planning, and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
Takes ownership, demonstrates accountability, and maintains high standards of quality and attention to detail.
Thrives in a dynamic, fast-paced project environment and collaborates effectively within multidisciplinary teams.
Builds strong working relationships with consultants, contractors, utility providers, and internal stakeholders.
