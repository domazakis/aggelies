Job requirements:
Minimum 3-4 years of experience in a similar supervisory role within a 5-star hotel, luxury resort, or comparable hospitality environment.
Solid understanding of housekeeping operations, room presentation, cleaning procedures, and the standards expected in luxury hospitality.
Previous hands on experience as a Maid is required.
Good command of English, with the ability to communicate clearly and effectively with guests and colleagues.
Strong leadership and people management abilities, with the confidence to guide a housekeeping team.
Well organized and capable of prioritizing tasks according to occupancy, arrivals, departures, and daily operational requirements.
Strong eye for detail and a consistent focus on cleanliness, presentation, and guest comfort.
Ability to identify operational challenges, take initiative, and find practical solutions in a timely manner.
Flexible and adaptable, with a willingness to support the department and develop professionally.
Basic computer literacy for daily reports, staff scheduling, room status updates, and departmental documentation.
Professional appearance, mature attitude, and a genuine commitment to delivering high quality guest experiences.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera environment, including frequent movement between different levels and navigating stairs throughout the property.
Job responsibilities:
Supervise and coordinate the daily operations of the Housekeeping Department, ensuring high standards of cleanliness, presentation, and guest comfort.
Allocate daily tasks and monitor the performance of housekeeping staff according to occupancy, arrivals, departures, and operational priorities.
Inspect guest rooms, public areas, and back-of-house areas to ensure they meet the hotel's luxury standards.
Ensure guest rooms are properly cleaned, serviced, inspected, and prepared for arrivals, including linen, amenities, and supplies.
Supervise the cleanliness and presentation of all public areas, including lobbies, corridors, stairways, terraces, and restrooms.
Coordinate with Front Office, Maintenance, and other departments regarding room status, guest requests, maintenance issues, and operational requirements.
Train, guide, and motivate housekeeping team members while ensuring compliance with departmental procedures and service standards.
Monitor housekeeping supplies, linen, equipment, and storage areas, ensuring proper organization and sufficient stock.
Address guest requests and complaints professionally and ensure appropriate follow-up.
Ensure proper handling of lost and found items and respect guest privacy and “Do Not Disturb” procedures.
Maintain accurate housekeeping reports, room status updates, staff assignments, and other departmental records.
Identify operational issues and take prompt, practical action to resolve them or escalate them when necessary.
Ensure safe working practices and proper use of cleaning products and equipment.
Support the housekeeping team with hands on duties when required, particularly during busy periods.
Ensure professional appearance, conduct, teamwork, and consistent adherence to hotel standards at all times.
