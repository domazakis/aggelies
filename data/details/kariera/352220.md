Job requirements:
Minimum 5-6 years of proven experience in Food & Beverage, with previous experience as a Captain, Head Waiter, Senior Waiter, or similar supervisory role within a luxury hotel, fine dining restaurant, or high-end hospitality environment.
Strong knowledge of luxury and fine dining service standards, food and beverage operations, table service, and guest relations.
Strong knowledge of wine and wine service, including grape varieties, wine regions, styles, food pairing, and appropriate serving techniques. Sommelier certification or formal wine training is considered an advantage.
Proven ability to lead and coordinate a service team while maintaining a hands-on approach during daily operations.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a warm, polished, and guest oriented approach.
Strong leadership, organizational, and problem solving skills, with the ability to remain composed and effective during busy service periods.
Excellent knowledge of food and beverage offerings, including menu ingredients, preparation methods, wines, cocktails, and other beverages.
Confident guest engagement and suggestive selling skills, with the ability to provide knowledgeable recommendations and create personalized dining experiences.
Fluency in English is required, additional languages are considered an asset.
Strong attention to detail, professional appearance, and a genuine commitment to maintaining luxury hospitality standards.
Positive attitude, professional demeanor, and ability to motivate and support team members.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking and navigating stairs between different levels, dining areas, service stations, and operational points of the property.
Job responsibilities:
Lead and coordinate the service team during daily operations, ensuring seamless, timely, and consistently elevated service.
Supervise the assigned dining or service areas and ensure that all operational and brand standards are maintained.
Conduct pre service briefings and ensure the team is informed of reservations, VIP guests, special occasions, guest preferences, menu changes, wine selections, and daily operational priorities.
Coordinate closely with the Restaurant Floor Manager, Head Waiters, Waiters, Assistant Waiters, Bartenders, Kitchen, and other departments to ensure smooth service.
Welcome and engage with guests when required, ensuring a warm, discreet, and personalized experience throughout their visit.
Demonstrate strong wine knowledge and confidently assist guests with wine selections, recommendations, pairings, and service.
Support the presentation and service of wines according to established luxury dining standards, including appropriate glassware, temperature, opening, pouring, and table service.
Monitor service timing, table presentation, staff performance, cleanliness, and overall guest satisfaction.
Support the team during service by assisting with food and beverage delivery and ensuring appropriate service sequences are followed.
Maintain strong knowledge of the menu, wine list, cocktails, and beverage offerings and assist guests with informed recommendations.
Encourage confident and appropriate suggestive selling of food and beverage offerings while maintaining a genuine, guest centric approach.
Anticipate guest needs and proactively address requests, special occasions, and preferences.
Handle guest feedback and service concerns professionally and discreetly, escalating matters to the Restaurant Floor Manager when appropriate.
Coordinate service efficiently across the property's unique Caldera layout, ensuring effective communication and smooth movement between different levels and service areas.
Support the training, coaching, and development of junior members of the team, including service standards, wine knowledge, and guest engagement.
Assist with table allocation, seating flow, reservations, and operational coordination when required.
Ensure service stations, dining areas, and back of house service areas are maintained to impeccable standards.
Monitor the availability of service equipment, tableware, glassware, wine tools, and other operational supplies and communicate replenishment needs.
Ensure compliance with all health, safety, hygiene, responsible alcohol service, and company policies.
Assist with opening, daily, and closing procedures and ensure the restaurant is fully prepared for each service.
Contribute to a positive, collaborative, and professional team environment that reflects the values of the property and the group.
Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned by the Food & Beverage leadership.
