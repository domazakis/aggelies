Job requirements:
Minimum 3-5 years of proven experience in bartending, with previous experience as a Head Bartender, Bar Supervisor, or in a similar leadership position within a luxury hotel, fine dining venue, upscale restaurant, or high end cocktail bar.
Extensive knowledge of mixology, classic and contemporary cocktails, spirits, wines, and beverage service, with a strong understanding of current industry trends.
Proven ability to lead, motivate, and develop a professional bar team while maintaining high service standards.
Strong knowledge of cocktail preparation, garnishing, presentation techniques, and beverage pairing.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a polished, confident, and guest oriented approach.
Strong organizational and problem solving skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities during busy service periods.
Confident, suggestive selling skills and the ability to guide the team in creating personalized beverage experiences.
Fluency in English is required, additional languages are considered an asset.
Strong commercial awareness, with an understanding of beverage costs, stock control, inventory management, and minimizing waste.
Positive attitude, professional demeanor, strong leadership presence, and a genuine team player mentality.
Ability to remain composed and lead effectively in a fast paced luxury hospitality environment.
Comfortable working in a unique Caldera setting, involving frequent walking and navigating stairs between the bar, service areas, and different levels of the property.
Professional appearance and a strong understanding of ultra-luxury hospitality standards.
Job responsibilities:
Lead the bar team during daily service, ensuring consistently high standards of beverage quality, service, presentation, and guest experience.
Prepare and oversee the preparation of cocktails and beverages, ensuring consistency in taste, quality, presentation, and execution.
Maintain excellent knowledge of the beverage menu, spirits, wines, cocktails, ingredients, and current mixology trends.
Support the development and refinement of the beverage offering, sharing ideas and feedback with the Restaurant Manager and F&B leadership.
Brief the bar team on daily operations, reservations, VIP guests, special requests, menu changes, and service priorities.
Train, coach, and support Bartenders and Baristas, promoting professional development and leading by example.
Engage personally with guests, anticipate their preferences, and provide a warm, discreet, and personalized bar experience.
Lead suggestive selling and beverage recommendations, supporting both guest satisfaction and beverage sales.
Monitor beverage quality, service timing, presentation, cleanliness, and overall guest satisfaction throughout service.
Assist with stock control, inventory counts, beverage consumption, and replenishment, reporting shortages or discrepancies to the relevant manager.
Support effective beverage cost control by minimizing waste and ensuring responsible use of products, equipment, and supplies.
Ensure the bar, equipment, glassware, mise en place, and service areas are maintained to impeccable standards throughout the day.
Coordinate the bar team efficiently across the unique Caldera layout, ensuring smooth communication and service between different levels and areas of the property.
Handle guest feedback and service concerns with professionalism, empathy, and discretion, escalating matters to management when appropriate.
Ensure compliance with responsible alcohol service, health, safety, hygiene, and company policies at all times.
Ensure opening, service, and closing procedures are completed correctly and that the bar is fully prepared for each service.
Work closely with the Restaurant Manager, Floor Manager, Head Waiter, Kitchen, and wider F&B team to ensure seamless overall operations.
Contribute to a positive, collaborative, and professional team culture that reflects the values and standards of the property and the group.
Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned by the Restaurant Manager or F&B leadership.
