Job Description
Project Manager Transformation Office
28/09/2026
Job Description:
We are looking for a
Project Manager Transformation Office
to join our team and play a key role in the design, execution, and monitoring of strategic transformation programs. Reporting to the Transformation Office Director, you will drive cross-functional initiatives, ensure governance and delivery excellence, and contribute to the continuous improvement of transformation processes across the organization.
Your role will encompass:
Support the design and structuring of transformation programs, including initiative portfolios and governance models
Develop and maintain program roadmaps, tracking progress through KPIs and performance dashboards
Monitor execution and ensure alignment with strategic objectives and timelines
Establish and manage program governance cadence (e.g. weekly meetings, SteerCos), including preparation of materials
Lead end-to-end execution of projects and initiatives driven by the Transformation Office
Coordinate cross-functional teams and ensure effective resource allocation
Collect, analyze, and interpret data to support decision-making and project delivery
Develop detailed action plans and track implementation progress
Manage external partners and third parties where applicable
Drive stakeholder communication, including senior leadership and C-level executives
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives, including process optimization and use of technology/ AI tools
What You Bring to the Role:
Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Business, or Economics; Master's degree is a plus
4+ years in transformation office, strategy or PMO roles in multinational companies (ideally within the energy and industrial goods space)
2+ years of experience in management consulting; experience in transformation programs and large-scale PMO
Proficiency in MS Office tools (Excel, PowerPoint, Word)
Familiarity with AI tools (e.g. Microsoft Copilot) is considered an asset
Fluency in English; additional languages (e.g. French, Spanish, Italian) are a plus
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured and strategic mindset
Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively in dynamic, fast-paced environments
Proven stakeholder management skills, with the ability to interact confidently with senior leadership
Team player with the ability to lead and collaborate across cross-functional teams
Share the same Value Based Behaviors:
People & Personal growth
Results orientation & Safety excellence
Collaboration & Trust
Empathy & Effective communication
Flexibility & Adaptability
