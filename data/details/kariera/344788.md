Αποθήκευση
You may not see the right opportunity for you today. That doesn't mean the conversation has to wait.
At Nova, we are always interested in meeting talented people who are curious, forward-thinking and ready to make an impact. Whatever your professional or academic background, experience or career path, we would be happy to learn more about you.
By sharing your CV, you will become part of Nova Talent Community. This gives our Talent Acquisition team the opportunity to consider your profile for future roles that match your experience, interests and career aspirations.
Who we would love to hear from
We welcome people from all professional backgrounds and career stages. From those taking their first steps to experienced professionals looking for their next challenge.
What matters most is your willingness to grow, contribute and be part of a team that is helping shape the future of connectivity and entertainment.
What happens next?
Once you submit your CV, our Talent Acquisition team will be able to review your profile and contact you if a suitable opportunity comes up.
Please note that this is an open application and is not linked to a specific vacancy. For roles currently available at Nova, you can also explore our open positions and apply directly to those that interest you.
Your next opportunity may not be open yet, but it could start here!
Share your CV and stay connected with Nova.
