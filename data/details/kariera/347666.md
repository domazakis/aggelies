About the job
Guest Experience:
Airport Representative
Locations:
Greece Corfu, Kos, Halkidiki, Crete
Duration:
7 months, between March-November
At Sani/Ikos Group, the
Guest Experience department
plays a key role in creating exceptional and personalized journeys for our guests. Our
Guest Experience Team
ensures seamless coordination of VIP transfers, professional driver services, and efficient support through the CRR team, which manages restaurant reservations and guest requests with delivering comfort, convenience, and outstanding hospitality at every touchpoint.
Responsibilities:
Guest Arrival Management:
Handle guests’ arrivals, including pick-up requests, luggage assistance, and transportation to the hotel.
Flight Coordination:
Be present at scheduled flight landings to assist incoming guests with baggage clearance and guide them to transport facilities.
VIP & VVIP Service:
Ensure VIP and VVIP guests are welcomed professionally, with seamless transportation arrangements.
Guest Assistance & Information:
Provide information on hotel facilities and assist guests with car transfers or official car rentals when needed.
Airport Liaison:
Maintain strong relationships with airport personnel, including airline employees, immigration, and customs officers, to facilitate smooth guest services.
About you
You have experience in guest services, front office, or airport operations, preferably in hospitality or travel. You demonstrate excellent communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills, along with a professional and guest-focused demeanour and the ability to work under pressure. You also possess knowledge of airport procedures, airline protocols, and transfer logistics.
