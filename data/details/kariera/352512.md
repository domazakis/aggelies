Astor Hotel Athens is seeking to cover the position of Receptionist
Key Responsibilities:
Manage check-in and check-out procedures in a professional and efficient manner.
Welcome guests with a friendly and professional attitude, ensuring a positive first impression and high standards of hospitality.
Handle guest requests, inquiries, and concerns promptly, providing effective solutions with a customer-oriented approach.
Answer and manage incoming phone calls and provide accurate information to guests.
Coordinate daily operations with housekeeping, maintenance, and other hotel departments to ensure smooth guest service.
Provide information regarding hotel facilities, services, and points of interest in Athens.
Handle reservations, payments, and administrative tasks related to front office operations.
Maintain a professional appearance and represent the values and standards of Astor Hotel Athens at all times.
Required Qualifications:
Previous experience in a similar Front Office / Receptionist role within the hospitality industry.
Studies in Tourism, Hospitality Management, or a related field will be considered an advantage.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Strong guest service orientation and problem-solving skills.
Good knowledge of computer systems and hotel reservation software (experience with Protel will be considered an advantage).
Ability to multitask, prioritize responsibilities, and work effectively under pressure.
Fluency in English; additional languages will be considered an asset.
Flexibility to work on rotating shifts, including weekends and holidays.
Team spirit and ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues.
What We Offer:
Opportunity to grow professionally within a well-established city hotel.
Supportive and professional working environment.
Continuous training and development opportunities.
A dynamic workplace in the heart of Athens.
