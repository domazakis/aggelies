About Grand Forest Metsovo
Perched at 1,350 metres above sea level, on the edge of a secluded black pine forest in the Pindus Mountains, Grand Forest Metsovo is a five-star luxury mountain retreat and a proud member of
The Leading Hotels of the World
.
More than a hotel, Grand Forest is a destination where nature, wellbeing and genuine hospitality come together to create meaningful experiences.
At the heart of our philosophy lie four guiding values:
Respect, Transparency, Integrity and Genuineness.
These principles shape the way we welcome our guests, care for our people and connect with our surroundings.
The Opportunity
We are seeking a passionate and skilled
Spa Therapist
to join our team at
Aspen Wellness
, our spa in collaboration with the prestigious Swiss skincare house
Valmont
.
As a Spa Therapist, you will play an essential role in delivering exceptional wellness experiences, combining technical expertise, intuitive service and genuine care.
We are looking for a professional who understands that true luxury lies in the details, who takes pride in their craft and who is committed to creating personalised experiences that leave a lasting impression.
Key Responsibilities
Deliver a range of professional spa treatments, including relaxing, deep tissue and therapeutic massages, body rituals and facial treatments.
Perform specialised face and body treatments in accordance with established spa protocols and luxury service standards.
Provide personalised consultations, recommending treatments and wellness experiences tailored to each guest's needs.
Develop a thorough understanding of Valmont products and treatment protocols, ensuring their effective application and presentation.
Maintain the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, safety and treatment room preparation.
Ensure all spa equipment, treatment products and supplies are properly maintained and replenished.
Create a welcoming, discreet and relaxing environment throughout the guest journey.
Build genuine connections with guests, anticipating their needs and ensuring their comfort and satisfaction.
Confidently recommend suitable spa treatments, experiences and retail products.
Collaborate closely with the Spa Manager and the wider hotel team to ensure seamless service.
Support the daily operation of the spa, including treatment scheduling and guest enquiries when required.
Participate actively in training programmes and continuously develop professional knowledge and skills.
What We're Looking For
Essential Qualifications
Relevant professional qualification or certification in Massage Therapy, Aesthetics, Beauty Therapy or a related field.
Minimum 2 years of professional experience in a luxury spa, five-star hotel or premium wellness environment.
Strong practical knowledge of massage techniques, facial treatments and body therapies.
Excellent command of English; knowledge of Greek or additional languages will be considered an advantage.
Exceptional grooming, communication and interpersonal skills.
A genuine passion for wellness, hospitality and personalised guest service.
Strong organisational skills, attention to detail and the ability to work effectively within a team.
Professionalism, discretion and a positive, guest-focused attitude.
Additional Assets
Previous experience with
Valmont
skincare products and treatment protocols.
Additional certifications in specialised massage techniques or advanced facial treatments.
Experience in luxury spa retail and product recommendations.
Familiarity with the service expectations of an international luxury clientele.
