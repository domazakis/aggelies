Waiter/Waitress
About the role
As a Waiter / Waitress, you will play a key role in shaping the dining experience of our guests. Through attentive service, menu knowledge, and genuine hospitality, you help turn every meal into a memorable moment.
Your role blends professionalism, communication, and pace — ensuring that service flows smoothly while maintaining high standards of quality.
Life Inside the Restaurant
Behind every set table lies a story of flavors, people, and experiences. Some days follow a steady rhythm — orders flow, teamwork feels effortless, and service runs seamlessly. Other days are more dynamic — higher volume, diverse guest needs, and moments that call for flexibility and quick thinking.
Within this environment, you bring consistency, positive energy, and attention to detail.
What you’ll do
Welcome and serve guests with a friendly, professional approach
Take and relay orders accurately to the kitchen or bar
Present the menu, make recommendations, and answer guest questions
Maintain clean, organized dining areas at all times
Collaborate closely with kitchen staff and fellow team members
Process bills and payments efficiently and accurately
Follow all health, safety, and hygiene standards
Address guest requests or concerns promptly to ensure satisfaction
Your Uniqueness is Welcome
We believe great waiters and waitresses are defined not only by speed or technique, but by their ability to connect with guests and create a welcoming atmosphere.
If you enjoy working with people and delivering meaningful hospitality, you’ll feel right at home with us.
We’re looking for someone who:
Has previous experience in a similar role (preferred but not required)
Demonstrates excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Can multitask effectively in a fast-paced environment
Has a positive attitude and strong team spirit
Pays attention to detail and service quality
Possesses basic math skills for handling payments
Is fluent in written and oral English
What we offer
Competitive salary and benefits
A welcoming and dynamic working environment
Opportunities for professional growth
Accommodation (resorts only) and meals provided
