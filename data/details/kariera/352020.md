With exceptional destinations including APHI AURA and APHI MIRA in Rhodes, and APHI KALLI in Crete, APHI RESORTS brings together authentic hospitality and inspiring guest experiences. As part of the Wavemaker Hospitality portfolio, alongside Atlantica Hotels & Resorts, Mare Resorts, Aphrodite Hills Resort, and AMOH, a Luxury Collection Resort, Rhodes, APHI RESORTS builds on a strong hospitality heritage while introducing a fresh approach to the guest experience.

APHI AURA located in Kolymbia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the
Head Bartender
.
POSITION SUMMARY:
As a
Head Bartender
, you play a crucial role in creating a positive and enjoyable experience for our guests. Your responsibilities extend beyond simply mixing drinks; you are a hospitality professional and a customer service expert.
Responsibilities
Craft and serve a diverse range of beverages, from classic cocktails to custom creations, ensuring precision and quality in every pour.
Maintain a deep knowledge of drink recipes, garnishes, and presentation techniques.
Provide exceptional customer service by engaging with patrons, taking drink orders, and making recommendations based on preferences.
Create a welcoming and friendly atmosphere, fostering positive interactions with guests.
Manage multiple orders simultaneously while maintaining a high level of customer satisfaction.
Adhere to hygiene and safety standards, including the proper handling of glassware and bar equipment.
Stay abreast of industry trends and customer preferences to keep our offerings fresh and exciting.
Collaborate with other staff members to ensure a seamless and coordinated service experience.
Adhere to all relevant laws and regulations related to the service of alcoholic beverages.
Requirements
2 years of experience a bartender
Extensive knowledge of beverages, including industry trends and best practices
Strong communication, and interpersonal skills
Ability to work under pressure and handle multiple tasks simultaneously
Excellent organizational and time management skills, with the ability to prioritize tasks effectively
Fluent in English
Benefits:
Salary package and other benefits (as per local legislation)
Advanced opportunities for professional growth
Accommodation (if necessary) and board
Participation in educational programs & trainings
Diverse and multicultural environments
Professional skills development
