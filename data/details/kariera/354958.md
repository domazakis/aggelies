Job Description:
We are currently seeking a
Project Manager
to join our Strategy & Performance team in Athens.
Your role will encompass:
Ensure all projects are delivered on time, within scope, and budget.
Perform end-to-end project planning, monitoring, budget/resource/risk/change management.
Contribute to the development and communication of business requirements with clear scope and deliverables.
Coordinate internal teams and third-party vendors for the flawless execution of multiple initiatives.
Oversee daily project operations and resolve issues to maintain timelines and stakeholder alignment.
Report on project progress, risks, and escalate key issues to management when required.
What you bring to the role:
Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
Master's degree is a plus.
5+ years of experience in project management.
Experience in the energy sector or IT/technology projects is a strong advantage.
Excellent communication skills in English (written and verbal).
PMP certification or formal training is highly desirable.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite.
Share the same Value-Based Behaviors:
People & Personal growth
Results orientation & Safety excellence
Collaboration & Trust
Empathy & Effective communication
Flexibility & Adaptability
