1. JOB OBJECTIVE:
Own local marketing strategy, using a combination of exceptional leadership skills, understanding of local insights and the ability to meet the needs of diverse customer and client base. Someone to continue to bring ASICS brand to the next level in Greece while maintaining the highest of standards.
2. KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES:
Description
Be the Marketing lead on the ground/in the country/ in the office and provide guidance to the team as appropriate. Be the spokesperson and main point of contact for the Marketing teams, but also for customers, agencies etc. externally.
Team leadership and development incl. Digital Marketing, TM, SM and Training&Education
Ensure all local activations are on-brand and deliver business results.
Lead on the execution of marketing strategy, while managing budgets.
Build compelling cross-category marketing strategies and MTP in line with overall ASICS brand strategy
Produce annual marketing plans for direct-to-consumer, wholesale initiatives.
Build plans that account for local marketing insights, opportunities and challenges.
Be the marketing lead on the ground and lead our team appropriately.
Work closely with the commercial team in Greece and the EM Marketing Team in Amsterdam to ensure state of the art Marketing executions.
Performance Indicators
Preference.
Brand metrics (sound mind sound body association, brand association).
Employee engagement.
Budget Management.
3. COMMUNICATIONS & WORKING RELATIONSHIPS:
Internal:
Head of Sales, GM Greece, all Marketing functions, Merchandising, HR, Head of EM Marketing in AMS
External:
Digital agencies, PR agencies, Event agencies, runners, athletes, suppliers.
