About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in
Mykonos, Greece
, scheduled for
May 2027,
and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the
debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand
, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality
is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What You Will Do:
Support the restaurant service team in delivering a seamless and refined dining experience.
Set, clear and reset tables efficiently according to restaurant service standards.
Replenish tableware, linen, water and service supplies discreetly.
Maintain immaculate cleanliness and organization of the dining room and service stations.
Assist waitstaff with food and beverage service when required.
Coordinate with colleagues to ensure smooth table turnover without compromising guest comfort.
Follow hygiene, safety and handling procedures at all times.
What You Need to Bring:
Previous experience in restaurant service is preferred; luxury hospitality exposure is an advantage.
Basic knowledge of table settings, service etiquette and hygiene standards.
Good English communication skills and willingness to learn.
Positive attitude, reliability and strong teamwork skills.
Attention to detail and ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
Professional presentation and a genuine interest in premium hospitality.
