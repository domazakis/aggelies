APHI MIRA (ex Miramare) located in Ixia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the Food and Beverage Manager.
POSITION SUMMARY:
As a Food and Beverage Manager, you will play a crucial role in overseeing our culinary and service operations maintaining the highest standards of quality, service, and guest satisfaction. You'll be the driving force behind creating a welcoming atmosphere that exceeds guest expectations, from the first impression to the last bite.
Key job responsibilities:
Overall Management of the F&B Department following the Financial & Quality targets, directives and standards as set by the company, including the following:
F&B budget preparation and follow up.
F&B Quality & Financialresults follow up and reporting.
Menu planning in cooperation with Chef & Bar Manager and following the specifications of the product.
F&B Stock and Purchasing control.
Organization and follow up of Hotel F&B services, F&B outlets and functions.
Organization, management and follow up on culinary events.
Close cooperation with the Hotel Controller for F&B menus costing and F&B control procedures.
F&B staff hiring, training, development and supervision following the brand’s standards
Supervision of monthly F&B stock takings.
F&B sales promotion and product development.
Make sure that quality systems & procedures run smoothly (HACCP, ISO 9001, and H&S).
Be present during restaurant service hours to control service and PR with clients.
Duty management shifts.
Be present at daily & weekly Management meetings and management cocktail.
Control of F&B staff weekly schedules/productivity
Any other matter related to the maximization of Financial & Quality results of his department
Requirements:
Hotel Management Diploma
5 years’ experience in the Hotel Industry in luxury property, 3 years’ experience in similar positions.
Fluent English, both written and spoken. Any other language is an advantage.
Good computer knowledge
Be trained and have relevant knowledge for implementation of the following management systems: ISO 9001;14001; 22000& OSHAS
Culinary knowledge
Good organizational and management skills.
Be able to work under pressure, with time limits and long hours.
Good PR skills
Decision maker
Good communication skills
Good negotiating and selling skills.
Be able to incite and lead the staff.
Benefits:
Salary package and other benefits (as per local legislation)
Advanced opportunities for professional growth
Accommodation (if necessary) and board
Participation in educational programs & trainings
Diverse and multicultural environments
Professional skills development
