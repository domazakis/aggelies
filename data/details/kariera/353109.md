During this period, we seek to recruit a professional for the following position
:
Trade Marketing Supervisor - ON Trade
for our Marketing Department
based in our premises at Kifisia
As a Trade Marketing Supervisor - ON Trade, reporting directly to the Trade  Marketing Manager, you will be responsible for implementing the agreed Trade Marketing plan in order to increase sales and brand equity in the distribution channels.
What you will be doing:
Participate in the development & implementation of Trade Marketing Plan
Monitoring and expenditure checking according to Trade Marketing A&P budget on a quarterly and Monthly basis , proceeding with the necessary adjustments according to volume changes and report to Finance.
Sales visits with Sales Reps in order to ensure products’ visibility, Merchandising Standards implementation and best practices per distribution channel and store type
Implementation of tailor made promotional activities with new generation POS material
Monitor market trends and competitors actions
Monitor and Complete Monthly activity sales calendar
Responsible for POS ordering, allocation, monitors POS warehouse stocks
Creation of POS material: market research, collection and evaluation of proposals, contacts with suppliers, keep electronic record
Analyze data and steer trade marketing activities towards the drivers that will impact sales per individual trade channel
Manages the point of sales material (including budget, logistics, distribution, assortment) by finding the most efficient solutions
Create evaluation reports based on collected data
Organizing and implementing consumer promotion activities in ON Trade Channel
POS Champion in Carlsberg Group with more responsibilities about POS materials (ex: run Tender through Ariba for POS materials, participate and align in monthly meetings with rest POS Champions, etc)
What we are looking for
BSc in Business Administration/Marketing/Economics

Proficient in MS Office; especially in excel
Fluency in Greek and English (written and spoken)
Highly developed communication & presentation skills
Analytical thinking with the ability to synthesize complex information into actionable insights
Results driven mindset
