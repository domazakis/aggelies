We at Nova, a very young and dynamic team, the 2nd largest telecom provider in Greece, are looking for a motivated
CVM Senior Product Manager
to join our B2B CRM team and support the design and execution of customer value management initiatives.
You have a challenging mission to:
Design and optimize data-driven CRM/CVM campaigns that enhance SME customer lifecycle value, drive growth and retention, and translate customer insights into measurable business impact.
What will you do:
Design, develop, and execute end-to-end CRM/CVM campaigns aligned with business objectives, including audience selection, offer setup, channel selection, and scheduling.
Develop Business customer segmentation and target strategies based on customer behavior, industry, and commercial opportunities.
Support SME customer lifecycle activities including acquisition, retention, upsell, cross-sell, churn prevention, and revenue growth initiatives.
Continuously optimize campaigns and customer segmentation strategies based on business results and customer behavior insights.
Analyze large customer datasets using IBM SPSS Modeler or other analytical/data mining tools to identify insights and optimization opportunities.
Monitor campaign performance, track KPIs, and prepare regular and ad hoc reports.
Collaborate closely with Product, Analytics, Sales, Finance, and IT teams to ensure smooth campaign implementation and alignment with business goals.
Stay up to date with industry trends, CRM best practices, and developments in telecommunications and customer analytics.
It would be nice if you have:
3-5 years of experience in CRM/CVM, Analytics, Marketing, Finance, or a related field.
Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Marketing, Finance, Computer Science, Business, or a related discipline.
Strong analytical, business understanding, reporting, and communication skills.
Experience in data analysis or data mining tools (IBM SPSS Modeler knowledge is a plus).
Ability to analyze large datasets and translate customer insights into actionable business recommendations.
Knowledge of B2B/SME customer lifecycle management and commercial campaigns.
Ability to identify growth opportunities and support revenue-generating initiatives within the SME segment.
Experience in customer segmentation and value management strategies is considered a plus.
Results-oriented mindset and ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
Very good command of the English language.
Collaborative team player.
Experience in telecommunications and familiarity with SQL or CRM/campaign management tools are highly appreciated
What will you get:
Wellness programs to keep yourself fit.
Mobile voice and data to talk.
Discounts on Nova Fixed / Mobile / TV for you, friends, family.
Flexy work from home to balance your life.
Private Insurance program to check up on your health.
