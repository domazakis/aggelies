Job requirements:
Minimum 1–3 years of experience in a professional kitchen; previous experience in a luxury hotel, resort, or restaurant is an advantage.
Basic culinary knowledge and a genuine passion for cooking and hospitality.
Strong willingness to learn, develop, and build professional culinary skills.
Basic understanding of food safety, hygiene, and HACCP procedures.
Ability to follow recipes, instructions, and established kitchen standards accurately.
Ability to work effectively as part of a professional and multicultural kitchen team.
Good organizational skills and attention to cleanliness and detail.
Positive, reliable, and professional attitude with a strong work ethic.
Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced kitchen environment and during busy service periods.
Flexibility to work shifts, weekends, and holidays according to operational requirements.
Previous experience with Mediterranean or Greek cuisine is an advantage.
Good English communication skills are preferred.
Job responsibilities:
Assist the Chef de Partie and Demi Chef de Partie with the daily preparation and operation of the assigned kitchen section.
Prepare ingredients and basic preparations according to recipes and instructions.
Ensure all mise en place is completed accurately and on time for each service.
Assist with the preparation, cooking, and presentation of dishes according to established standards.
Follow recipes, cooking techniques, portion sizes, and presentation guidelines.
Maintain excellent standards of cleanliness, hygiene, and organization throughout the kitchen.
Handle, label, store, and rotate ingredients correctly to maintain freshness and quality.
Follow all HACCP, food safety, hygiene, and allergen procedures at all times.
Assist with receiving, storing, and organizing food products when required.
Support the kitchen team during busy services, special events, and pre-opening activities.
Help minimize food waste through proper preparation, storage, and portion control.
Clean and maintain workstations, utensils, equipment, and kitchen areas after service.
Communicate effectively with the Chef de Partie and other members of the kitchen team.
Continuously develop culinary knowledge and technical skills through training and hands-on experience.
Contribute to a positive kitchen environment based on teamwork, respect, professionalism, and authentic hospitality.
