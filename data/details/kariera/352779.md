Reservations Manager
About the role
As a
Reservations Manager
, you will lead the Reservations team, ensuring every guest interaction begins with seamless, professional, and personalized booking experience. Combining leadership, commercial awareness, and operational excellence, you will oversee reservation processes, optimize occupancy and revenue opportunities, and maintain the highest service standards across our hotels.
Working closely with Front Office, Sales, Revenue Management, and Hotel Operations teams, you will play a key role in driving business performance while delivering exceptional guest experiences.
Life Inside the Hotel Group
Every reservation represents an opportunity to create memorable guest experiences while supporting the commercial success of our hotels. Some days involve forecasting demand, monitoring booking trends, and optimizing inventory. Others require leading your team through peak booking periods, coordinating with multiple departments, and responding quickly to changing business needs.
Within this dynamic environment, your leadership, analytical thinking, and commitment to service excellence will make a meaningful impact.
What you'll do
Lead, coach, and develop the Reservations team to deliver exceptional service and achieve departmental objectives
Oversee daily reservation operations, ensuring accuracy, efficiency, and compliance with company standards
Monitor room inventory, availability, and booking pace in collaboration with Revenue Management
Maximize room revenue through effective inventory control, upselling strategies, and rate optimization
Coordinate closely with Sales, Front Office, Revenue Management, and Hotel Operations to ensure seamless guest experiences
Manage group reservations, allotments, and special requests while ensuring operational readiness
Monitor reservation systems, distribution channels, and booking platforms to ensure data accuracy
Analyze reservation trends, prepare reports, and identify opportunities to improve performance
Handle complex guest requests, escalations, and reservation-related issues professionally
Ensure compliance with company policies, data protection regulations, and quality standards
Your Uniqueness Is Welcome
We believe outstanding Reservations Managers combine strong leadership with commercial insight and a genuine passion for hospitality. Beyond managing systems and processes, you inspire your team, build strong cross-functional relationships, and ensure every guest's journey begins with confidence.
If you enjoy leading people, optimizing performance, and delivering exceptional service, you'll feel right at home with us.
We're looking for someone who:
Has previous experience in a Reservations Supervisor or Reservations Manager role within the hospitality industry
Demonstrates strong leadership, coaching, and team management skills
Has excellent communication and interpersonal abilities
Possesses strong analytical and organizational skills with great attention to detail
Has experience working with Property Management Systems (PMS), Channel Managers, CRS, and MS Office
Understands revenue management principles and hotel distribution channels
Thrives in a fast-paced, results-driven environment
Works collaboratively across multiple departments
Has an excellent command of the English language; additional languages will be considered an asset
