The Marketing team at
kariera.gr
is looking for a new member to join our team as a Marketing Coordinator.
As we continue to grow, we’re looking for a proactive and creative marketing professional who is ready to contribute to our brand, campaigns, events, and digital presence.
If you’re passionate about marketing, enjoy working across different projects and teams, and want to make an impact in a dynamic environment, we’d love to hear from you.
Key Responsibilities:
• Executing and optimizing email marketing campaigns to engage and nurture leads.
• Ownership of the social media calendar and content across multiple channels.
• Commercial support, including sales presentations and reports and coordination
client marketing projects.
• Support branding and marketing initiatives across departments.
• Support the promotion and marketing plan of B2B & B2C events.
• Interacting with Greek universities and Youth organizations.
• Coordinating with internal teams and providing detailed briefs to creative and
performance agencies for effective campaign creation.
• Collaborate with external agencies for PR activities, including press releases and
media interviews.
• Handle day-to-day marketing administration and internal requests.
What we're looking for:
• 2+ years of experience in Marketing.
• Strong organizational and project coordination skills.
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills for collaboration with internal
teams, clients and external partners.
• Good understanding of digital marketing, email marketing and social media.
• Strong attention to detail and ability to manage deadlines.
• Proactive, hands-on mindset with a willingness to get involved across different
areas of Marketing.
• Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced and collaborative environment.
• Eager to stay updated on digital marketing trends to bring fresh ideas to the team.
What we offer:
• Competitive salary
• Private medical insurance
• Hybrid working model
• Breakfast, fresh fruit, snacks & great coffee/tea/chamomile
