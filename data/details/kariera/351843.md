We are currently looking for a Lease Specialist to join our fantastic team!
*** Hybrid Mode (Permanent residency in Athens is a prerequisite for this position)
About the Role:
We are looking for a Lease Specialist to join our Expansion team and support the management of our retail property portfolio
Key Responsibilities
Coordinate the preparation of lease contracts and verify their completeness in order to sign a contract
Prepare documents related to lease agreements - letters, amendments, corporate guarantees, bank guarantees, prolongations
Ensure the implementation of the provisions under the lease agreement
Monitor the costs associated with the lease contracts
Communicate with business partners and service providers
Prepare analyses, financial reports, statements, and presentations to provide the data needed to monitor and improve the implementation of the expansion process
We are looking for a professional who possesses:
Previous experience in lease administration, real estate, property management, or a similar role
Good understanding of commercial lease agreements and related processes
Strong organizational, analytical, and negotiation skills
Excellent communication skills and attention to detail
Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Excel.
Excellent command of English
IT skills: MS Office (Advanced knowledge of MS Excel functions), ERP systems
Proficiency in preparation of presentations, reports, and analysis
The ideal candidate is proactive, detail-oriented, and comfortable managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced retail environment
We offer:
A dynamic and collaborative work environment that supports professional growth
Competitive compensation and benefits package
Opportunities for continuous learning and career development
Exposure to strategic projects and cross-functional collaboration
A culture that values safety, quality, and continuous improvement
