About the Role
You will report to our Operations Director (COO) and lead multiple customer implementation projects at the same time — owning scope, timeline, risk and delivery. You are the bridge between customers, Operations, Product and Development: turning business needs into working solutions, driving successful Go-Lives, and mentoring junior consultants.
It is a hands-on, high-ownership role in a small, senior team where everyone steps into whatever operational gap needs owning.
What You’ll Be Doing
Lead multiple implementation projects end-to-end — own scope, timeline, risks and deliverables
Run discovery; gather and analyse business requirements and translate them into functional solutions and implementation workflows
Coordinate customer meetings and steering committees, keeping stakeholders aligned, both internal and external
Plan and execute successful Go-Lives, then manage post-Go-Live stabilization and customer adoption
Coordinate across teams — Sales handovers, Product on functionality gaps, Development during execution, and a clean handover to Support
Mentor junior consultants and coordinate project squads
Build trusted customer relationships and advise on best practices
