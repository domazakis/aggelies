We are looking for a multi-tasking person with excellent communication skills and an upbeat attitude.
Qualifications
Degree in Business Administration / Communication / HR and/or other relevant expertise
Experience in personnel management - preferably in the tourism event sector or in the cruise industry
Strong communication, people skills and understanding of service and customer orientation
Organizational strength, decision-making skills
Excellent Computer Skills (Windows environment, Microsoft Word, Excel, Power Point, Project Management Tools)
Excellent English knowledge (written & spoken)
Enthusiasm as well as joy in learning new things
Willingness to travel
Knowledge of structures/operations of theaters /shows and understanding of creative work would be an advantage
Knowledge of Vessel Management & Maritime Business would be an advantage
Knowledge of required seafarers’ documentation would be an advantage
Experience in working and interacting with performers, technicians and managers in the creative sector would be an advantage
German language would be an advantage
Responsibilities
Reports directly to the Entertainment sea chefs Office in Berlin for all administrative tasks
Works in cooperation with Entertainment Recruiting, Casting and Travelling Managers – link between the Cyprus office, Berlin and Athens office.
First point of contact for Entertainment Crew and their agents in preparation of documents
Keeps track of the entertainment employee pool, updating and verifying information
Takes over the responsibility of contract offers and contract booking of all entertainment employees MS fleet
Monitors rotation, communications contract changes, provides timely notification and handles administrative processing – including Documentation and Reporting
Handles fee negotiation, reimbursement requests, visa requirements, questions about flights, payslips or document requirements
Schedules and organizes feedback meetings and briefings
Occasionally undertakes business trips to the Berlin office or to the ships
Is aware, implements and complies to Company’s Integrated Management System (IMS)
Competencies
Flexibility, organising ability and time management skills
Systematic, consistent, professional and career orientated
Ability to work under pressure
Able to motivate crew
Able to work independently and as part of a team within specific time constraints
Flexibility and organizing ability
Poise, very good manners
Tenacity, efficiency, commitment
Show dedication and full commitment to the tasks at hand
Desire to grow and develop within the Company
