About ERMA TECH Group
ERMA TECH Group is a global leader in sustainable technology, providing advanced solutions that support environmental protection and maritime sustainability. We are committed to helping the shipping industry reduce its environmental impact through innovative technologies that enhance operational efficiency and ensure compliance with international regulations.
Driven by engineering excellence, innovation, and a strong customer focus, we combine technical expertise with family values and a long-term business vision. Our international team of professionals works together to develop and deliver reliable, future-ready solutions for the maritime industry.
When you join ERMA TECH Group, you become part of a team of thinkers and innovators who are shaping the future of sustainable shipping.
About the Role
We are looking for a
Project Engineer
to join ERMA FIRST ESK Engineering Solutions S.A. and support the technical coordination and execution of projects within the maritime industry.
This role combines project management, engineering, and customer interaction. The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating project activities, reviewing technical documentation, monitoring project progress, and ensuring that technical requirements and deliverables are met throughout the project lifecycle.
We are looking for an engineer with a structured and proactive approach, strong problem-solving skills, and the ability to coordinate multiple projects while working closely with internal teams and external stakeholders.
Key Responsibilities
Plan, coordinate, and monitor project schedules, milestones, deliverables, and budgets.
Coordinate project stakeholders and maintain effective professional relationships with customers, suppliers, and internal teams.
Prepare, organize, and maintain comprehensive project documentation and records.
Review technical documentation, engineering drawings, specifications, and other project deliverables to ensure compliance with technical requirements.
Contribute to engineering activities and the technical implementation of projects.
Respond to customers' technical queries and coordinate with relevant engineering teams to provide accurate and timely solutions.
Monitor project progress and provide regular status updates to the Project Management Team.
Identify, assess, and manage technical and operational risks, project deviations, and potential delays, proposing appropriate corrective actions and contingency plans.
Coordinate with cross-functional teams to resolve technical issues and ensure the timely completion of project activities.
Manage multiple projects in parallel, ensuring alignment with agreed schedules, quality standards, and customer expectations.
Job Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Engineering is essential, ideally in Naval Architecture or Marine Engineering, or Mechanical Engineering.
At least
3 years of relevant professional experience
in project engineering, project coordination, or a similar role.
Experience in the marine, maritime, or shipbuilding industry will be considered an advantage.
Ability to understand and review engineering drawings, technical specifications, and project documentation.
Strong technical, analytical, and organizational skills.
Excellent problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
Strong planning and coordination skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and multiple projects simultaneously.
Excellent command of written and spoken English.
Good knowledge of MS Office applications.
Experience with AutoCAD and/or 3D modelling software will be considered an advantage.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with a collaborative, team-oriented mindset.
Willingness and availability to travel when required.
