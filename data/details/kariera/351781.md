About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Coordinate and monitor Housekeeping guest requests, ensuring timely follow-up and completion.
Support the daily operation of the Housekeeping department in close collaboration with Supervisors and Management.
Monitor arrival, departure and stayover room status, supporting the efficient allocation of Room Attendant assignments.
Coordinate Day and Turndown Service, ensuring guest preferences and service timings are respected.
Follow up on Engineering and maintenance requests affecting guestrooms and Housekeeping operations.
Prepare and update daily Housekeeping briefings, reports and operational tracking tools.
Maintain accurate guest preferences and Housekeeping-related information in Opera.
Monitor staffing levels and communicate operational needs or potential challenges to Housekeeping Management.
Handle telephone calls and communication with guests and colleagues in a professional, courteous and timely manner.
Ensure smooth communication and coordination between Housekeeping and other hotel departments to deliver a seamless guest experience.
What you need to bring:
Previous experience in Housekeeping or a similar operational role, preferably in a luxury hotel.
Degree or diploma in Hospitality, Tourism or a related field will be considered an asset.
Excellent command of English; additional languages are an advantage.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills with a guest-focused mindset.
Strong organizational and coordination skills with excellent attention to detail.
