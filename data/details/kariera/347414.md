Key Responsibilities
Oversees the daily operations of the Kids Club, ensuring a safe, welcoming, and well-organized environment for all children.
Supervises and ensures all team members deliver the activity programs and follow the standards, training, and safety guidelines according to the company standards.
Monitors attendance, schedules, and resources to ensure efficient operation and a consistent guest experience.
Handles parents’ inquiries and feedback with professionalism, warmth, and attention to detail.
Maintains high standards of cleanliness, safety, and equipment maintenance within the Kids Club area.
Supports the HR team in staff onboarding, scheduling, and performance reviews related to the Kids Club.
Requirements
Previous experience in a supervisory or managerial role within Kids Clubs, childcare programs, or family-focused hospitality environments, ideally in 5* resorts.
Excellent leadership, organizational, and communication skills.
Fluent in English; additional languages (such as German, French, or Italian) will be considered an asset.
Strong understanding of child development, educational activities, and creative play.
Energetic, empathetic, and service-oriented personality with genuine passion for creating memorable experiences for children and families.
First Aid/CPR certification or equivalent training preferred.
