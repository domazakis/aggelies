About the role
As a
Bartender
, you will be a key part of our guests’ social experience, crafting exceptional drinks while delivering warm, engaging, and professional service. Your role combines technical skill, creativity, and hospitality, turning every interaction at the bar into a memorable moment.
From classic cocktails to innovative creations, you will help shape the atmosphere and energy of one of the hotel’s most vibrant spaces.
Life Inside the Hotel Group
Some shifts are energetic and fast-paced, with orders flowing and conversations filling the bar. Others allow space for creativity refining recipes, perfecting presentation, and connecting with guests.
Within this rhythm, your consistency, attention to detail, and positive attitude ensure every drink meets our quality standards and every guest feels welcome.
What you’ll do
Prepare and serve high-quality beverages, including cocktails, mocktails, and specialty drinks
Deliver friendly, attentive, and professional service to all guests
Maintain cleanliness, organization, and proper stocking of the bar area
Assist in developing new drink recipes and contributing to menu innovation
Handle guest inquiries and special requests with efficiency and enthusiasm
Follow health, safety, and hygiene standards at all times
Collaborate with the bar team to ensure smooth daily operations
Support the overall guest experience with a service-oriented mindset
Your uniqueness is welcome
Great bartenders do more than mix drinks they set the tone. We value individuals who bring personality, passion, and pride in their craft, while working seamlessly as part of a team.
We’re looking for someone who:
Has previous experience as a
Bartender
in a high-end hotel, resort, or hospitality environment
Possesses strong knowledge of mixology, spirits, and beverage preparation
Demonstrates excellent communication and customer service skills
Can work efficiently in a fast-paced environment while maintaining attention to detail
Is a team player with a positive attitude and passion for hospitality
Is professional, reliable, and guest-focused
Why join us?
A supportive and dynamic working environment
Opportunities for career growth and development
Competitive salary and benefits package
