Job requirements:
Minimum 7 years of proven experience in Food & Beverage, with previous experience as a Floor Manager, Restaurant Supervisor, Assistant Restaurant Manager, or similar leadership position within a luxury hotel, fine dining restaurant, or high end hospitality environment.
Proven experience managing and coordinating a professional service team in a fast paced, guest focused environment.
Strong knowledge of luxury and fine dining service standards, restaurant operations, guest relations, and service procedures.
Strong knowledge of wine and wine service, including grape varieties, regions, styles, food pairing, and professional serving techniques. Sommelier certification or formal wine training is considered an advantage.
Excellent leadership, organizational, communication, and problem solving skills, with the ability to manage the restaurant floor effectively.
Strong knowledge of food and beverage offerings, including menu ingredients, preparation methods, wines, cocktails, and other beverages.
Confident guest engagement and suggestive selling skills, with the ability to anticipate guest needs and deliver highly personalized experiences.
Ability to lead by example, motivate team members, and maintain high standards throughout every stage of service.
Fluency in English is required, additional languages are considered an asset.
Strong commercial awareness, including an understanding of revenue generation, upselling, service efficiency, and operational costs.
Positive attitude, professional demeanor, discretion, and a genuine passion for luxury hospitality.
Comfortable working across two distinct restaurant environments, including the unique Caldera setting, which involves frequent walking and navigating stairs between different levels and service areas and our Blu restaurant.
Job responsibilities:
Oversee and support the restaurant floor operations across both our upcoming luxury Caldera property and Blu Restaurant, ensuring consistency in service excellence, guest experience, and operational standards.
Lead, supervise, and coordinate the front of house teams, adapting leadership and service approach to the individual character and operational needs of each venue.
Ensure seamless and consistently elevated service throughout each shift, from guest arrival to departure.
Conduct pre-service briefings and communicate daily operations, reservations, VIP guests, special occasions, guest preferences, menu changes, and operational priorities.
Coordinate closely with all departments across both venues.
Maintain a visible presence on the floor and proactively anticipate guest needs, preferences, and special requests.
Monitor service timing, table presentation, staff performance, cleanliness, and overall guest satisfaction.
Support and oversee wine service, assisting guests and the service team with wine selections, pairings, and professional service, where applicable.
Maintain comprehensive knowledge of the menus, wine lists, cocktails, beverages, ingredients, allergens, and relevant service standards of both venues.
Drive appropriate suggestive selling and upselling initiatives while ensuring recommendations remain genuine and guest centric.
Monitor table turnover, reservations, seating flow, service pacing, and restaurant capacity to optimize both guest experience and operational efficiency.
Coordinate the floor operation efficiently across the unique Caldera layout, ensuring effective communication and smooth movement between different levels and service areas.
Monitor staffing levels and section allocations and make adjustments according to business levels and operational requirements at each venue.
Coach, mentor, and develop Captains, Head Waiters, Waiters, and other front-of house team members, promoting consistent service standards across the group.
Support training and onboarding, ensuring team members understand and consistently deliver the group's luxury hospitality standards.
Handle guest feedback, complaints, and service recovery with professionalism, discretion, and sound judgment.
Monitor operational supplies, equipment, tableware, glassware, and other service requirements and communicate replenishment needs.
Work closely with management to support revenue objectives, control operational costs, minimize waste, and maximize service efficiency.
Ensure compliance with all health, safety, hygiene, responsible alcohol service, and company policies.
Oversee opening, daily, and closing procedures and ensure each venue is fully prepared for every service.
Contribute to the continuous development and alignment of F&B service standards across the group's restaurant portfolio.
Foster a positive, professional, and collaborative team culture that reflects the values and standards of the group.
Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned by the Food & Beverage leadership.
