We at
Nova
, a dynamic and constantly evolving media and telecom organization, are looking for a
Fixed Product Manager
to join our
Fixed & TV team
.
You have a challenging mission to:
Own the strategy, development, and commercial performance of Fixed products, leveraging customer, market, and competitor insights. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to drive the product roadmap, propositions, GTM initiatives, and new product development, with a focus on growth, profitability, and customer experience.
What will you do:
Contribute to the development and maintain Fixed products roadmap, manage pricing decisions to ensure company's profitability and meet customer's needs
Propose & implement commercial objectives and propositions strategies, in line with objectives and underpinned by customer, market & competitor insights
Monitor closely and analyze market trends, and competition; manage the insights and market analysis/research that drives all product efforts and commercial decisions
Monitor and analyze business and financial KPIs, securing performance and profitability
Be the business owner for driving proposition changes working closely with product, technology, billing and operational teams
Work closely with commercial & finance teams to build business cases, considering both broader business and bottom-line financial benefits and risks
Design and execute regional campaigns focusing on growth and high utilization of FTTH networks in designated areas of interest
Collaborate with marketing teams on Go To Market plans and briefs for products and propositions changes and how they are delivered
Follow-up and cooperate with all teams for the implementation of product development projects, marketing initiatives, GTM plans and promos, providing clarifications and supporting the delivery upon commitment
Develop, test and deliver alternative concepts and identify opportunities for growth
Support product development teams with prioritization and development of roadmap, ensuring delivery of commercial and proposition objectives
Monitor customer satisfaction indexes and identify areas of improvement, propose and implement corrective actions in a timely manner
Identify customer needs, propose and lead new products development to meet customer needs better than the competition
It would be nice if you have:
BSc in Business Administration, Marketing, Engineering or Science departments
Postgraduate studies in Marketing Business fields will be considered a strong plus
At least 3 years of experience in the marketing field and especially in product management, preferably in Telecoms
Creativity, efficiency, and attention to detail
Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail and proactiveness.
Strong time-management skills; Ability to work under pressure and adapt within a dynamic environment
Flexibility and accountability, with a "can-do" approach
Creativity and problem-solving skills
Team-player skills, enjoying working collaboratively across functions
Strong analytical & commercial thinking
Excellent communication and presentation skills, oral and written, in Greek and in English
Advanced knowledge of the MS Office suite
What you will get:
Wellness programs to keep yourself fit.
Mobile voice and data to talk.
Discounts on Nova Fixed / Mobile / TV for you, friends, family.
Flexy work from home to balance your life.
Private Insurance program to check up on your health.
Toddlers allowance to daycare your kids.
