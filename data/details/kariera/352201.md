About the Role
Are you passionate about creating unforgettable hospitality experiences? Do you thrive in a fast-paced environment where leadership and innovation go hand in hand?
We’re seeking for a
Food & Beverage Manager
who will take charge of all F&B operations, inspire teams, deliver exceptional service and elevate our F&B operations to the next level.
Key Responsibilities:
Lead, train and motivate a dynamic F&B team to achieve excellence.
Ensure smooth operations across all outlets with a hands-on approach.
Drive quality, safety, and sustainability standards (HACCP, ISO).
Manage budgets, control costs, and optimize resources.
Monitor and analyze financial performance indicators to maximize profitability and operational efficiency.
Collaborate with Chefs and other departments to craft unique guest experiences.
Be the go-to person for guest interactions and quick problem-solving.
Innovate and implement best practices to keep us ahead of the curve.
Foster a collaborative and participatory work environment that encourages teamwork.
Qualifications:
Degree in Tourism or Business Administration.
Knowledge of ISO food safety & hygiene standards (certification preferred).
Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar F&B management position within a 5* hotel.
Ability to manage budgets and analyze financial data.
Familiarity with F&B management software and MS Office applications.
Fluent in English (a second language is a plus).
Strong leadership and communication skills.
Problem-solving, decision making and a drive for process improvement.
Customer-focused with a passion for hospitality.
Why Join Us?:
Competitive salary and career growth opportunities within a rapidly expanding hospitality group.
Accommodation and meals provided, if necessary.
A supportive work environment that values its people, the planet, and the local community.
All applications and submitted information will be treated with strict confidentiality.
