About PM-PARTNERS
PM-PARTNERS is a project management consulting firm, founded in 1995, specializing in Portfolio, Programme and Project Management Services. We support complex and high-demanding projects across the Energy, Industry, Infrastructure and Financial Services sectors, combining structured project controls with hands-on delivery support.
Overview of the role
We are looking for an experienced Scheduling Specialist / Planner to join our team and support a portfolio of major construction, energy and industrial projects.
The role will focus on integrated planning and scheduling, progress measurement, critical path analysis, contractor schedule review and management reporting, working closely with Project Managers, PMO teams, consultants and contractors.
Key responsibilities
Develop and maintain baseline, detailed and integrated project schedules, together with schedule basis documents, WBS structures, coding, milestones and calendars.
Translate engineering, procurement, construction, testing and commissioning scope into robust CPM schedule logic and realistic activity sequences.
Integrate schedules from multiple contractors and disciplines into the overall master schedule and ensure consistent interfaces, assumptions and dependencies.
Update schedules using verified actual progress, evaluate forecasts, logic, float, constraints and critical / near-critical paths, and identify emerging schedule risks.
Review and challenge Contractor schedules, including logic integrity, constructability, resource loading, productivity assumptions, sequencing and compliance with contractual milestones.
Prepare look-ahead schedules, progress curves, S-curves, schedule KPIs, trend reports and concise management dashboards for project and portfolio reporting.
Perform site walks and coordinate with project teams to validate physical progress and reconcile site status with schedule updates.
Assess delays and changes for schedule impact, support Time Impact Analysis, recovery planning, scenario analysis and evaluation of Extension of Time / claim-related submissions.
Develop and review recovery schedules and corrective-action plans where progress deviates from the approved baseline.
Support Project Managers with resource planning, schedule forecasting, Earned Value / progress measurement and time-cost integration.
Facilitate or participate in schedule review, progress, coordination and constructability meetings, maintaining effective communication with all stakeholders.
Promptly highlight critical deviations, risks and required corrective actions to the Project Manager and PMO.
