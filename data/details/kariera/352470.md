About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in
Mykonos, Greece
, scheduled for
May 2027,
and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the
debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand
, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality
is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What You Will Do:
Deliver attentive, personalized food and beverage service in line with ultra-luxury standards.
Prepare and maintain tables, service stations and mise en place before each service.
Present menus, explain dishes and make thoughtful recommendations based on guest preferences.
Take and accurately communicate orders, observing dietary requirements and allergies.
Coordinate with Culinary and Bar teams to ensure timely and elegant service.
Anticipate guest needs, manage requests discreetly and support service recovery.
Maintain immaculate presentation, cleanliness and compliance with hygiene procedures.
What You Need to Bring:
Previous experience as a Waiter in a luxury hotel, fine dining or high-end restaurant.
Good knowledge of food, beverages, table service and hospitality etiquette.
Excellent command of English; additional languages are an advantage.
Warm, polished communication skills and a naturally guest-focused attitude.
Attention to detail, teamwork and ability to remain composed under pressure.
Flexibility to work varying shifts and commitment to impeccable grooming standards.
