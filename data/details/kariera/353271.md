About the job
Food and Beverage:
Bartender​
Locations:
Greece
: Corfu, Kos, Halkidiki, Crete
Duration:
7 months, between March-November
At Sani/Ikos Group, the
F&B department
is about more than just serving food; it's about creating unforgettable gastronomical experiences. From anticipating guests' preferences to suggesting exquisite culinary delights, every interaction is a chance to make each meal memorable.
Our
F&B Team
focuses on the finest details and ensures a seamless flow of service, taking pride in the smiles of satisfaction we witness every day.
Responsibilities:
Guest Service:
Greet guests warmly, take drink orders, and provide excellent customer service throughout.
Beverage Preparation:
Prepare alcohol and non-alcoholic beverages according to the established sequence of service.
Inventory Management:
Monitor stock levels, restock supplies, and assist with inventory control.
Teamwork:
Collaborate with and management to ensure smooth bar and restaurant operations.
Cleanliness & Safety:
Keep the bar area clean, organized, and compliant with health and safety regulations
About you
You have previous experience working in a bar or restaurant environment, ideally within a hotel setting. You are fluent in English, while knowledge of an additional language is considered an advantage. A degree or diploma in Hospitality or Tourism is a plus, supporting your professional background. You demonstrate excellent communication and customer service skills, creating positive and memorable guest experiences through attentive and professional service.
