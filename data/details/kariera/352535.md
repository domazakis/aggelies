Asics is a Japanese sports company. It was founded in 1949 and the name ASICS, originates from the Latin phrase "Anima Sana in Corpore Sano" which translates into "healthy mind in healthy body"
Asics today is one of the largest sports companies in the world. The excellent know-how, the persistence on detail and the high technology used, result in the design and production of products that meet the highest standards.
The Marketing Manager manages the day to day marketing activities of the organization and long-term marketing strategy for the company.
Duties of the Marketing Manager include:
Execute ASICS's marketing vision and partnership with the strategic accounts to create consumer connection, build the ASICS brand and develop the business.
Manage the annual budget and ensure accuracy and discipline in monthly and quarterly budget management process
Support team members with the day to day marketing tasks and organizing marketing projects and activities.
Organizing the production of branded items such as merchandise POP material and additional marketing communications, such as brochures, flyers and exhibition-related projects.
Co-ordinating marketing campaigns with sales activities.
Creation and publication of all marketing material in line with marketing plans.
Monitor and report on effectiveness of marketing communications.
Maintain effective internal communications to ensure that all relevant company functions are kept informed of marketing objectives.
Marketing Manager Skills and Qualifications:
At least 5 years at a relevant work experience.
Fluency in Greek and English, written and verbal.
Excellent communication skills.
Public Speaking.
Interpersonal Communication.
Familiarity with online content marketing, E-commerce and social media development strategies.
