Are you looking to launch your career in a fast-paced, international business environment?
Do you enjoy collaborating with global clients, solving complex business challenges, and gaining exposure to a wide range of industries?
Optimal, on behalf of its client, a leading insights-on-demand company, is seeking motivated
Global Clients Business Associates
to join their growing team. This role offers a unique opportunity to work closely with international clients, develop commercial acumen, and contribute to high-impact business decisions.
As a Global Clients Business Associate, you will play a key role in managing client relationships, understanding business needs, and delivering tailored insights by connecting organizations with industry experts worldwide.
Key Responsibilities
Understand client needs and project requirements
Identify and evaluate relevant industry experts
Connect experts with clients through curated consultations
Conduct research across different industries and markets
Reach out to potential experts through various communication channels
Build strong skills in sales, negotiation, time management, and professional communication
Requirements
0–3 years of work experience
Bachelor’s degree in any field
Fluency in English
Strong communication and organizational skills
Nice to Have
Strong academic performance
Leadership experience through extracurricular activities
Interest in business, consulting, or market insights
Natural negotiation and relationship-building skills
Growth mindset and motivation for continuous learning

Private medical insurance and savings plans
Health and well-being initiatives and team activities
Opportunities for international mobility across global offices
