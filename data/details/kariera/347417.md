Conrad Corfu
Conrad Corfu, opening in 2027, is the latest addition to the Numo Hotels & Resorts portfolio. More than a place to stay, it’s a destination where authentic Corfiot heritage meets stylish modern living, designed to connect people with culture, nature, and each other.
Key Responsibilities
Organize, coordinate, and supervise daily Spa operations (treatments, reception, facilities).
Ensure safe, high-quality delivery of wellness services in line with brand standards.
Monitor wellness trends and introduce new treatments, practices, and products.
Manage and control Spa budgets, daily transactions, and shift reports.
Implement upselling techniques and revenue-enhancing strategies.
Recruit, train, and evaluate Spa personnel, ensuring service excellence and professional standards
Lead, motivate, and schedule the Spa team (therapists, reception, support staff).
Promote and drive sales of spa products through effective merchandising and staff engagemen
Provide personalized guest service, handle feedback and complaints effectively.
Collaborate with Guest Experience, Marketing, and other departments for guest packages and promotions.
Ensure strict hygiene, cleanliness, and safety standards in Spa facilities.
Maintain accurate records of appointments, stock levels, and professional product usage.
Requirements
3 years minimum experience in similar position in 5-star hotels’
Excellent command of English language, additional foreign languages (preferably German) would be considered an asset
Strong computer skills and excellent knowledge of Microsoft office
Experience in GDS, IDS, OTA’s extranets and Property Management systems, preferably Opera
Excellent communication skills and teamwork spirit
Previous working experience with Hilton Hotels & Resorts is considered an additional asset
