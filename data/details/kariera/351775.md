About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Clean and maintain all assigned public areas, including lobbies, lounges, corridors, elevators and staircases.
Ensure public restrooms are consistently clean, sanitized, well presented and fully stocked.
Maintain assigned back-of-house areas, including staff areas, offices, pantries and restrooms.
Ensure all public spaces are maintained to the hotel’s luxury cleanliness and presentation standards.
Anticipate guest needs and respond promptly and professionally to requests related to cleanliness.
Identify and report maintenance issues, equipment malfunctions and safety hazards.
Follow all health, safety, hygiene and chemical handling procedures.
Maintain a professional appearance and consistently follow the hotel’s grooming and conduct standards.
Contribute to an exceptional guest experience through attention to detail, discretion and genuine care.
What you need to bring:
Previous experience in Housekeeping, cleaning or a similar hospitality role, preferably in a luxury hotel or resort.
High school diploma or equivalent will be considered an asset.
Good understanding of cleaning, hygiene and safety standards.
Strong attention to detail and commitment to maintaining high standards of cleanliness.
Guest-focused mindset with a professional, discreet and helpful approach.
Positive attitude, reliability and a hands-on approach.
