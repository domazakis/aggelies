Senior Project Development Manager, Data Centers
23/06/2026
Job Description:
We are looking for a
Senior Data Center Development Manager
to join our Global Development team and lead the origination, development, and execution of data center projects across Greece and the MEA region.
Your role will encompass:
Identify, assess, and develop new data center opportunities across Greece and MEA
Lead site selection, feasibility studies, due diligence, permitting, and development activities
Build and manage a pipeline of projects aligned with the company's growth strategy
Structure and negotiate key commercial agreements, including land agreements, PPAs, grid connections, partnerships, and customer contracts
Develop business cases integrating data centers with renewable energy solutions (PV and BESS)
Manage stakeholder relationships with hyperscalers, colocation providers, utilities, regulators, investors, and local partners
Coordinate cross-functional teams (Engineering, Legal, Finance, and Asset Management) to ensure successful project execution
Oversee projects through development, financing, and construction readiness while ensuring compliance with technical, environmental, and financial requirements
Monitor market trends and drive innovative solutions related to renewable energy integration, hybrid energy models, and grid optimization
What you bring to the role:
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Energy, Finance, Real Estate, or a related field
8+ years of experience in data center, energy, or large-scale infrastructure project development
Proven track record in delivering complex development projects
Strong understanding of:Data center markets (hyperscalers, colocation, edge computing) - Power infrastructure and grid connectivity - Renewable energy integration (PV and BESS)
Experience working across international markets, preferably within MEA.Strong commercial, negotiation, stakeholder management, and project execution skills
Experience in financial modeling and investment analysis
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, international environment
