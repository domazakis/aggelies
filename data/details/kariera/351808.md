Job requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field.
2-3 years of progressive Human Resources experience.
Working knowledge of employment laws and HR best practices.
Solid understanding of core HR business processes (Recruitment, Onboarding, Benefits, Performance Management) and their automation.
Strong communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills.
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
High level of professionalism and confidentiality.
Proficiency with HRIS systems.
The role requires a willingness to share working time between our two primary locations: Santorini and Athens. The place of employment will be the premises of the Hotel in Santorini during the summer season and at RMKA's Athens offices (Glyfada) during the winter months.
Job responsibilities
Administer and support core HR functions including recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, and employee records management.
Serve as a primary point of contact for employees regarding HR policies, procedures, and benefits.
Support employee relations matters, including investigations, documentation, and resolution of issues.
Coordinate benefits administration, open enrollment, and employee inquiries.
Maintain accurate and confidential employee files and HRIS data.
Assist with training, development, and engagement initiatives.
Prepare HR reports, metrics, and audit documentation as needed.
