Louis Hotels
, one of the biggest hotel groups in Greece and Cyprus, is seeking to recruit an individual for its newest 5-star hotel ‘King Jason Zante’, in
Zakynthos Island, member of Louis Hotels Elegant Collection, for the below position:
Hotel General Manager
Main Responsibilities:
Providing day-to-day leadership and direction to all hotel departments according to our operations standards.
Ensuring full compliance with hotel operating controls, SOP’s, policies, and service standards.
Delivering financial performance as set in company budgets and issuing monthly financial statements.
Management of payroll and other operating expenses.
Budgeting and Revenue Management.
Ensuring proper maintenance of hotel assets.
Creating and maintaining a unique guest experience.
Accommodating special guest requests whenever possible and resolving guest.
Setting team and individual goals.
Developing staff skills and conducting performance evaluations.
Qualifications:
Degree in Hospitality/Tourism.
3 years’ minimum experience as a Hotel Manager in a 5* Hotel.
Strong background in F&B and hotel operations’.
Excellent command of English language is essential; knowledge of a second foreign language will be considered as an asset.
Self-motivated, enthusiastic, guest oriented and with a hands-on operations management approach.
Excellent MS Office, PMS-Computer skills.
