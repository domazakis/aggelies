This is a dynamic, hands-on role combining brand strategy, digital marketing, PR, content, events and strategic partnerships, with the objective of strengthening the hotel's presence across international markets and supporting its commercial goals.
The Marketing Manager will work closely with Management and the Revenue, Sales and Operations teams, as well as with external partners and agencies.
Key Responsibilities
Coordinate and implement the hotel's overall marketing and brand strategy.
Manage and coordinate external partners and agencies across Digital, PR, Creative and Web.
Oversee social media strategy and content across digital channels.
Monitor the performance of digital campaigns (Meta, Google, etc.) and recommend optimization opportunities.
Manage and oversee website and newsletter content.
Coordinate PR activities and monitor the delivery of agreed initiatives.
Ensure consistency of brand identity and tone of voice across all online and offline touchpoints.
Evaluate advertising activities based on performance, commercial value and brand impact.
Plan and execute on-property events and brand activations.
Develop and manage collaborations with brands, influencers and content creators.
Oversee guest-facing printed and creative materials.
Collaborate closely with Revenue, Sales and Operations to support the hotel's commercial objectives.
Requirements
Proven experience as a Marketing Manager or in a similar role within hospitality or the luxury sector.
Strong understanding of digital marketing, performance campaigns and social media strategy.
Experience managing external partners and agencies.
Ability to monitor and evaluate key marketing KPIs.
Ability to combine creative thinking with data-driven decision-making.
Excellent communication, organizational and project management skills.
Fluency in English.
Nice to Have
Previous experience in luxury resorts or hotels serving international markets.
Experience in influencer marketing and brand collaborations.
Understanding of international tourism markets and luxury hospitality target audiences.
Familiarity with digital analytics tools and advertising platforms.
Additional foreign languages.
What We Offer
Full-time, year-round employment
Competitive remuneration package.
Professional and dynamic working environment within a 5-star hotel.
Collaboration with specialized and international partners and agencies.
Opportunity to play an active role in the development and evolution of the brand.
Professional development opportunities.
