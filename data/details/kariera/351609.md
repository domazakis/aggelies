About Us
At Numo Hotels & Resorts, we exist to redefine the essence of hospitality through a

NUMO Ierapetra
Numo Ierapetra, Curio Collection by Hilton, is an adults-only escape that shines a fresh light on the hidden gem of southern Crete. Nestled along sun-soaked shores and framed by lush evergreen gardens, our resort blends barefoot luxury with earthy design and a vibrant atmosphere. It’s where modern style meets the laid-back charm of Ierapetra, perfect for those looking to unwind in style.
Get the chance to work in the hotel that was awarded the silver in Best Hotel to Work In in 2024 at the Greek Hotel of the Year Awards.
Key Responsibilities
Oversee and coordinate day-to day Front Office operations (Reception, Guest Relations, Night Audit).
Ensure excellent guest service throughout arrival, stay, and departure.
Monitor KPIs, budgets, daily financial transactions, and reports.
Lead, train, and evaluate the Front Office team, preparing work schedules.
Handle guest complaints, feedback, and online reviews with professionalism.
Ensure compliance with company policies, safety, and quality standards.
Collaborate with Housekeeping, Maintenance, Reservations, and Guest Experience.
Requirements
Degree or Diploma in Hospitality Management or related field
At least 3 years of experience in a front office management position in a 5-star hotel
Strong leadership and training skills to ensure team development
Excellent proficiency in English, knowledge of a second language is a plus
Proficient in hotel management software (preferably Opera) and Microsoft Office Suite
Exceptional customer service skills with a focus on guest satisfaction
Strong problem-solving abilities and the ability to work under pressure
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Attention to detail and exceptional organizational skills
Flexibility in scheduling and the willingness to handle diverse situations
