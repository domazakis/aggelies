APHI MIRA (ex Miramare) located in Ixia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the Front office Manager.
POSITION SUMMARY:
A Front Office Manager is crucial in creating positive and efficient guest experience. Effective communication, leadership skills, and attention to detail are essential for success in this role.
Key job responsibilities:
Management of the Hotel’s Front Office operations following the company’s budget, directives and procedures including:
Maintain an excellent reception service and a high guest satisfaction rate.
Daily follow up of occupancy.
Close cooperation with the Room Division Manager in order to achieve the occupancy & upselling revenue targets.
Daily check and follow up of F/O operation procedures.
Ensure good coordination and communication within departments.
Follow guest correspondence.
Preparation of room allocation
Preparation/control invoices for the accounts department.
Promote in-house sales (terms upgrade, safe key, mini bar)
Any other matter related to the maximization of financial and quality results of his/her department.
Ensure the smooth running of IMS and H&S procedures.
Requirements:
Hotel Management diploma.
3 years’ experience in the Hotel Industry in luxury property, as a Head Receptionist, or 5 years’ experience as a Receptionist.
Excellent knowledge of intercompany software. Word, Excel, Microsoft office and the internet.
Excellent knowledge of the hotel’s PMS (Protel)
Be trained and have relevant knowledge for implementation of the following management systems: ISO 9001;14001; 22000& OSHAS
Fluent in English, any other language is an advantage
Good communication skills both spoken and written.
Excellent Management skills
Good organizational skills
Be able to motivate and train the staff.
Good promotional and selling skills
Sales and Marketing knowledge.
Understands and follows budgeting as directed by the company.
Excellent Supervision abilities
Any other matter related to the maximization of financial and quality results of his/her department.
Benefits:
Salary package and other benefits (as per local legislation)
Advanced opportunities for professional growth
Accommodation (if necessary) and board
Participation in educational programs & trainings
Diverse and multicultural environments
Professional skills development
