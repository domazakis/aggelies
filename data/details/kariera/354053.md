MARKETING & SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN CONSULTANT
PLACE OF WORK ATHENS, GREECE
For our Design center in Athens, we are looking for a creative, committed and ambitious individual to join our team full time
Kronospan Design Center Athens is looking for an individual who is creative in Marketing as a profession, and at the same time enjoys the field of Interiors and Architecture. This is the area the Kronospan Design Center Athens operates in.
Main duties and responsibilities :
Creating and uploading Digital Content
Creating and uploading Social Media Content in line with the company branding
Creating marketing content for events
Actively participates in the organization of events, workshops and other promotional activities
Help with Design Center merchandising and appearance
Keep up with interior trends
Work with a wide range of customers: architects, builders, designers, homeowners in order to present and promote the full range of Kronospan products
Develop material and color solutions for Architects, Interior Designers, and others in our industry
Provide a successful buying experience throughout the customer journey with the positive attitude to exceed our customer’s expectations
Maintain knowledge of all product information and specifications
Requirements :
Marketing & Social Media or a Graphic Design qualification.
Extensive knowledge of Social Media tools and how to apply and operate them
Photography knowledge is a plus
Having basic experience in events planning is a plus
Professionally and business orientated
Organisational and time management skills with ability to prioritise tasks and work methodically
Excellent presentational skills; ability to speak in front of audience
Problem solving, listening skills and experience in working with clients
The ability to deal with a vast array of customers with varying levels of product knowledge
Positive and team-worker attitude
Highly organized team player with excellent interpersonal skills and strong attention to detail
MS Office and Excel skills
General skills in Adobe Photoshop, Sketch Up and other design programs.
Fluency in Greek and English
Flexibility to work on a Saturday’s
Formal work experience is a must
We offer :
Exciting and dynamic work environment
Continued training, support and career development
The opportunity to work with and learn from industry leading committed people, in an international company
Monthly gross earnings ranging from €1,000 to €1,200 (depending on qualifications and prior experience)
Additional monthly performance-based bonuses, contingent upon the achievement of specific eligibility criteria
Full social security coverage and statutory allowances
