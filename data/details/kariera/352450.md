About ROLCO
ROLCO-BIANIL S.A. is one of the leading Greek industries in detergents and cleaning products, with a strong presence in both the Greek and international markets.
With continuous investment in innovation, quality, and people development, the company produces high-standard products, actively contributing to the evolution of the industry.
As part of our ongoing growth, we are looking for an Assistant Brand Manager to join our team.
Role Overview
The Assistant Brand Manager supports the Brand Management team in developing and executing brand plans, marketing campaigns, product launches and portfolio initiatives. The role combines market analysis, cross-functional project coordination, campaign support and performance tracking.
Key Responsibilities
Support Brand Managers in developing and implementing brand plans, marketing campaigns, product launches and portfolio initiatives.
Analyze market, category, competitor and consumer data to identify trends, opportunities and risks.
Prepare marketing briefs, presentations and campaign materials; support packaging updates and product claims.
Assist in planning and executing advertising, promotional and digital campaigns.
Coordinate with agencies and internal teams to ensure timely delivery of marketing activities.
Support new product development by collecting input, monitoring timelines and following up with relevant teams.
Track marketing budgets and help ensure activities remain within approved cost parameters.
Monitor brand, campaign and promotional KPIs; prepare performance reports and recommendations.
Support consumer engagement activities, surveys and feedback initiatives.
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Administration or a related field; a Master’s degree is an advantage.
At least 2 years of experience in brand management or marketing, preferably within the FMCG industry.
Strong analytical skills, attention to detail and a results-driven mindset.
Good understanding of digital marketing, social media and campaign execution.
Excellent organizational, project coordination, communication and presentation skills.
Ability to collaborate effectively in a fast-paced environment and willingness to learn.
Fluency in English.
