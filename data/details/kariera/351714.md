is a new collection of uplifting resorts and part of Wavemaker Hospitality, a dynamic house of hospitality brands backed by more than 45 years of industry expertise, a portfolio of over 40 hotels, and a legacy of welcoming more than 500,000 guests each season.
With exceptional destinations including APHI AURA and APHI MIRA in Rhodes, and APHI KALLI in Crete, APHI RESORTS brings together authentic hospitality and inspiring guest experiences. As part of the Wavemaker Hospitality portfolio, alongside Atlantica Hotels & Resorts, Mare Resorts, Aphrodite Hills Resort, and AMOH, a Luxury Collection Resort, Rhodes, APHI RESORTS builds on a strong hospitality heritage while introducing a fresh approach to the guest experience.

APHI MIRA (ex Miramare) located in Ixia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the Operations Manager.
POSITION SUMMARY:
We seek an experienced and hands-on Operations Manager to support the Hotel Manager in leading the hotel’s day-to-day operations.
The role will coordinate the Front Office, Housekeeping, Maintenance and Food & Beverage activities, ensuring seamless departmental collaboration and consistently high five-star standards. With strong leadership, commercial awareness and a guest-focused approach, the successful candidate will drive operational efficiency, manage costs and budgets, develop and retain high-performing teams, maintain productive supplier relationships and continuously enhance the overall guest experience.
Key job responsibilities:
Assisting the Hotel Manager to manage all aspects of hotel operations, including front desk, housekeeping, maintenance and food & beverage services.
• Lead, mentor, and develop a diverse team of staff to deliver outstanding service, demonstrating strong team leadership.
• Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations and hotel policies.
• Optimize operational efficiency while maintaining budgetary guidelines and applying effective cost control measures.
• Analyse guest feedback and implement improvements to enhance the guest experience.
• Monitor industry trends and competitor performance to identify opportunities for growth.
• Oversee and support maintenance operations to ensure the property meets 5* standards.
• Monitor budgets, forecasts, and P&L reports to achieve financial goals.
• Demonstrate strong understanding of Room Division operations to ensure smooth daily performance.
• Apply solid Food & Beverage expertise to maintain service excellence and financial efficiency.
• Show excellent problem-solving skills, ensuring quick and effective solutions.
• Socialize with guests and maintain strong guest relations to create memorable experiences.
• Recruit and retain the best possible staff, fostering a high-performance culture.
• Oversee staff accommodation issues to ensure employee well-being and satisfaction.
• Maintain and strengthen good relations with suppliers to secure quality and cost-effective services.
Requirements
Minimum of 3 years of experience in a similar position
Food & Beverage oriented and minimum 3 years of experience in this position
Bachelor’s degree in hospitality management or related field.
Strong leadership, team-development and supervisory skills, with the ability to motivate others and maintain high service standards.
Outstanding communication, active listening, problem-solving, organisational and multitasking abilities.
Confident user of Microsoft Office, hotel systems and digital communication tools.
Flexibility to work according to hotel operational requirements.
Solid knowledge of hotel maintenance, budgeting, forecasting, cost control, Food & Beverage operations, problem solving, guest relations, recruitment, and supplier management
Benefits:
Salary and benefits package in line with local legislation.
Accommodation, where required, and meals.
Training, professional development and career-growth opportunities.
A diverse, multicultural and supportive working environment.
