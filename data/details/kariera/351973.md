APHI AURA located in Kolymbia, Rhodes currently seeks the position of the
Restaurant manager.
Position Summary
The
Restaurant Manager
is responsible for ensuring high standards of guest satisfaction by coordinating service teams and addressing guest concerns effectively. This role is critical in maintaining operational excellence, driving staff performance, and enhancing the overall guest experience.
Responsibilities:
Ensure excellent customer service is delivered consistently.
Staff coordination and task assignment during service.
Resolve guest complaints and issues promptly, maintaining a professional and empathetic approach.
Train the staff to follow the ISO 22000 and H&S procedures., mentor, and evaluate service staff to uphold hotel standards and procedures.
Manage scheduling, staffing & department’s procedures.
Uphold safety, hygiene, and brand compliance standards across service areas.
Collaborate with the F&B Manager to develop and implement service improvement initiatives.
Maintain clear and open communication with other hotel departments to ensure seamless guest experiences.
Ensure compliance with all company policies and procedures, including health and safety regulations, data protection, and security procedures.
Requirements:
Hospitality school diploma or related field preferred.
5 years’ experience as a Head waiter and 2 years in charge of a restaurant.
Strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills.
Good knowledge in hotel management systems (e.g., Protel).
Passion for customer service and hospitality excellence.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to interact effectively with guests and staff.
Strong organizational, time-management skills, problem-solving and decision-making skills, with the ability to handle complex and/or escalated guest issues and find creative solutions.
Fluent in English; additional languages a plus.
Benefits:
Salary package and other benefits (as per local legislation)
Advanced opportunities for professional growth
Accommodation (if necessary) and board
Participation in educational programs & trainings
Diverse and multicultural environments
Professional skills development
