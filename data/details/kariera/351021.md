Account Manager | Digital & PR | SCL Mellon
About SCL Mellon
SCL Mellon is a multi-award-winning, digital-first boutique agency based in Athens, specializing in strategy, content, and performance marketing to build brands and drive measurable growth. The team combines data-driven insight with creative execution to help clients stay ahead in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.
The agency is known for a curious mindset and a commitment to testing emerging technologies and new approaches. SCL Mellon offers a collaborative environment where professionals can work closely with diverse clients and contribute to innovative digital initiatives.
About the Role
The
Account Manager
is a full-time, on-site role based in Vrilissia, responsible for managing day-to-day relationships with clients.
We are looking for a professional who can understand the strategic thinking behind a digital campaign, and at the same time see opportunities beyond owned channels — through media, creators, partnerships and earned visibility.
This is a role for someone who enjoys moving between strategy, client management and execution, and can connect the dots across different communication disciplines.
Role Description
Manage day-to-day client relationships and ensure a high level of service.
Work closely with strategy, creative, social and performance teams to develop and deliver integrated campaigns.
Coordinate digital and social media projects from brief to execution.
Contribute to communication strategies combining digital, PR, creators and activations.
Support PR initiatives, media outreach and earned visibility opportunities.
Coordinate influencer and creator collaborations, from research and outreach to briefing, execution and reporting.
Support launches, events, partnerships and brand activations.
Ensure projects are delivered on time, on budget and to a high standard.
Monitor digital, media and cultural trends and turn them into relevant opportunities for clients.
What we are looking for
3+ years of experience
in account management, digital marketing, PR or communications, preferably within an agency environment.
Strong understanding of the digital and social media ecosystem.
Previous experience in PR, media relations, influencer marketing or creator collaborations.
Strong client management and communication skills.
Ability to think strategically and challenge a brief rather than simply execute it.
Excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple accounts and projects simultaneously.
Strong written and verbal communication skills in both Greek and English.
Confidence working in a fast-paced agency environment with high standards.
Curiosity about brands, culture, media, creators and emerging communication trends.
A collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively across different disciplines.
Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience; prior agency experience is an asset.
