About us
ORA Hospitality
is launching its flagship resort in Mykonos, Greece, scheduled for May 2027, and is currently building the team who will bring this project to life. The resort will mark the debut of a new ultra-luxury hospitality brand, currently under development. Located in the bay of Kalo Livadi, the property will feature 88 rooms and suites, alongside 5 private villas, specialty restaurants, wellness center, among other facilities.
ORA Hospitality is the luxury arm of ORA Developers, building a global portfolio of exceptional hotels and resorts designed to inspire how guests feel long after they leave. The collection includes the award-winning Silversands Grenada and Silversands Beach House in the Caribbean, with a growing pipeline of new destinations under development worldwide.
Joining ORA Hospitality means being part of an opening team and helping shape a brand from the ground up.
What you will do:
Oversee all Front Office and hotel operations during the night shift, ensuring a smooth, secure, and exceptional guest experience.
Supervise the Night Audit and Night Reception team, providing guidance, support, and training as needed.
Handle guest check-ins, check-outs, and urgent requests with efficiency, professionalism, and discretion.
Monitor hotel security, emergency procedures, and compliance with health and safety standards.
Coordinate with other departments to ensure seamless operations and prepare for the following day’s arrivals and events.
Resolve guest complaints or incidents promptly, maintaining a high standard of service recovery.
Prepare nightly reports, including occupancy, revenue, and incident logs, for management review.
Act as the on-duty representative of the hotel, maintaining brand standards and luxury service expectations.
What you need to bring:
Minimum 3 years of experience in a relevant position, preferably in luxury or 5-star properties, with night shift or supervisory experience.
Excellent command of the English language; additional languages are an advantage.
Strong leadership and team supervision skills.
Sound knowledge of Front Office procedures, PMS, and Microsoft Office.
Excellent problem-solving, decision-making, and communication skills.
Calm, composed, and professional under pressure, with a proactive approach to guest service.
