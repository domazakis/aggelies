Talent Acquisition Specialist
About the Role
As a
Talent Acquisition Specialist
, you will play a key role in shaping the future of Zeus Hotels & Resorts by attracting and hiring exceptional talent across our growing portfolio in Greece. Your mission is to identify the right people, build meaningful candidate experiences, and support our hotels in creating high-performing teams.
With strong communication skills, a proactive mindset, and a passion for people, you will transform recruitment into a strategic advantage that drives our continued success.
Life Inside the Hotel Group
Every recruitment journey is different. Some days are dedicated to sourcing talent, conducting interviews, and building candidate pipelines. Others involve collaborating with hotel managers, supporting hiring decisions, attending career events, or strengthening our employer brand.
In this fast-paced and people-driven environment, you will be the connection between talented professionals and exciting career opportunities within one of Southern Europe's fastest-growing hospitality groups.
What you'll do
Manage the full recruitment lifecycle for positions across the hotel group
Partner with department heads and hotel managers to understand hiring needs and define candidate profiles
Source and attract qualified candidates through job boards, social media, networking, referrals, and direct search
Conduct interviews and coordinate all stages of the recruitment and selection process
Build and maintain strong talent pipelines for seasonal and permanent positions
Ensure an excellent candidate experience throughout the hiring journey
Maintain accurate recruitment reports, databases, and hiring metrics
Support employer branding initiatives, recruitment events, and university partnerships
Contribute to continuous improvement of recruitment processes and best practices
Your Uniqueness is Welcome
Recruitment is more than filling vacancies, it's about recognizing potential, building relationships, and creating teams that deliver exceptional hospitality experiences. We are looking for someone who enjoys connecting with people, thrives in a dynamic environment, and is passionate about finding the right talent.
We're looking for someone who:
Holds a Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field
Has
3–5 years
of experience in Talent Acquisition or Recruitment, ideally within hospitality or another service-oriented industry
Demonstrates excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Is highly organized and able to manage multiple recruitment projects simultaneously
Has excellent command of both
Greek and English
, written and spoken
Is confident using recruitment platforms, LinkedIn, and Microsoft Office
Has a proactive, positive, and collaborative mindset
Is willing to travel within Greece when required
What we offer
Competitive compensation package
A dynamic and professional working environment within a leading hospitality group
Continuous learning and career development opportunities
The opportunity to contribute to the growth of one of Southern Europe's fastest-growing hotel groups
A collaborative HR team that values innovation, teamwork, and professional growth
