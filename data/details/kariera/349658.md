Quality Officer
Ρόδος, Δωδεκάνησα, Ελλάδα | Ref:  HHC/QO · Quality Department
Job Position:                   Quality Officer
Ref:                                    HHC/QO
Location:
Kiotari,
South Rhodes
Company:                          H HOTELS COLLECTION
About us:
H Hotels Collection | Hatzilazarou Group one of the leading and constantly growing Hotel Groups in Greece, operating in the hospitality industry for 30 years, is offering new positions for its hotels in Rhodes.
Job description:
The ideal candidate will be responsible for ensuring compliance in line with legislation and all system processes including quality and safety, implementation of quality management actions and contributing to the development of company policies and procedures.  Ongoing learning in Group Hotels and project management.
Key responsibilities:
Works closely with Hotel Managers and HODs to establish, maintain and optimize QMS in all group properties
Ensures full compliance of hotel SOPs, policies, procedures, and brand standards.
Ensures H&S compliance in line with local and international legislation, as well as ABTA-FTO regulations.
Enhances approach to sustainability and establish key steps for waste management and energy efficiency.
Works closely with the HR Department to establish, maintain and optimize HR processes.
Cooperates with the Purchasing Department to establish quality requirements from external suppliers.
Identify relevant quality-related training needs and deliver training to ensure procedures are properly understood, carried out and evaluated.
Serves as a key contact for external partners.
Ensures all QM deliverables are achieved in a compliant timely manner.
Qualifications:
Business School / Hotel School Degree [master’s degree is desirable]
Excellent command of MS Office
Fluency in English
Own vehicle and valid driver's license
Skills and experience:
Experience in Hotel Quality or Operations departments
Experience in QMS systems and health & safety processes is preferred
Administration and scheduling
Willingness to learn in a challenging environment
In the context of the above position, the company offers:
Competitive compensation package.
10-month contract [February - November]
Housing (out of the area residents).
Professional development opportunities in an ever-growing Group.
Continuous training in line with the company’s standards & dynamic development
Positive and supportive working environment in an organization that respects its people, the environment and local communities.
For all applications and information submitted, absolute confidentiality will be maintained.
