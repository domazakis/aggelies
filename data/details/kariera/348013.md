Maintenance Experiencer
YellowSquare Athens is looking for a Maintenance Experiencer!
Are you a pro at fixing things but also love the hostel vibe? Do you believe that a safe, well-maintained space is the secret to a great guest experience? If you’re reliable, organized, and ready to bring your technical skills to a dynamic international team, you’re in the right place!
YellowSquare Athens
is looking for a
Maintenance Experiencer
. You’ll be the backbone of the hostel, ensuring everything runs smoothly, from quick fixes to long-term safety, all while keeping the vibe high.
Why join us:
Be part of a passionate, international team that values creativity, community, and collaboration;
Grow with us: this role offers real opportunities for professional development in a young and expanding company;
Enjoy a dynamic work environment where your hands-on approach directly impacts the happiness of guests from all over the world.
What you’ll be doing:
Keeping the Square running: Managing daily maintenance tasks to keep our hostel efficient, safe, and welcoming;
Proactive Problem-Solving: Identifying and enforcing procedures for maintenance and safety to prevent issues before they arise;
Orders and Supplies: Monitoring equipment needs and placing orders efficiently and sustainably;
Safety First: Ensuring full compliance with local regulations on safety, health, and hygiene;
Team Spirit: Collaborating with all departments to contribute to innovative projects that improve the guest and staff experience.
What you should have:
Previous experience in facility management or maintenance (hospitality background is a huge plus!);
A hands-on approach: reliable, proactive, and able to work independently;
Strong organizational skills: the ability to juggle technical tasks with a smile;
High proficiency in spoken English and Greek (you’ll be in daily contact with international staff and guests);
Positive attitude: we love people who bring good vibes and a "can-do" spirit!
What we offer:
A dynamic and international work environment at YellowSquare Athens;
A full-time permanent position with a contract and all conditions provided by the national collective agreement for the hospitality sector;
Salary and benefits based on your seniority and experience
Growth opportunities within a creative and community-driven company.
This role is perfect for someone who is technically skilled but also thrives in a fast-paced, social environment. If you’re ready to keep the Square in top shape — we want to hear from you.
Apply today and join the YellowSquare Athens team!
