About the job
Culinary:
Demi Chef De Partie
Locations:
Greece: Corfu, Kos, Halkidiki, Crete
Duration:
7 months, between March-November
At Sani/Ikos Group, the
Culinary department
is where creativity meets craftsmanship. From passionate Chefs to dedicated Cooks, our team members craft culinary masterpieces with meticulous attention to recipes and guidelines.
Whether preparing dishes for our buffet or à la carte restaurants, pride is taken in delivering menus crafted by Michelin-starred chefs.
Responsibilities:
Food Preparation:
Prepare and cook dishes in your assigned section under the guidance of the Chef de Partie or Sous Chef.
Station Management:
Keep your section organized, stocked, and clean during preparation and service.
Quality Standards:
Ensure all dishes meet the restaurant’s presentation and quality standards.
Teamwork:
Work closely with the kitchen team to maintain smooth and efficient operations.
Training & Development:
Support junior employees (e.g., Commis Chefs) and continue developing your own skills.
Hygiene & Safety:
Follow all food hygiene, safety, and cleanliness standards.
About you
You have previous experience as a Commis Chef or Demi Chef de Partie in a professional kitchen, with a solid understanding of basic cooking techniques and kitchen equipment. You are a strong team player with good communication skills and work effectively within a structured kitchen environment. You are eager to learn, receptive to guidance, and comfortable taking direction from senior chefs. You are committed to maintaining high standards of quality, cleanliness, and safe working practices at all times.
* Not sure if you meet all the role requirements? We still welcome your application and a chance to learn more about why you think you could be a valuable addition to the Sani / Ikos team.
