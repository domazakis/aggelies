Internal Communication Officer
09/10/2026
Job Description:
We are currently looking for an
Internal Communication Officer
to join our
Corporate Communications
team in
Athens, Greece
.
By joining this role, you will contribute to the development and execution of internal communication initiatives that keep employees informed, engaged, and aligned with the company's strategy, values, and culture. You will play a key role in delivering impactful communication campaigns, managing internal communication channels, and supporting employee engagement initiatives across the organization.
Your role will encompass:
Support the planning and execution of internal communication strategies and annual communication plans
Develop engaging content for internal communication channels, including intranet, newsletters, announcements, and employee communications in both Greek and English
Manage and continuously enhance the company's intranet, ensuring content remains relevant, engaging, and up to date
Collaborate with HR and business stakeholders to promote employee engagement initiatives and internal campaigns
Support communication activities related to organizational initiatives, projects, and corporate programs
Coordinate and support internal events and employee-focused activities
Ensure consistency of messaging and alignment between internal and external communications
Work closely with external agencies and communication partners to support project delivery
Assist in communication reporting, budget tracking, contract management, and administrative processes
What you bring to the role:
Bachelor's degree in Communications, Public Relations, Journalism, Marketing, or a related field
5-8 years of experience in Internal Communications, Corporate Communications, or a similar role
Excellent content creation and copywriting skills in both Greek and English
Experience managing intranet platforms and digital communication tools
Strong stakeholder management and project coordination skills
Ability to communicate effectively across different audiences and organizational levels
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple priorities
Proficiency in Microsoft Office and digital communication platforms
Share the same Value Based Behaviors:
People & Personal Growth
Results Orientation & Safety Excellence
Collaboration & Trust
Empathy & Effective Communication
Flexibility & Adaptability
