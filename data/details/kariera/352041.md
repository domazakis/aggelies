Job requirements:
Minimum 5 years of experience in Front Office, Guest Relations, or a similar guest facing role, with previous supervisory or leadership experience, preferably within a luxury, boutique, or high end hospitality environment.
Warm, confident, polished, and naturally engaging communication style, with a genuine passion for personalized and elevated guest service.
Fluency in English and Greek is required, additional languages are considered an asset.
Proven ability to lead and motivate a Front Office team, providing guidance, support, coaching, and direction throughout daily operations.
Strong problem solving and service recovery skills, with the ability to handle complex or sensitive guest situations with discretion, professionalism, and sound judgment.
Proficiency in Opera PMS and MS Office, with strong knowledge of check-ins, check-outs, room allocations, reservations support, guest postings, billing, reporting, cashier procedures, and maintaining accurate guest profiles.
Strong understanding of Front Office procedures, hotel operations, and luxury hospitality standards, with the ability to ensure consistency and attention to detail across all shifts.
Highly organized, proactive, and detail-oriented, with the ability to prioritize, delegate, and make effective decisions in a fast-paced environment.
Strong cash handling and financial awareness, including accurate cashier procedures, shift balancing, end-of-shift closing, and monitoring of departmental transactions.
Excellent communication and coordination skills, with the ability to work effectively with Housekeeping, Maintenance, Food & Beverage, Spa, Reservations, and Management.
Professional appearance and impeccable grooming in line with the property's luxury boutique hospitality standards.
Job responsibilities:
Manage the daily operations of the Front Office and Guest Relations functions, ensuring smooth, efficient, and consistent service delivery.
Supervise and support Front Office and Guest Relations colleagues during assigned shifts, providing guidance, direction, and coaching as required.
Lead by example in delivering a warm, personalized, and seamless guest experience from arrival through departure.
Oversee daily Front Office activities, including arrivals, departures, room allocations, reservations support, guest postings, billing, cashier operations, reports, and PMS accuracy.
Review VIPs, special occasions, in-house guests, pending requests, and handovers to ensure smooth continuity between shifts.
Take ownership of guest requests, complaints, and service recovery, ensuring issues are resolved promptly and followed through personally.
Coordinate closely with Housekeeping, Maintenance, Food & Beverage, Spa, Reservations, and other departments to ensure efficient resolution of guest and operational matters.
Ensure guest profiles, preferences, comments, and follow-up notes are accurately maintained.
Support VIP arrivals, group movements, special occasions, and personalized guest experiences, ensuring a smooth and discreet service approach.
Assist with training, onboarding, coaching, and development of Front Office and Guest Relations team members.
Support the Front Office Manager with staff scheduling, task allocation, shift organization, and operational planning as required.
Ensure all cashier procedures, daily postings, billing, reconciliations, reports, and departmental closures are completed accurately and on time.
Maintain excellent lobby and Front Office presentation, ensuring the property consistently reflects its boutique luxury standards.
Promote the hotel's restaurant, experiences, and services through thoughtful and genuine recommendations.
Take a leadership role in the Front Office operation, ensuring continuity, smooth daily operations, effective decision-making, and clear communication with Management and other departments.
Maintain high standards of guest confidentiality, professional conduct, grooming, and service procedures at all times.
Participate in daily briefings and operational meetings, communicating relevant guest feedback, outstanding matters, and improvement opportunities.
