About Daios Cove
Located in a stunning private bay near Agios Nikolaos, Daios Cove is a five-star destination known for its modern design, outstanding service, and genuine Cretan hospitality. Our team is passionate about creating unforgettable guest experiences and setting the standard for excellence in luxury hospitality. Join us and be part of a dynamic, international environment where your talent and commitment are valued and rewarded.
In a Nutshell:
The Wellness Director leads all aspects of the Wellness Center — spa, wellness, and guest experience — ensuring a successful opening each season and the smooth running of day-to-day operations. This includes supervising the spa's modalities and amenities, hydrotherapy and bathing facilities, the integrative clinic, and programs for mind-body & movement activations, wellness events, and related activities.
As the gatekeeper of our guests' wellness journey, you will apply a high level of professional experience and skill in delivering a wide range of holistic programs with accuracy, attention to detail, and confidence.
A strong all-round communicator, you will be an ambassador for GOCO Hospitality and Daios Cove, working collaboratively to positively impact guest wellbeing and inspire them to succeed.
Some of the responsibilities you will be entrusted with:
Lead the philosophy, strategy, and day-to-day operations of our Wellness Center.
Ensure an exceptional, personalized guest journey and the highest standards of luxury service.
Develop innovative wellness, longevity, preventive health, and lifestyle programs.
Cultivate and establish strategic partnerships with visiting wellness practitioners, wellness specialists, suppliers, and organizations to enhance the retreat's offerings and market position.
Influence, support, and encourage guests in following their program, providing professional, non-judgmental advice that motivates their success.
Maintain and continuously update knowledge of the latest trends, treatments, and equipment in spa and wellness.
Lead and mentor team members, inspiring passion and innovation, including by being proactive in the company's daily briefings.
Develop and implement pricing strategies and promotional activities to maximize revenue and occupancy within the wellness division.
Collaborate with the Commercial teams to create compelling wellness packages, promotions, and membership programs that attract target markets.
Monitor market trends and competitor activities to identify opportunities for growth and innovation within the wellness & health sector.
Manage the wellness division's budget to ensure cost-effective operations and achieve financial targets.
Set a high standard for appearance, grooming, and conduct; uphold the company's vision and philosophy; and project a professional image to guests.
