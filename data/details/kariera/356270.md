About the role:
Allis Resort Porto Heli-MGallery Collection, Member of Accor is opening in June 2027  seeking a warm, highly organized and guest-focused
Guest Relations Manager & Events Executive
. Reporting to Front Office Manager and Director of Sales the Guest Relations Manager plays a crucial role in creating tailor-made experiences, supporting guests throughout their stay and ensuring seamless coordination between departments. This position requires strong communication skills, attention to detail and a genuine passion for creating memorable journeys for our guests.
Responsibilities
Greet and assist guests upon arrival and departure in a warm and friendly manner.
Address guest inquiries and requests.
Resolve guest complaints and concerns with professionalism, offering suitable solutions.
Maintain detailed knowledge of hotel services, amenities, and local attractions to assist guests.
Coordinate with other departments (Housekeeping, Front Desk, F&B, etc.) to ensure seamless guest experiences.
Assist with VIP check-ins, both at lobby desk and in-room.
Manage VIP guests and special requests, ensuring a high level of service is provided.
Provide assistance with reservations and booking modifications.
Ensure all guest feedback is recorded and used to improve service quality.
Maintain a high level of professionalism and confidentiality.
Performing other related tasks based on the hotel's needs.
Coordinate corporate events, private functions, weddings, and hospitality experiences.
Build and maintain strong relationships with clients, suppliers, and partners.
Conduct venue tours and assist with client meetings.
Liaise with operations, catering, marketing, and front-of-house team.
Ensure all event details are accurately communicated and executed to all teams.
Manage event calendars, bookings, invoices, and administrative documentation.
Assist with promotional campaigns, social media content, and networking events.
Monitor guest satisfaction and resolve issues professionally.
Requirements
Strong communication skills and excellent spoken and written English.
Experience within an international brand will be an asset.
Organizational skills, attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple tasks effectively.
Ability to work in a fast-paced environment while maintaining calm and professionalism.
Good knowledge of Opera PMS is an advantage.
Strong interpersonal skills and a warm, naturally service-driven personality.
A reliable and proactive approach aligned with brand standards.
Benefits
Staff accommodation.
Meals when on shift.
Continuous training and development opportunities.
Career growth opportunities within the Company
