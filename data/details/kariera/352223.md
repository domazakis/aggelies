Job requirements:
Minimum 5-7 years of experience in high-end hotels, luxury resorts, or fine-dining kitchens, with previous Sous Chef or supervisory experience.
Strong culinary knowledge and excellent technical skills across hot and cold kitchen sections.
Proven ability to lead, supervise, train, and motivate a professional kitchen team.
Strong knowledge of Mediterranean, Greek, and contemporary cuisine; knowledge of local Santorini products is an advantage.
Excellent understanding of HACCP, food safety, hygiene, allergen management, and kitchen health & safety standards.
Good knowledge of food cost control, portion control, stock rotation, waste reduction, and inventory management.
Strong organizational, communication, and time-management skills.
Ability to maintain high standards of quality, presentation, consistency, and service in a fast-paced luxury environment.
Hands-on, proactive, and solution-oriented approach with strong attention to detail.
Previous pre-opening or luxury hospitality experience is highly desirable.
Job responsibilities:
Support the Executive Chef / Chef de Cuisine in the daily operation and management of the kitchen.
Supervise and coordinate kitchen activities, ensuring efficient workflow and smooth service.
Lead the kitchen team during service and ensure consistent quality, presentation, timing, and portion control.
Organize and monitor daily mise en place, preparation, and production.
Train, coach, and motivate kitchen staff while maintaining discipline and professional standards.
Ensure full compliance with HACCP, food safety, hygiene, and sanitation procedures.
Monitor food quality, storage, stock rotation, waste, and portion sizes to support effective food-cost control.
Inspect kitchen areas and equipment regularly to ensure cleanliness, organization, and safe operation.
Assist with menu development, new dishes, seasonal products, and culinary projects.
Work closely with the F&B and service teams to ensure excellent communication and guest satisfaction.
Assist with staff scheduling, inventory, ordering, and daily operational planning.
Support the Executive Chef with pre-opening preparations, training, and the successful launch of the new restaurant.
Step into any kitchen section when required and maintain a hands-on leadership approach.
Uphold Sandblu's standards of excellence, teamwork, integrity, and authentic Greek hospitality.
